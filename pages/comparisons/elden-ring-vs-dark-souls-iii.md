They tied at 2 out of 3, but we would pick Elden Ring for a workout because the open world lets you choose your own pace.

## Elden Ring

Elden Ring gives you a huge map and a horse. When a fight is too hard you can ride off and do something else, and the exploring is easy to do at a steady pace. The big fights still need your full attention.

## Dark Souls III

Dark Souls III is tighter and more focused, with less room to get away from trouble. It also scored a No for pause friendly, so plan on finishing what you started before you step off. The controls are simple, but the timing has to be right.

## Which should you play?

Pick Elden Ring if you want to play during a real workout. Save Dark Souls III for an easy pace, and dont count on stopping mid fight in either one. If a boss is getting to you, slow down or stop the machine first.
