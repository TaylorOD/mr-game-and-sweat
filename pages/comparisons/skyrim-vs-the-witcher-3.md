They tied at 2 out of 3. Skyrim is the better pick if you want to wander, and The Witcher 3 is the better pick if you want a story to follow.

## Skyrim

Skyrim lets you pick a direction and go. A lot of the game is walking across the map, which is pretty much perfect for a treadmill. Fights can need a quick block or dodge, but you can usually take them at your own pace.

## The Witcher 3

The Witcher 3 has a huge open world too, but more of your time goes to story and conversations. That helps a long ride go by, but it also means more watching and less playing. Combat wants good timing, so it can be tricky at a hard pace.

## Which should you play?

Pick Skyrim for long, easy sessions where you just want to keep moving. Pick The Witcher 3 if the story is what will get you back on the machine tomorrow. Both are long games, so one of them could last you months.
