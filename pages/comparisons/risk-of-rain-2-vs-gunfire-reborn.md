Gunfire Reborn is the better workout game on our scores. Risk of Rain 2 is great too, but it gets too hectic to play at a hard pace.

## Risk of Rain 2

Risk of Rain 2 is a third person shooter where the difficulty keeps climbing the longer you play. That constant pressure is what makes it fun, and also what makes it hard to play while running. It scored a 2 for treadmill and bike.

## Gunfire Reborn

Gunfire Reborn is a first person shooter with runs broken into levels. There are still intense fights, but it scored a 3 for treadmill and bike and a 3 for computer requirements. The stops between levels give you room to reset.

## Which should you play?

Pick Gunfire Reborn if you want a shooter that works during a real workout. Save Risk of Rain 2 for an easy walking pace, or for days you care more about the game than the miles.
