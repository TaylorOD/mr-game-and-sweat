They tied at 3 out of 3. Stardew Valley is the calmer pick and pauses anywhere, while Terraria gives you more action.

## Terraria

Terraria is a 2D sandbox where you dig, build and fight. Simple controls and a slow start make it easy to play while moving. Things get busier once the bosses show up, and our review marks it as only sometimes pause friendly.

## Stardew Valley

Stardew Valley is a slow farming game with no rush most of the time. You plant, fish, mine and talk to the town at your own pace. It is a great fit for an easy walk or a long ride, and it is fully pause friendly.

## Which should you play?

Pick Stardew Valley if you want something relaxing that wont fight your workout. Pick Terraria if you want more combat and bigger goals to chase. Both are long games, so either one will last you a lot of workouts.
