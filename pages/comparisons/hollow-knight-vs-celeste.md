Both scored a perfect 3. Hollow Knight is the easier one to play while moving, and Celeste is better if you can break your workout into short tries.

## Hollow Knight

Hollow Knight is a big side scrolling world to explore, with plenty of quiet stretches between fights. You can wander, look around and plan your next move. The boss fights are the tough part, so save those for when you slow down.

## Celeste

Celeste is a precision platformer split into short screens. Dying costs you almost nothing because you restart right away, which helps when your hands arent steady. It also has an assist mode you can turn on if a section is too much mid workout.

## Which should you play?

Pick Hollow Knight for long, steady sessions where you want to explore. Pick Celeste if you like quick tries and a clear goal on every screen.
