Forza Horizon 5 wins on our scores, 2.7 to 2.3. Both scored a 3 for treadmill and bike, so you cant go too wrong with either.

## Forza Horizon 5

Forza Horizon 5 is an open world racer set in Mexico. It is forgiving, there is a lot to do, and you can use a rewind when you mess up a turn. That makes it easy to keep going when you are tired.

## Need for Speed Heat

Need for Speed Heat splits things into day races and night runs where the cops chase you. It works well during a workout, since it is mostly steering and accelerating. It scored lower than Forza on gameplay.

## Which should you play?

Pick Forza Horizon 5 for the better game overall. Pick Need for Speed Heat if police chases sound like more fun than a festival full of events. Either way, a controller makes it easy to play at a steady pace.
