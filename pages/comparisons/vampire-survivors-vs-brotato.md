They tied at 3 out of 3. Pick Brotato for a short workout and Vampire Survivors for a longer one.

## Vampire Survivors

In Vampire Survivors you mostly just move, and your weapons do the attacking for you. That makes it about as simple as an action game gets while you run or ride. A typical sitting lands in the 30 to 60 minute range, which fits a normal workout.

## Brotato

Brotato works in short waves of action with a shop between them. Those breaks are a good spot to grab water or change your pace, and the short rounds line up nicely with intervals. A session runs under 30 minutes.

## Which should you play?

This comes down to how long you are working out. Brotato is the better pick for intervals and quick sessions. Vampire Survivors is the better pick when you want one long, steady run to disappear.
