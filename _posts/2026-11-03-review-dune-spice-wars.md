Dune: Spice Wars is a bad workout pick because Arrakis demands your attention, and a treadmill is no place to lose track of your plans. The game is good at keeping you busy with territory, resources, diplomacy, and combat.

## Gameplay

This real time 4X strategy game gives you a lot to manage as you fight for control of Arrakis. The mix of expansion and combat keeps things engaging, but you need to watch the map and think through your choices instead of zoning out to a steady rhythm.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Full controller support is a plus, but it does not make the constant map checks and careful decisions any easier while you move. You can play with a controller, but this is the kind of strategy game that deserves your full attention.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum specs call for 8 GB of RAM and a GTX 1050 or RX 550. That is a basic dedicated graphics card, not the sort of machine that needs a high end gaming PC.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Dune: Spice Wars is a solid strategy game, but its demands on your attention make it a poor fit for exercise. Play it at your desk.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3
