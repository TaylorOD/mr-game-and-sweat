I’d start with KurtzPel’s PvE missions for a workout, because fighting a boss gives you something to pay attention to without making every mistake somebody else’s problem. The PvP sounds fun, but I’d save it for when I’m sitting down.

## Gameplay

The two Karmas you bring into battle are a good idea, because choosing between two weapon and class types gives you a little room to make the fight feel like yours without asking you to learn an entire pile of characters first. I think that’s a nice amount of decision making for an action game, though I’d want to settle on a pair before trying to play while exercising. Making a big choice while your legs are already doing their own thing is a lot.

The PvE missions and massive boss monsters sound like the part I’d come back to. Action combat keeps your attention in a way that a long conversation or a map full of tiny errands probably wouldn’t, and bosses give the fighting a clear target. I’d be curious how much the game asks you to learn each encounter, because some memorizing is fine and some makes me feel like I should be taking notes instead of getting my heart rate up.

PvP is a different deal. I can see the appeal of fighting another person, and that kind of match can make a straightforward battle feel important, but it also means you’re trying to react to someone who is actively trying to beat you. I think that’s exciting when you’re focused on the game and a little much when you’re also keeping your feet moving. I would not pick that mode as my first workout plan.

KurtzPel is free to play and in Early Access, so you can try it without paying up front, which is genuinely useful when you’re not sure whether its combat is for you. The Early Access label also means I’d go in expecting the game to keep changing, rather than treating every rough edge as a surprise personal insult. I don’t know how much variety the missions have, and that matters, because a fun boss fight can carry a lot but it can’t carry everything forever.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

On a treadmill, I’d want the PvE missions, a familiar Karma pair, and a pace where you can keep your hands steady enough to aim and react. The full controller support is a real plus, since you don’t need to reach for a keyboard in the middle of a fight, but controller support doesn’t make fast action automatically comfortable. You still have to watch what’s happening and respond.

I’d be more comfortable trying it on a bike, especially if the resistance is low enough that I’m not bouncing around while the boss is attacking. I think the game’s third person action could work there, but I’d still start with PvE, since the moment another player is involved I’d probably care more about winning than holding a steady pace. That’s not a moral failing. It’s just how games get me.

The treadmill version sounds like the kind of workout where you should keep the pace modest and not try to prove anything to the game. If a fight starts demanding quick reactions, you can always ease off the belt or stop playing that mode while moving. I don’t know how forgiving the combat feels, so I wouldn’t promise you can safely zone out and mash buttons. You’ll need to look.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum requirements point to an older dedicated graphics card being enough, with the listed target reaching sixty frames per second at low settings. I’d check the graphics card first, and if the computer is near the minimum, I’d use low settings rather than asking it to look fancy while it’s busy keeping up.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

KurtzPel makes sense for you if you want action during a workout and are happy to keep the competitive PvP for later. I’d give the PvE missions a try first, and I’d skip the treadmill if you find yourself leaning forward to win.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
