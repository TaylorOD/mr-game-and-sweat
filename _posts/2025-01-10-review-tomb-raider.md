Tomb Raider mixes exploration, puzzles, combat, and a story about Lara Croft. It makes a good workout game, though its computer requirements are a drawback.

## Gameplay

The gameplay is engaging and paced well, with exploration, puzzles, and combat to keep things moving. Long cut scenes are uncommon, and most action does not require much precision. That makes it easy to keep playing while you work out.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

A controller lets you play without reaching for keyboard keys. The controls suit a relaxed pace for jogging or cycling, though some combat calls for quick reactions. Overall, it is easy to keep a steady workout going.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game may need a moderately powerful computer to run smoothly. Higher graphics settings call for a stronger graphics card, and older computers may need lower settings.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Tomb Raider is a good fit for exercise thanks to its gameplay and controller support. Its computer requirements are a real drawback, so it is not an unqualified recommendation.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
