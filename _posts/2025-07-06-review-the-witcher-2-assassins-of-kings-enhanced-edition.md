The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings Enhanced Edition has action, exploration, and a story with difficult choices. It can fit a workout, but some parts ask for more focus than you may want to give while exercising.

## Gameplay

Combat and exploration offer a decent mix, but the tactical fights need your attention. Cutscenes and slower sections can interrupt your pace, so this is not an easy game to play on autopilot.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a standard controller while running or riding. Light exercise should be manageable, but precise combat may be tricky at a harder pace.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game can run on a mid range computer, though stronger hardware is needed to get the most from its graphics. Streaming or multitasking may strain a less powerful system.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The Witcher 2 has a strong story, but its slower sections and demanding combat make it only a fair fit for exercise. Controller support helps, though you will likely need to keep your workout at a steady, gentle pace.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
