Outlast 2 can keep your attention with its tense chases and desperate hiding, but those same moments make it a risky pick for a treadmill. On a bike, you can pedal through quieter stretches, then expect to slow down when danger gets close.

# Gameplay

You explore Temple Gate, a wilderness town led by Sullivan Knoth and his followers, while trying to survive its dangers. Sneaking around and escaping pursuers keeps the horror tense, though exploration and story scenes break up the action.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

# Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game has full controller support, which helps, but it still asks for quick reactions during chases and careful movement while sneaking. You can keep pedaling through calmer sections, but loose inputs are a bad match for moments when hiding or escaping matters.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

# Computer Requirements

The minimum requirements call for a 64 bit version of Windows, 4 GB of memory, and a GTX 260 or Radeon HD 4870 with 1 GB of video memory. That is an old dedicated graphics card, not integrated graphics, and the listed target is 720p at 30 frames per second.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

# Conclusion and Score

Outlast 2 has enough tension to hold your attention, but its chases and stealth make it a poor choice for exercising at a steady pace. It fits better on a bike than a treadmill, where you can ease off when things get rough.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3
