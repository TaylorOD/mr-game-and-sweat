Outlast 2 is both a gripping horror experience and a unique challenge for gamers who want to integrate their workout routines. The game's tension and atmosphere can keep you engaged, but the need for occasional stillness while hiding may interrupt your stride on the treadmill or bike.

# Gameplay

The gameplay in Outlast 2 is intense and immersive, engaging you from the moment you step into the eerie world of Temple Gate. The effective use of sound design and visual storytelling creates an environment filled with suspense, which keeps you on edge. However, there are slow burn moments where you’ll have to sneak and hold your breath in fear, which might pull you out of the rhythm you’ve built while exercising. While most gameplay is action-packed and drives the pace, expect some slower segments that can feel like they counteract your aerobic efforts.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

# Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Outlast 2 features full controller support, which is beneficial for those looking to engage in a workout while enjoying the game. However, the reliance on stealth and the need to stop at times to gauge your surroundings creates potential interruptions in your workout flow. Moreover, the horror elements may cause you to pause more often than you'd like to catch your breath, potentially breaking your exercise session into disjointed segments. If you're comfortable with such interruptions, you’ll certainly find an adrenaline boost from the scares.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

# Computer Requirements

The game’s requirements are quite moderate, making it accessible to those with even older machines. With just 4 GB of RAM and a basic graphics card, it shouldn’t demand too much of your system, allowing you to enjoy it without significant performance hiccups during your workout. The minimum requirement settings allow for a smooth experience at 720p, which is perfect for those looking to multitask while exercising.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

# Conclusion and Score

Outlast 2 offers a terrifying and captivating journey that can heighten your exercising experience, provided you can manage its thrilling interruptions and unsettling moments. While it may not be perfect for maintaining a steady workout pace continuously, its engaging gameplay and moderate PC requirements make it a worthwhile addition to your exercise gaming repertoire. Just be ready for some moments where you might need to take a break from running or biking to catch your breath—not just from the physical exercise, but from the sheer horror of it all.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
