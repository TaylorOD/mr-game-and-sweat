Garry's Mod is a sandbox game built on the Source engine, with lots of user made content and ways to play. It has stayed popular for years, but that flexibility does not automatically make it easy to use while you work out.

## Gameplay

There is plenty to do, from building things to playing odd multiplayer modes. There are few cut scenes or slow sections, so you can keep playing without many long pauses.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game is fully playable with a keyboard and mouse, so a gamepad is not required. Some user made games need quick movements and reflexes, which can make it harder to keep a steady workout rhythm.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Garry's Mod is not especially demanding, and it runs well on mid range systems. Keeping your system up to date can help, especially in larger multiplayer sessions or when loading complex mods.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The variety and steady pace make the game easy to keep playing during a workout. Still, the controls and quick moments in some modes can get in the way, so it is not an ideal fit for every treadmill or bike session.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3

Garry's Mod offers creative play and plenty of options, but the workout setup has some friction. It is a decent choice for exercising, not an effortless one.
