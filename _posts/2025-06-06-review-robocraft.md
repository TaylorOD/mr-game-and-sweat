Robocraft mixes building robots from blocks with team based combat. The setup gives you plenty to customize, and it works well as a game, though it takes some focus to build your robot.

## Gameplay

Matches move quickly, with little downtime and a steady stream of combat. You can customize your robot with blocks, weapons, and abilities, while team fights reward quick thinking and strategy.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support makes Robocraft workable on a treadmill or bike without precise mouse movements. The action is manageable, but customizing robots takes care, and you may need breaks between matches when your workout gets intense.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Robocraft runs well on mid range and low end computers. Its graphics are not especially demanding, so your computer should not need much performance to run the game.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Robocraft has strong gameplay and modest computer requirements, but its fit for exercising is more mixed. Combat is manageable, though robot customization can take attention away from your workout.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
