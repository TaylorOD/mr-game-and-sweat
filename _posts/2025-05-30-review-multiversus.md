MultiVersus is a fighting game where characters from different franchises battle. The action is fun, though pauses between rounds can slow things down.

## Gameplay

Matches move quickly and ask you to switch between attacking and defending. The 2v2 mode also takes teamwork and strategy. Brief pauses between rounds break up the action, but there are no long cutscenes.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You play with a controller, so both hands stay occupied. The fights need quick reactions, which can be awkward while running or riding. The directional inputs and button presses are simple to follow, but this is not hands free.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is free to play and not too demanding, so a mid range PC should be enough. It requires about 4GB of RAM and a decent graphics card. Higher visual settings may cause dropped frames if your setup cannot handle them.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

MultiVersus is a decent option if you want a fighting game to keep you occupied during a workout. The controller and quick reactions can get in the way, so it is a workable fit rather than an easy one.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
