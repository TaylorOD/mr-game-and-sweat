Sons Of The Forest mixes survival and horror in a world full of danger and things to explore. There’s plenty to do, but it can be tricky to play while you work out.

## Gameplay

You can craft, build, and scavenge as you explore, with creatures to fend off along the way. There’s enough to keep you busy, and few long cutscenes to interrupt your workout. The gameplay is the strongest part of the package.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a standard controller, but some situations call for quick reactions, especially when evading enemies. That can be tough when you’re walking or biking, so you’ll need to keep your pace manageable and stay focused. It works for workouts, but it isn’t an easy fit.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game needs a decent computer to run smoothly, with a powerful GPU and ample RAM recommended. Lag can distract from both the game and your workout, so your setup matters.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Sons Of The Forest has engaging survival gameplay, but the quick movements can make it awkward during cardio. A capable computer also matters if you want to avoid interruptions.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3 

The gameplay is strong, but the workout controls and computer demands hold it back. It’s a decent option for cardio, as long as you’re ready to slow down and stay focused.
