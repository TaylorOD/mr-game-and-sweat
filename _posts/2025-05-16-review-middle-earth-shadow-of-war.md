Middle-earth: Shadow of War follows Shadow of Mordor with action RPG combat and character progression. It has some good fights, but the long story breaks make it a mixed pick for a workout.

## Gameplay

Combat lets you switch between melee, ranged, and special moves, and taking on orc captains or sieges can be fun. But frequent cutscenes and long dialogue slow things down, so the pace is uneven.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controls need moderate input, which is manageable while you exercise. Quick reactions in combat and escape scenes can still make it hard to keep a steady pace.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game has demanding recommended system requirements. Older computers may have performance issues.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The combat can be enjoyable, but cutscenes and quick reaction moments get in the way of a steady workout. It is playable on a treadmill or bike, but it is not an easy game to exercise with.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3
