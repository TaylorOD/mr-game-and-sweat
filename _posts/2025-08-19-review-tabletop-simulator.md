Tabletop Simulator lets you play board games, card games, and more in a virtual space. You can also modify games and create your own, but it is not always a smooth fit for working out.

## Gameplay

There is a wide variety of games, from chess and poker to more complex tabletop role playing games. You pick up pieces, roll dice, and move tokens by hand, which can feel engaging, but slow turns and waiting on other players can drag. It is a decent option for exercise, though the pace is not always steady.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller, mouse, or keyboard, and the lack of quick reactions makes it manageable while exercising. Grabbing and moving pieces takes focus, so it suits treadmill walking or relaxed biking better than a hard workout. It works, but it takes some attention away from exercising.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Tabletop Simulator runs on fairly modest hardware, with functional 3D models that are not demanding. The minimum requirements are an Intel Core 2 Duo, 4 GB RAM, and a GPU that supports DirectX 9. You also need a stable internet connection for a smoother online experience.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Tabletop Simulator is a good fit for board game fans, but it is not especially fast paced for a workout. Its flexible pace makes it playable while exercising, though waiting and handling pieces can get in the way. Its low computer requirements are a plus, but overall it is only a decent workout choice.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
