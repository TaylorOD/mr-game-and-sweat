The Enforcer versus Criminal setup sounds like a fun way to keep a steady workout moving, but I’d save the serious shootouts for when you’re sitting down. APB Reloaded puts you in a crowded online city, and other players are not going to wait while you get your balance back.

## Gameplay

I think the appeal here is pretty clear: you pick a side, customize your character and gear, then head into the city for action with other players around. That can make the streets feel busy in a way a single player shooter can’t quite manage, and I like the idea of getting into a game without paying upfront. The free part is nice. The rest still needs to be worth your time.

The customization seems like a big part of the deal, and that can be a fun reason to stick around if you enjoy making a character feel like yours. I’d probably spend longer deciding what my character should look like than I’d like to admit, which is not exactly the point of logging in to play a shooter. At least that’s a choice you can make for yourself.

The action is where I think APB will keep your attention. You’re doing missions in a city where the other side is made up of people, so I’d expect the encounters to feel less predictable than shooting at the same computer controlled enemies over and over. I can’t tell you how well every fight plays out, but the idea of a street suddenly turning into a firefight sounds like it could pull you right into the game.

Driving around between the action gives the game some breathing room, and I think that’s a better fit for a workout than a game that asks you to aim constantly from the first minute to the last. But the breathing room comes with a lot of moving around in an online world, and I don’t know how quickly you’ll get to the next interesting bit. You may spend some time getting where you’re going.

I’d try it if you like online shooters and want something free to sample, especially if customizing your character is part of the fun for you. If you mainly want a fast, straightforward action game with no other players complicating the plan, this probably isn’t going to give you that. You’re joining a city that’s already in motion.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The unpredictable fights are what make me hesitant to recommend this while you’re moving. In a third person shooter, aiming and turning quickly matter, and APB’s missions put you up against other players who can take advantage of a moment when you’re not paying full attention. I’d keep it for an easy, steady workout, not intervals where you’re already trying to get your breathing under control.

A bike may be the easier place to manage the aiming, since your hands don’t have to keep you steady, but I still wouldn’t expect the game to wait for you during an online fight. On a treadmill, I’d lower the pace and skip playing during any part of a workout where you need to move fast. It’s not that you can’t play while moving. It’s that the game may ask for your full attention at the exact wrong time.

Controller support is something I’d check and test before setting up a workout around it. Even with a controller, I think shooting at human opponents while your hands are bouncing around could get frustrating pretty quickly. Driving around or getting through quieter stretches sounds more manageable, though you can’t count on the city staying quiet.

I’d also be ready to pause the workout instead of trying to rescue a match when things get hectic. That’s not ideal for a game you want to play while exercising, but it’s better than treating every firefight like you’re competing for a trophy. Maybe just keep the pace modest.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The listed minimums are old enough that you don’t need a powerful modern gaming computer to meet them, though the game does require a broadband connection and the action depends on playing online. I’d check that your graphics hardware can handle the game first, especially if you’re using an older computer.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

APB Reloaded makes sense if you want a free online shooter and like building a character, but I’d play it seated before trying to mix it with a workout. For exercise gaming, I’d only give it a try at an easy pace if you’re comfortable with online fights demanding your attention.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
