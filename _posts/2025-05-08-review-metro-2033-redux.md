Metro 2033 Redux puts you in the Moscow Metro after the apocalypse, where survival means sneaking and managing supplies. The story slows down at times, so the action is not always steady.

## Gameplay

The game mixes horror, stealth, and action, but some sections move slowly. Long cutscenes and dialogue can distract you from your workout. The dark tunnels and mutated foes keep you alert when the action picks up.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

A standard controller is comfortable to hold during longer sessions. You do not need perfect split second aim, but sudden threats and frantic dodging can be tough when your attention is split. Slower moments give you a chance to settle into a steady pace.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is not especially demanding for its age. A midrange PC with 4GB of RAM and a GTX 460 or Radeon HD 6850 can run it on low to medium settings. Older systems may need adjusted settings to keep performance smooth.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The story and gameplay are good, but the slow sections make this a mixed workout game. It fits a steady pace better than a high intensity session.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
