Assassin’s Creed 2 has plenty of climbing and combat to keep you engaged, but long story scenes interrupt the pace. Stealth and rooftop jumps can also be tough to manage while you exercise.

## Gameplay

You explore Renaissance Italy, climb across rooftops, fight guards, and take on missions tied to a story of family, vengeance, and conspiracy. The mix is engaging, but travel and story scenes slow things down. **2 out of 3** fits its bursts of action and its quieter stretches.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

A controller works well, and basic movement is easy to follow. But stealth, timed moves, and precise rooftop jumps need your attention, so this can be awkward while you’re moving.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum specs call for an older dedicated graphics card with 256 MB of memory and DirectX 9 support. That is a modest bar by modern standards, though the listed requirements do not promise smooth play on integrated graphics.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Assassin’s Creed 2 has a good mix of exploration, climbing, and combat, with a story that takes its time. It is better suited to a bike or a slow treadmill pace than a workout where you need to keep your eyes off the screen.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3
