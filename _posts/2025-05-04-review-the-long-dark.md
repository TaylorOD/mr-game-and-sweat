The Long Dark is a survival game set in the cold wilderness of Canada. You play a pilot stranded after a mysterious geomagnetic disaster, and the focus is on dealing with the weather and wildlife.

## Gameplay

You spend your time gathering resources, crafting tools, and managing your health, warmth, and hunger. The pace is measured, with little frantic action, and the few cut scenes leave plenty of time to explore. Wolves and bears add some tension, but they do not make the whole game hard to follow while exercising.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game works well with a controller, and its methodical pace does not ask for quick button presses. That makes it easy to keep your exercise rhythm as you play. Resource gathering and setting up camp also give you natural chances to take a breather or adjust your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The Long Dark does not seem especially demanding, and its art style uses atmospheric 3D and hand painted textures. The review says it can run on a range of systems, though its requirements are not quite a clear win. Minimum requirements point to a modest setup, but that still leaves some uncertainty about older machines.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The survival mechanics and steady pace make The Long Dark a good workout game for both treadmill and bike use. Its computer requirements are less of a sure thing, which keeps the overall package from being an easy recommendation.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
