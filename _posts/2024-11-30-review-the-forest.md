**The Forest** starts with you stranded on an island after a plane crash. You scavenge, build shelters, and fend off cannibals and other threats.

## Gameplay

Gathering supplies, crafting tools, exploring, and fighting give you plenty to do. Nighttime threats keep you alert, and there are few slow stretches or long cutscenes. The gameplay is the strongest part of the game.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a standard controller, and the controls do not demand constant quick reactions. Still, running or dodging can be tricky while you exercise, and pauses for building can break your rhythm. It works for a workout, but it is not an effortless fit.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum requirements are fairly modest, with an NVIDIA GTX 660 or equivalent and a reasonable amount of RAM listed. It should run on a range of systems, though the requirements are not especially light or fully specific.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The survival gameplay makes The Forest worth a look, but the workout fit has some rough spots. Controller play is manageable, though movement and building pauses can get in the way.

### Overall Score: 2.3 out of 3
