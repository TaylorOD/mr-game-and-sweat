Dragon's Dogma 2 has a big fantasy world, engaging combat, and a wide range of quests. It works well for exercise, though its computer requirements could be a hurdle.

## Gameplay

Combat is the main draw, with plenty of strategic options from the pawn system. Quests keep things moving, and most cutscenes are short, so you spend less time waiting around. Exploration and boss fights ask for focus, but the action gives you something to do while you work out.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game supports a controller, and most of its action should be manageable while you run or ride. Dodging and climbing larger enemies call for quick reactions, so you may need to focus during those moments. Overall, it fits treadmill and bike play well.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The official specs have not been announced, so the review estimates that the game may need a mid range computer, a decent graphics card, and at least 8GB of RAM. Higher settings will need stronger hardware, which could be a problem for older computers. You may have to lower the graphics settings to get good performance.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Dragon's Dogma 2 has strong combat and works well for exercise, but its computer requirements are a drawback. It is a good workout game if your computer can handle it.

### Overall Score: (3+3+2)/3 = 2.67 out of 3
