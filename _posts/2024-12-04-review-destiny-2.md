Destiny 2 is a first person shooter with missions, different game modes, and a sci fi story. It has a lot going on, though it is not always a smooth fit for exercise.

## Gameplay

Combat moves quickly, and missions mix shooting, powers, and exploration. Cutscenes slow things down at times, but they usually happen between mission sections or before boss fights. That makes the action a decent workout companion, even if the pace is not constant.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller lets you play without quick mouse movements, and most encounters do not demand frantic reactions. Some fights call for dodging or moving quickly, so keeping a steady workout pace still matters. It is easy to get focused on the game and let your exercise slip.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Destiny 2 needs a capable computer, especially at higher resolutions and frame rates. You can adjust settings to suit your hardware, but lower end systems may still have lag. It is generally well optimized for modern computers, though not every setup will run it smoothly.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Destiny 2 has enough action to make it a solid option for running or cycling, and controller play helps. Slower moments and the attention the game asks for keep it from being a perfect workout fit. It is worth considering if your computer can handle it.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
