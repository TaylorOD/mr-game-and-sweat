Toby Fox’s Undertale is an RPG with unusual combat, story choices, and memorable characters. It has some good workout fit, but slower parts keep it from being an easy pick.

## Gameplay

You can fight or look for peaceful solutions, and your choices affect the story. The turn based battles leave time for decisions, but dodging projectiles still takes focus, and long dialogue or cutscenes can interrupt your pace.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support means you do not need to manage keyboard inputs while exercising. Turn based combat helps, but boss fights need more attention, so it is not always easy to focus on your workout too.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Undertale runs smoothly on older hardware and does not need much computer power. You can play without worrying much about lag or demanding graphics.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Undertale is a good RPG, but its slower stretches and moments that need your attention make it only a decent workout game. Its controller support and low computer requirements help.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
