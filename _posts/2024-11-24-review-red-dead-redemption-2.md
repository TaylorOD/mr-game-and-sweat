Red Dead Redemption 2 has a lively world, interesting characters, and plenty of things to do. But long conversations and cutscenes can leave you watching instead of moving.

## Gameplay

Exploring and taking on side quests can keep you interested, but the pace often slows down. You may end up standing still during long story scenes, which is not ideal for a run or ride.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller while focusing on your movement, but combat and navigation need precise inputs. When the action heats up, you may need to slow down or stop to keep up.

### Category Score: 1.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game asks a lot of your computer, including a decent graphics card and enough RAM. You may need a powerful setup, which can be awkward to fit around a workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The story and world are strong, but the slow pace and cutscenes can pull your attention away from exercise. It can work for a workout if you are fine with easing up when the game gets busy.

### Overall Score: 1.83 out of 3
