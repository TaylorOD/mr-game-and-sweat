XCOM 2 is a turn based strategy game about leading a resistance against an alien regime controlling Earth. Managing your squad and planning missions have appeal, but long loading times and slower scenes can make the pace drag.

## Gameplay

Managing a squad gives you plenty to think about, and the missions put your decisions to the test. Long loading times, dialogue and cutscenes slow things down, while the turn based combat gives you time to plan between moves.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game is mainly designed for keyboard and mouse, but it supports controllers, which makes it easier to use on a treadmill or bike. It does not depend much on quick reflexes, though keeping your focus on both the game and your workout can still take some effort.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The system requirements are moderate: at least an Intel Core 2 Duo or equivalent, 4 GB of RAM and a graphics card like the NVIDIA GTX 460. It can run on lower settings, but less powerful PCs may lag during busy action scenes.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

XCOM 2 has solid strategy and controller support, but slow sections can interrupt your workout. It works for exercise, though it is not an effortless fit.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
