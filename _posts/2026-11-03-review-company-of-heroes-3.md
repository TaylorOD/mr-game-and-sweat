Company of Heroes 3 is a poor workout game because you need to track squads, read the battlefield, and issue precise orders. Its tactical fights are great when you can give them your full attention, which is exactly why they clash with running or biking.

## Gameplay

You control infantry and vehicles in real time, using cover, flanking, and squad abilities to win fights. The battles are engaging, but there are slower stretches where you need to study the map and plan your next move. That kind of focus will distract you from your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Company of Heroes 3 asks for frequent, precise orders and careful attention to several squads at once. Trying to manage a battle while moving is a good way to miss a threat or send a unit somewhere silly. Keep your hands and eyes on the game.

### Category Score: 0 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum specs call for 8 GB of memory and a GTX 950 or Radeon R9 370, along with a 64 bit processor and 40 GB of storage. That is an older dedicated graphics card, but you still need more than basic integrated graphics.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Company of Heroes 3 is a smart, satisfying strategy game, but it demands too much attention to pair well with exercise. Play it at your desk.

### Overall Score: 1.33 out of 3
