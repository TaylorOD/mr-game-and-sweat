MiSide mixes RPG elements with adventure, quests, character progression, and exploration. It can be a decent distraction while you exercise, though it does not always fit smoothly into a workout.

## Gameplay

The mix of puzzles and combat keeps the gameplay engaging. The pacing is mostly good, with a few slow sections and occasional cutscenes you can skip. It gives you enough to focus on without constantly demanding your attention.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

MiSide has full controller support, and its generally forgiving pace works on a treadmill or bike. Some parts call for quick movements, and intense battles can throw off your rhythm. So it is playable during exercise, but not always a comfortable fit.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game needs a decent graphics card and processor, though it can run on mid range computers. Performance is generally smooth, with little lag even in busy scenes.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

MiSide has engaging gameplay and mostly well paced missions, but some action can interfere with your workout. Its controller support and computer requirements are manageable, though neither makes it a perfect exercise game. Overall, it is a solid game with a few workout friendly limits.

### Overall Score: 2.3 out of 3
