Elite Dangerous gives you a huge galaxy to explore, trade in, and fight across. It can fit a treadmill or bike session, but long waits and busy fights make it uneven.

## Gameplay

The galaxy is huge, with exploration, trading, combat, and other activities. Once you get through the learning curve, exploring and missions can be satisfying, though long trips and waiting for NPCs slow things down. Those quiet stretches can drag, so keep track of your ship and objectives if you want to stay involved.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You do some ship management as you move between star systems, but most of the game does not demand fast reactions. Controller support helps, though dogfights can pull your focus from running or biking. It works reasonably well for exercise, but still asks you to pay attention.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Elite Dangerous asks a fair bit from your computer, especially at higher settings. A decent setup with a solid graphics card, processor, and enough RAM helps it run well. Older hardware may need lower graphics settings, so computer requirements are a moderate concern.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Elite Dangerous has enough exploration and other activities to hold your interest, but slow travel can interrupt a workout. Controller support helps, though dogfights still take focus, and the game needs a reasonably capable computer. It is a decent fit for treadmill or bike play, but not an effortless one.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
