Firewatch follows Henry, a fire lookout spending the summer alone in the Wyoming woods. A series of events gives him something to investigate.

## Gameplay

You explore, find clues, and talk with people as the story unfolds. The relaxed pace suits a workout, but long conversations and cut scenes can distract you. If you want constant action, the quiet stretches may drag.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game works well with a controller while you ride or walk. Walking, climbing, and looking around are unhurried, so you can adjust your pace without much trouble. During longer story sections, though, your attention may drift from the game.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Firewatch runs on a relatively low end computer. You do not need a powerful machine to play it.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Firewatch is a good fit for treadmill and bike workouts, and its computer requirements are modest. The slow gameplay and long dialogue sections are not for everyone. The story and workout friendly pace make it a solid choice, though not a perfect one.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
