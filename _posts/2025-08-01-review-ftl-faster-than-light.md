FTL: Faster Than Light is a strategy game where you command a spaceship through a dangerous galaxy. You face hostile enemies and manage your crew and resources.

## Gameplay

You handle events, crew and resources while fighting tactically and racing from an enemy on your trail. Most of the game moves quickly, with short pauses for dialogue and ship upgrades. There is plenty to think about, and the action gives the game a satisfying pace.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

FTL mainly uses a mouse, which can be tough to manage while exercising. The strategy does not demand fast reflexes, and you can pause for tactical decisions, but it still is not an easy fit for a run or ride.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

FTL has low computer requirements and should run on most systems. Its simple 2D graphics make it a good choice if your computer is doing double duty during a workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

FTL has strong strategy gameplay and runs on modest hardware. The mouse controls make it less convenient during exercise, so it is a good game with some real workout drawbacks.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
