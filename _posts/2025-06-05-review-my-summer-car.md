My Summer Car mixes car repair, survival, and everyday chores in a Finnish summer. Building and fixing the car can be chaotic, but the slower pace makes it a decent, if not perfect, workout companion.

## Gameplay

There is plenty to do, from tuning your car to visiting the store and gathering parts. The routine can keep you busy, though some tasks drag and can make it harder to keep your workout intensity up.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game does not demand quick reflexes, but you still need to pay attention to the controller or keyboard. Its slower pace and lack of rapid inputs make it manageable while exercising, as long as you can stay focused on both.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

My Summer Car is relatively modest on computer requirements and runs well on mid range systems. Higher settings can put more strain on your computer, and the graphics or physics can cause an occasional hiccup.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Car repairs and daily chores give you something to focus on during a workout, and the controls are not too demanding. Slow stretches and the need to pay attention keep it from being an ideal fit, but it works well enough on a treadmill or bike.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
