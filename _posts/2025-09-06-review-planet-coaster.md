Planet Coaster lets you build roller coasters, manage a park, and keep visitors happy. There is plenty to customize, so you have lots to work on as you exercise.

## Gameplay

Building rides and managing the park keeps you busy, and the customization gives you plenty of ways to shape it. The interface is easy to use, and there are few slow stretches to pull your attention away.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller makes menus and park design manageable while you exercise. You do not need quick reactions, though occasional camera adjustments take a little attention. You can also set your own pace without feeling overwhelmed.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Planet Coaster asks more of your computer than simpler games, and sprawling parks run more smoothly on better hardware. Older systems can run it if you keep the visuals below maximum, but performance depends on your setup.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Planet Coaster gives you a solid way to fill cardio time with park building and management. Its computer demands are a drawback, but the controller support and relaxed pace make it a good fit for a treadmill or bike.

### Overall Score: 2.66 out of 3
