Battlefield 4 puts you in large multiplayer battles that mix combat with strategy. The action can be fun, but it is not consistently busy enough to keep every workout moving.

## Gameplay

Combat has plenty of explosive moments, but the pace depends on the game type. Conquest can leave you waiting while teams regroup, and respawn waits or cut scenes can interrupt your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controls are intuitive, and the game does not rely heavily on fast movements, so you can keep a steady pace on a treadmill or bike. Battles sometimes call for quick decisions, though, and those can pull your attention away from exercising.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Battlefield 4 has fairly demanding computer requirements. The recommended setup includes a CPU comparable to an Intel Core i5, an NVIDIA GTX 660 or equivalent, and around 8GB of RAM.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Battlefield 4 can pair well enough with a workout, but slow stretches and attention demanding battles keep it from being an easy fit. It has fun moments, though the interruptions make the overall experience mixed.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
