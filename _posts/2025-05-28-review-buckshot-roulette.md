## Gameplay

Luck and skill both matter as you face shootouts, traps, and enemies. The pace stays busy, with few slow stretches, and the short cut scenes add some humor. It is easy to focus on dodging shots and firing back.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

It works with a controller and does not ask for rapid inputs, so you can keep your pace on a treadmill or bike. The firefights are easy to fit around running or cycling. Just keep in mind that the action can draw your attention away from your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You do not need a high end computer, but the game is aimed at mid tier systems. That makes its requirements reasonable, not a major selling point.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Buckshot Roulette is a strong pick if you want brisk gameplay that fits a treadmill or bike session. Its computer requirements are only okay, so the whole package is good rather than essential.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
