Counter-Strike: Global Offensive is built around competitive multiplayer matches and short rounds. That keeps things moving, but the game can be tough to juggle with a workout.

## Gameplay

The short rounds mean you do not have to sit through long cutscenes or waits. Still, sneaking around, reacting quickly, and trying to win a round take a lot of focus, so it is only a mixed fit for exercise.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

CS:GO mainly uses a keyboard and mouse, though some players use gamepads. Its quick movements and reactions can make it hard to keep a steady pace on a treadmill or bike, even with practice.

### Category Score: 1.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game has modest computer requirements, including a minimum of 4 GB of RAM and a processor such as an Intel Core 2 Duo or AMD Athlon 64. That makes it workable on older computers without demanding much from your setup.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

CS:GO has quick rounds and low computer requirements, which help when you want to game while exercising. But its focus on fast reactions makes it hard to balance with running or cycling, so it is a decent option rather than a great workout game.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
