New World: Aeternum is an MMO with exploration, survival, and role playing on an island. It has plenty to do, but it takes some focus to play while you exercise.

## Gameplay

You explore, take quests, craft, and fight mobs, with no long cutscenes to pull you away from the action. Combat has some slower stretches, but it usually moves along well, even if the pacing is not perfect.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support helps when you are on a treadmill or bike. Combat leans more on timing and positioning than quick reflexes, though some encounters need more hand eye coordination and screen focus.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game can be demanding if you want 60 FPS at ultra settings in dense areas. A midrange gaming PC should manage, but lowering the graphics may help, and a solid internet connection matters for the MMO.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

There is enough action and exploration to keep you playing, and the controller works well for exercise. Still, some fights can take your attention off your workout, so it is only a decent fit.

### Overall Score: 2.17 out of 3 

New World: Aeternum is a solid game for exercise in parts, but it asks for more focus than you may want while moving. It is worth a try if you are fine with pausing your workout attention during tougher fights.
