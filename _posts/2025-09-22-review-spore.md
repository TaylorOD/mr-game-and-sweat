SPORE moves from evolving a microbe to building a civilization and exploring space. The different stages give you plenty of variety, though some parts slow down.

## Gameplay

You start in a petri dish, gathering nutrients and evolving your creature. The game then moves through creature, tribal, civilization, and space stages, but transitions and stretches of exploration can drag. That variety helps, but the slower moments can throw off your rhythm.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

SPORE uses mouse and keyboard controls and does not demand quick reflexes. Still, some stages involve plenty of navigation and clicking, which can be awkward while exercising. Holding a mouse while keeping a steady pace is its own small challenge.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The system requirements are modest, and the game runs smoothly on most mid range PCs. You do not need the latest hardware to play.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3 

## Conclusion and Score

SPORE has enough variety to make a workout session more interesting, but its slower moments and awkward controls hold it back. It can work on a treadmill or bike, as long as you are fine with pausing your pace to navigate and click.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
