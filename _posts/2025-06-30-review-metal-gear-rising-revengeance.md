## Gameplay

The sword combat feels responsive, and chaining moves is satisfying. Quick reactions keep you engaged, while brief cut scenes break up the action now and then.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

A controller makes the game workable on a treadmill or bike, but the quick reactions take attention away from keeping a steady pace. Boss fights can be harder to manage while exercising, so this is better at a moderate pace than an intense one.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is not too demanding for a PC. A midrange system should handle it, and decent hardware can run it at 60 fps. A decent processor and mid tier graphics card should be enough.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The action is fun, and the combat gives you plenty to focus on. But the quick inputs and boss fights make it only a decent fit for a workout. The brief cut scenes are a small pause, not a major problem.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
