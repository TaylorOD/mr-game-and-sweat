Gunfire Reborn is a first person shooter with action and RPG elements, wrapped in a roguelite format. You fight fantastical creatures across different environments.

## Gameplay

Combat is fast and engaging, and each character has unique abilities. You can play solo or in co op, and there are no long cutscenes or tedious pauses to break up a session. The action keeps you focused without making you lose track of your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game supports a controller, and its twin stick shooting works well while you exercise. Combat gets intense at times, but it does not demand rapid movements or twitch reactions, so you can keep a steady rhythm.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Gunfire Reborn runs on mid range computers, so you do not need a high end machine. It typically needs around 2GB of RAM and an entry level graphics card.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Gunfire Reborn is a good fit for exercise thanks to its engaging gameplay, controller support, and modest computer requirements. It gives you plenty to focus on while you run or ride.

### Overall Score: 3 out of 3
