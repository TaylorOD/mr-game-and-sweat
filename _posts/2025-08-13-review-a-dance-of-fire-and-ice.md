## Gameplay

You guide two orbiting circles along a winding path and time your input to the music. The controls are simple, and the quick level changes keep you engaged without lengthy cutscenes or slow sections.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game uses basic inputs and does not call for sudden, twitchy reflexes, so you can keep a steady pace while playing. It supports controller input, which makes it easy to use while running or cycling, though you still need to follow the screen and the beat.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game has modest computer requirements and can run on entry level systems. Its pixelated art style does not demand much from your computer.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

A Dance of Fire and Ice pairs simple rhythmic play with controls that work well for treadmill and bike workouts. It is easy on computer resources, too, making it a solid choice if you want a game that keeps moving without pulling you away from your workout.

### Overall Score: 3 out of 3
