Outlast is a first person horror game about surviving Mount Massive Asylum. You play investigative journalist Miles Upshur, with only a night vision camcorder to help you.

## Gameplay

The game has a steady pace, with stealth, problem solving, and few slow sections. That makes it engaging, but the tense encounters can take your focus off your workout.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Outlast works with a standard controller, but quick movements and interactions need some precision. You can play while exercising, though you may want to pause when things get tricky.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Outlast runs best on a fairly capable computer, but it does not need top tier hardware. The minimum requirements are a dual core 2.4 GHz CPU, 4 GB of RAM, and a dedicated graphics card such as an NVIDIA GeForce 8800 or AMD Radeon HD 4000 series.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Outlast is a strong horror game with little downtime, but it can distract you from your workout. The controller setup is workable, though the quick actions make it less comfortable to play while exercising.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
