REMNANT II keeps you busy with gunfights and boss battles, but that focus can make treadmill running awkward. A bike is the safer choice, though aiming and dodging still need steady hands.

## Gameplay

The shooting, exploration, and boss fights give you plenty to do, and the combat is engaging. There are slower stretches between fights, so the pace is not constant. Gear and character choices add more time spent thinking than fighting.  
### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game has full controller support, which is a good start. But aiming, dodging, and reacting to bosses take precision, so loose inputs while running can get you in trouble. A stationary bike is a more comfortable fit.  
### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum specs call for a GTX 1650 or Radeon RX 590, 16 GB of RAM, and 80 GB of storage. That is a basic dedicated graphics card, not an integrated graphics setup.  
### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

REMNANT II is a good action RPG, but its precise combat makes it a tough pick for treadmill play. Try it on a bike if you want to exercise while gaming, and save the harder boss fights for when you can focus.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3
