Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor puts you in control of Talion, a ranger with the power of a wraith. Its combat and movement are fun, but the slower stretches make it a mixed fit for a workout.

## Gameplay

Talion can leap around and fight with fluid moves, and chaining attacks feels good. Long cutscenes and slower stretches can break your rhythm, so the gameplay is solid but not ideal for keeping a steady workout pace.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller or keyboard, but combat and stealth sometimes call for quick reflexes. Keeping an eye on the action while you run or ride can be tricky, so it is manageable but takes some focus.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is moderately demanding on your computer. You will want a solid graphics card and a decent amount of memory, and older hardware may need a check against the game’s requirements.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Shadow of Mordor has enough enjoyable action to work for a workout, but cutscenes and moments that need concentration can get in the way. It is a decent choice for the treadmill or bike, not an easy game to play on autopilot.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
