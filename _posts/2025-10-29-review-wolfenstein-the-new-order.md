**Wolfenstein: The New Order** takes place in an alternate history where the Nazis won World War II. As B.J. Blazkowicz, you set out to fight their regime, though the game’s slower moments can interrupt a workout.

## Gameplay

The shooting is fast and engaging, with stealth and exploration mixed in. Long cutscenes and slower sections can break your rhythm, so the gameplay is a mixed fit for exercise.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support helps when you’re on a treadmill or bike, but chaotic firefights and precise aiming demand your attention. It can work, though you may need to ease up on your pace during the action.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game needs a dedicated GPU and reasonable processing power to run smoothly. It calls for at least 4GB of RAM and a mid-range graphics card, so it may be a stretch for a lower end computer.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The action and story have their appeal, but cutscenes and demanding firefights can disrupt your workout. It works for treadmill or bike play, though it is not an easy game to keep up with while exercising.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
