SCUM is a multiplayer survival game set in a big open world. You balance survival needs with character customization while dealing with the environment and other players.

## Gameplay

Hunger, thirst, body management, and gathering resources give you plenty to keep track of. Exploring, crafting, and fighting can hold your attention, but stealth and scavenging slow things down. The quick cut scenes do not get in the way much.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a standard controller, which helps on a treadmill or bike. Quick reactions and tricky terrain can be hard to manage while exercising, so it is easier at a steady pace. You may need to slow down during hectic moments.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

SCUM asks a lot of your computer and needs at least 8 GB of RAM for a smooth experience. Older systems may have performance problems. A modern processor and graphics card are recommended, so check your setup before playing.

### Category Score: 1.5 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

SCUM has engaging survival gameplay, but its slower stretches and demanding computer requirements hold it back as a workout game. Controller support helps, though it is not always easy to play while running or riding.

### Overall Score: 2.0 out of 3
