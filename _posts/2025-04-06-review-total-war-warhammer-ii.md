Total War: WARHAMMER II mixes turn based strategy with real time battles in the Warhammer world. There is plenty to plan, which can be awkward when you are trying to keep moving.

## Gameplay

Managing resources and troop movements gives you a lot to think about, and battles can run long. You can automate battles, but it is tempting to take control yourself. Cutscenes and narration also slow things down, and keeping track of the game can distract from your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game works best with a mouse and keyboard or controller, so it takes some setup to play while exercising. It does not demand quick reactions, but long stretches of planning can leave you doing little besides watching the screen. You can keep an eye on the game while holding your pace, but you will need to manage both.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game can be demanding on a computer, especially at high settings or on larger maps. The minimum specs include an Intel Core 2 Duo, 2GB of RAM and a dedicated GPU such as the NVIDIA GTX 460, and lag or loading can interrupt your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

There is a good mix of planning and battles, but the game asks for more attention than you may want to give it during exercise. It is a decent option if your workout comes first, though it can pull your focus away.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3

You can play it in stretches while on a treadmill or bike, but do not expect it to run itself while you exercise. The slower pace helps, though long battles and planning make it an imperfect workout game.
