Clicker Heroes is an incremental game where you click monsters, unlock heroes, and collect gold. Much of the game is watching numbers go up while your heroes fight.

## Gameplay

You start by clicking monsters, then unlock and upgrade heroes, which adds some strategy to the repetitive clicking. Automatic progress leaves you free to focus on your workout, but it can also mean long stretches where little happens.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game asks for no quick reflexes or precise movements. You can click at your own pace and watch your heroes battle, so it is easy to keep up while exercising.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Clicker Heroes runs well on low end hardware and has simple 2D graphics. You do not need a powerful processor or graphics card to play it.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Clicker Heroes is a simple, low fuss game for a workout, and it does not need much from your computer. The repetitive clicking and slow moments keep it from being more than a decent fit.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
