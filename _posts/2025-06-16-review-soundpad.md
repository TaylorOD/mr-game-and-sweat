Soundpad is built around creating and manipulating sound. You can also sync music to visual experiences.

## Gameplay

The gameplay has you reacting to changing audio cues and making your own sound effects. It stays active with little downtime, which makes it easy to keep playing, but the fast pace can ask for more attention than a workout game should.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Soundpad supports a controller and is described as somewhat hands free, but it still relies on rhythmic timing. If coordination is a challenge while you run or ride, that can get in the way of your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Soundpad has modest computer requirements and does not need a powerful graphics card or much processing power. It should run smoothly on a range of systems, including older ones.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Soundpad is engaging to play, and its light computer requirements are a plus. But the timing and coordination demands make it only a decent fit for running or riding.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
