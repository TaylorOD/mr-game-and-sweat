Satisfactory mixes factory building with exploration on an alien planet. You gather resources and build production lines, then manage them as your factory grows.

## Gameplay

The loop of gathering, crafting, and improving your factory gives you plenty to do. Unlocking technology and expanding your operation keeps the game moving, while planning your layout gives you a chance to settle into a steady pace. There are no long cut scenes to break up your play.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use keyboard and mouse or a gamepad. Most of the game is calm exploration and building, with little need for quick reactions. You may still pause to check production or find your way through the landscape, but much of the time you can play at a relaxed pace.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Satisfactory needs a decent computer to run smoothly. The minimum specs listed are an Intel Core i5, 8GB of RAM, and a graphics card equivalent to an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660. The recommended specs include an Intel Core i7 and a GTX 1060 or better, so a weaker computer may cause performance problems during a workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Satisfactory has a strong gameplay loop and works reasonably well for exercise, though pauses can interrupt your rhythm. Its computer requirements are a drawback, so check that your system can run it smoothly.

### Overall Score: 2.5 out of 3
