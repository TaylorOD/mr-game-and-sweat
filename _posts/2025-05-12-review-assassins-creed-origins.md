You play as Bayek in ancient Egypt, where personal vendettas connect to larger conspiracies. There is plenty to explore, though the story can get in the way of keeping your workout moving.

## Gameplay

Quests encourage exploration, and combat mixes melee attacks with ranged abilities. Moving between parkour and fights is fun, but long cutscenes and narrated history snippets slow things down. You may need to change your pace or stop to follow the story.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller works well for playing on a treadmill or bike, and most of the game does not demand quick reflexes. Combat and timed events can still pull your attention from your footing or balance. Those moments are occasional, so the game is usually manageable while you exercise.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements range from moderate to high, especially at higher graphics settings. Lower end hardware may run the game, but slowdowns are possible.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Origins has a good mix of exploration and combat, but the story sometimes interrupts the action. It works reasonably well for exercising, though you will need to be careful during combat and timed events.

### Overall Score: 2.17 out of 3
