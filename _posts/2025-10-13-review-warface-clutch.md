Warface: Clutch is a fast shooter with controller support, so it can work alongside a workout. Still, it asks for more focus than a relaxed game does.

## Gameplay

Matches move quickly, and short respawn times keep you in the action. Team management and loading screens cause a few brief pauses, but the quick pace can make it harder to keep a steady workout rhythm.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support makes the game possible to play on a treadmill or bike. Quick reactions and extra dexterity can be tricky while you exercise, so it takes some balancing.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements are moderate, and you do not need a top tier setup. A decent graphics card and enough RAM can help keep the game running smoothly.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Warface: Clutch offers fast shooter matches, but its quick reactions can make it a mixed fit for exercising. It works on a treadmill or bike with a controller, though you may need to ease up on your workout to play comfortably.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
