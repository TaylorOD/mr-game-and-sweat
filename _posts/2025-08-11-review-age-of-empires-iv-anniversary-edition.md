Age of Empires IV: Anniversary Edition is a strategy game about managing resources, building an empire, and fighting battles. It has plenty to do, but it asks for more attention than you may want to give while exercising.

## Gameplay

Managing your civilization and switching between building and combat keeps the game moving. Campaigns and skirmishes have tasks without long cutscenes, but key battles can make you stop and think through your next move. It’s engaging, though you may lose track of your workout.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support makes it easier to play without a mouse, and the game does not rely on quick reflexes. Still, issuing orders and planning attacks takes focus, so it is only a decent fit for treadmill or bike play. Keep an eye on your effort when the action picks up.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements are moderate. The review lists an Intel i5 or AMD equivalent, 8 GB of RAM, and an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 or AMD RX 560 graphics card. A less powerful computer may struggle.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Age of Empires IV has satisfying strategy and works with a controller, but it can demand more attention than your workout allows. It is a good fit for some exercise sessions, though intense battles can pull your focus away.

### Overall Score: 2.17 out of 3
