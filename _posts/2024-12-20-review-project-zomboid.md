**Project Zomboid** is a survival game where you scavenge for food, build shelter, and manage your character’s mental and physical state. It has a lot of strategy and gives you a mix of tense zombie encounters and quieter stretches to explore or fortify your defenses.

## Gameplay

The survival systems give you plenty to think about, from crafting items to finding food and avoiding zombies. The pace shifts between tense moments and quieter tasks, so you have room to keep a steady workout going. There are no lengthy cutscenes or drawn out tutorials to interrupt play.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controls use an analog stick and buttons, and the pace does not demand constant quick reactions. That makes it a comfortable fit for a treadmill or stationary bike. In game exploration can match a moderate workout without asking you to make rapid movements all the time.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is not especially demanding on graphics, and it can run on modest PCs. Still, large zombie hordes and exploring the expansive map can benefit from a decent processor and memory. Older hardware may work, but performance is not something to ignore.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Project Zomboid has strong survival gameplay and works well for exercise, thanks to its steady pace and controller controls. Its PC requirements are manageable, but large hordes and map exploration can ask more of your system.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
