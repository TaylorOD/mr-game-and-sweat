Apex Legends mixes battle royale shooting, character abilities, and squad strategy. It is fun to play, but the quick movement and aiming can be awkward while you exercise.

## Gameplay

The shooting, abilities, and team strategy make for engaging gameplay. There is little downtime or long cutscenes, so you stay focused on the action.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller, but rapid aiming and quick direction changes take a lot of focus. That can make it tough to keep a steady rhythm on a treadmill or bike, especially if you are moving fast.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum computer requirements are fairly low, so mid range PCs can run the game. You will need a stronger setup for higher graphics settings.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Apex Legends has engaging gameplay, but its fast controls are a challenge during a workout. It can work if you find a comfortable pace, though it is not an easy game to play while exercising.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
