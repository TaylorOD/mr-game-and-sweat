CarX Drift Racing Online is built around drifting, with realistic physics and plenty of customization. The driving feels good, but its fit for a treadmill or bike is only okay.

## Gameplay

The drifting feels satisfying, with a strong sense of speed and precision. There are few slow sections or long cutscenes, so you spend most of your time driving. Online multiplayer and time trials give you more challenges to tackle.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support makes it comfortable to play while exercising, and the controls are intuitive enough that you do not need quick, jerky movements. You can focus on keeping your pace while driving. Still, it is a less useful workout pick if you want fast twitch action.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements are reasonable, and you do not need a top tier PC to play. Midrange PCs should handle it, and the game is well optimized. That makes it accessible, though the requirements are not a major selling point.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

CarX Drift Racing Online has satisfying drifting and very few interruptions, which makes it fun to play. It works reasonably well on a treadmill or bike, but fast interactions may not suit every workout. With reasonable computer requirements, it is a solid option, though not an exceptional workout game.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
