Batman: Arkham Origins follows a younger Bruce Wayne as he becomes the Dark Knight in Gotham City. Its darker story gives you a solid reason to keep playing, though it is not always a smooth fit for exercise.

## Gameplay

The open world, voice performances, stealth, combat, and detective sections give you plenty to do. Slow stretches and long cutscenes can interrupt your pace, while combat asks for more focus than you may want to give it during a workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller, and getting around Gotham does not take many complicated controls. Combat still calls for quick reactions and precise movements, so keeping a steady pace can be tough when the action heats up.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game does not need the latest hardware to run smoothly, and its recommended specs are modest compared with newer big releases. A mid range computer should be enough, though a steady frame rate helps when you are trying to focus on exercise.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The story and gameplay can hold your attention, but cutscenes and demanding combat make the workout fit uneven. Controller support and modest computer requirements help, but you may need to pause or slow down at times.

### Overall Score: 2.3 out of 3
