Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition is a strategy game about building, managing resources, and commanding units. That gives you plenty to think about, but it can be a lot to juggle while exercising.

## Gameplay

You need to keep an eye on your buildings, resources, and units, and the pace can be brisk. There are few long cut scenes or slow stretches, but quick decisions take focus, especially if you are pushing yourself on a treadmill or bike.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller, which helps when you are moving, but the game still asks for quick decisions and input. Building and gathering offer quieter moments, so you can catch your breath, but this is not a game you can play on autopilot.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game runs smoothly on a wide range of computers and does not need top tier hardware. That leaves you with less to worry about on the computer side.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The strategic gameplay keeps you busy, and quieter stretches give you a chance to ease up. Still, the focus it asks for and the quick decisions make it only a decent fit for exercise. Its reasonable computer requirements are a plus.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
