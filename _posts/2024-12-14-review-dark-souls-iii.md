DARK SOULS III has challenging combat and a dark fantasy setting. Its slower sections and cutscenes can break up a workout.

## Gameplay

Combat asks you to focus on enemy encounters and use tactics. The difficulty rewards patience, but long cutscenes and slow exploration interrupt the pace. Overall, the combat can keep you engaged, though it is not a smooth workout companion all the time.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller, and the controls rely on timing and strategy rather than constant rapid movements. Boss fights demand your attention, and frustrating deaths can make it harder to stay steady. It works for a moderate workout, but you will need to manage your focus.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is not overly demanding, but it does need a decent PC. The review lists an Intel Core i5 2500 or AMD FX 6300, 8GB RAM, and a GeForce GTX 750 or Radeon HD 7950 for moderate settings. Meeting those requirements should help avoid performance drops during play.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

DARK SOULS III offers a real challenge, but its slower sections and interruptions are not ideal for every workout. Controller support helps, though the game still asks for plenty of focus.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
