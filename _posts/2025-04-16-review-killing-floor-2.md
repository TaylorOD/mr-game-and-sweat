Killing Floor 2 has you fight waves of zombies and other monsters in places like eerie labs and decaying wastelands. Teamwork matters, since you need to plan while the enemies close in.

## Gameplay

The action is fast and keeps you engaged. There are no long cutscenes, and you spend your time moving, dodging and shooting. The weapons and perks give you different ways to play.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller, but the fast movement and quick reflexes can be a challenge while exercising. You may need to keep your feet steady on a treadmill and pay attention to your surroundings. The controls do not require complicated movements, but this still takes some care.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is not too demanding, though it recommends a decent graphics card and a modern multi core processor for smooth play. Mid tier computers can run it, but higher end systems may perform better.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Killing Floor 2 is a strong action game, but its pace can make it hard to manage during a workout. Controller support helps, though quick reactions and computer requirements keep it from being an easy fit for every setup.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
