This War of Mine follows civilians trying to survive in a city during wartime. Its focus on the daily struggle to stay alive gives it a thoughtful tone.

## Gameplay

You scavenge for food, medicine, and shelter while trying to keep your characters emotionally stable. During the day, you stay hidden, and at night, you can search for supplies. The slow stretches during gathering or character interactions can distract you from your workout, so the gameplay does not always hold your attention.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game works well on a treadmill or bike with a standard controller. It has no fast action or quick movements, so you can keep a steady pace while playing. You still need to pay attention to choices that affect your characters’ safety.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

This War of Mine can run on modest hardware. It requires Windows XP or later, 2GB of RAM, and a processor of at least 2.0 GHz. It is not graphically demanding, so it should run on a laptop without much trouble.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

This is a good fit for exercise if you want a slower game rather than frantic action. The gameplay can drag, but its controller support and modest computer requirements make it easy to pair with a workout.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
