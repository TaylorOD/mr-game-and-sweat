Arma 3 is a military simulation with open world missions and tactical combat. It has some appeal for a workout, but its uneven pace and demanding controls make it a mixed fit.

## Gameplay

The gameplay is engaging, and missions shift between intense combat and slow, methodical planning. There are no long cutscenes to pull you away, but those slower stretches can leave you waiting for things to happen.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Arma 3 supports a controller, though it is mainly designed for keyboard and mouse. Quick moves during encounters can distract you from keeping a steady pace on a treadmill or bike, especially if multitasking is new to you.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You need a mid range CPU and GPU, plus at least 6 GB of RAM. Mods can raise the performance demands, so a system that only meets the minimum requirements may have hitches.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Arma 3 offers engaging tactical gameplay, but its quick moves can throw off your workout rhythm. Controller support helps, though it does not make the controls an easy fit for exercise. You will also need a decent PC to avoid performance problems.

### Overall Score: 2.2 out of 3
