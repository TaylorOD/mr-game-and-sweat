You play as samurai Jin Sakai during the Mongol invasion of feudal Japan. The game mixes combat, exploration, and a story, but some of its slower scenes may not hold your attention while you exercise.

## Gameplay

You can fight with stealth, swords, and other tools, but some sections move slowly and lengthy cutscenes or dialogue can pull your focus away. Combat and exploring help keep things engaging, though the slow parts make the gameplay uneven for a workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller, but combat calls for quick reactions to deflect attacks and time parries. Exploration is easier to pair with moderate exercise, while fast fights may mean adjusting your pace. It can work for treadmill or bike sessions, but you will need to manage your effort.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

On PC, the game is visually demanding and calls for a solid GPU, such as an NVIDIA GTX 970 or AMD Radeon R9 390, plus at least 8 GB of RAM. Console players have fewer requirements to worry about, and the game runs on PS5. The listed PC needs are worth checking before you play.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The story and visuals have their appeal, but slower scenes can interrupt a workout. Exploration pairs better with exercise than combat, which asks for quick reactions. It can fit a workout if you adjust your pace, but it is not an easy game to play without paying attention.

### Overall Score: 2.3 out of 3
