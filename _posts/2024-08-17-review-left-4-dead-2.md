Left 4 Dead 2 is a cooperative shooter where you and your friends fight zombies. It gets going quickly, which suits a workout better than long breaks between action.

## Gameplay

The gameplay is fast and keeps you busy, with no long cut scenes or slow stretches. You work with your team, move through different environments, and deal with attacks as they come. It asks for your attention without getting in the way of your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controls let you move in any direction while shooting and dodging, and the game does not demand extreme reflexes. That makes it easier to hold a steady pace while you play. A wide range of controllers is supported, so setup for a treadmill or bike should be straightforward.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Left 4 Dead 2 does not need a powerful computer to run. The review lists a dual core processor, 2 GB of RAM, and an older graphics card as the minimum requirements.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Left 4 Dead 2 is a strong fit for treadmill and bike workouts. The action stays engaging, the controls suit a steady pace, and the computer requirements are modest.

### Overall Score: 3 out of 3
