Little Nightmares is a puzzle platformer where you guide Six through a grim world. Its eerie setting and story give you a reason to keep going.

## Gameplay

The puzzles, platforming and enemy encounters keep you engaged. The pace mixes tricky sections with chase sequences, and there are few long cutscenes to interrupt your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game works well on a treadmill or bike. It needs some timing and dexterity, but the controls are simple and the movements should not throw off your pace.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Little Nightmares has modest computer requirements and can run on lower end setups. Its detailed visuals do not need a high end graphics card.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Little Nightmares is a strong fit for workouts, with engaging puzzles and few pauses. It also works well on a treadmill or bike and does not ask much from your computer.
