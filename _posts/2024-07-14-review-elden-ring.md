ELDEN RING is an open world action RPG with exploration, combat, and crafting. A controller makes it usable on a treadmill or bike, but demanding fights can pull your attention away from exercise.

## Gameplay

Exploration gives you some quieter stretches, and the cutscenes are relatively short. Combat and boss fights need more focus, so the game is not always easy to pair with a workout. You can take breaks in the action, but the pacing is uneven.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support helps you play while exercising, and exploring lets you move at your own pace. Fights can call for precise timing and quick reactions, which may distract you or throw you off balance. It is a mixed fit for treadmill or bike sessions.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You need a moderately powerful PC to run the game smoothly, especially at its visual best. The review recommends a NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 or AMD equivalent and a processor that can handle the environments and enemy AI. Older hardware may need lower settings.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

ELDEN RING has stretches that can work with exercise, but its demanding fights make it hard to stay focused on both. Controller support helps, though the computer requirements and uneven pacing make it a mixed choice for workouts.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
