Path of Exile is a complex action RPG with plenty to do. Its fast combat and steady stream of enemies and loot make it a good fit for a workout.

## Gameplay

Combat stays busy, with enemies filling the screen and skills to switch between. There are few long cutscenes, so you can keep playing without many breaks in the action.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game supports controllers, and its interface does not demand much keyboard or mouse work. Dodging and positioning take some attention, but they are manageable while you focus on your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Path of Exile can push your computer with its graphics, though it runs on midrange systems. You do not need a top of the line setup, but computer requirements are still a consideration.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Path of Exile is a solid choice for playing while you exercise. The gameplay and controller support work well for a workout, though its computer requirements keep it from being an easy pick for every setup.

### Overall Score: 2.7 out of 3
