Plague Inc: Evolved is a strategy game where you create a pathogen and try to wipe out humanity. Its dark humor and planning give you something to focus on during a workout.

## Gameplay

You balance infectivity, lethality, and resistance as the world responds to the outbreak. The decisions are engaging, but some turns drag while you wait for the infection to spread. There are no lengthy cutscenes, so most of your attention goes to the strategy.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with an Xbox or PlayStation controller, and the controls do not call for quick reactions or precise movements. That makes it easy to keep playing while you run or ride. It is a good fit for exercise, though you still need to keep your focus on your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game runs smoothly on a wide range of computer hardware, including lower end systems. Its stylized visuals do not require the latest graphics cards.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Plague Inc: Evolved is a solid workout game with engaging strategy and simple controls. Waiting for the infection to spread can slow things down, so the pacing is not always ideal. Its modest computer requirements are another plus.

### Overall Score: 2.5 out of 3
