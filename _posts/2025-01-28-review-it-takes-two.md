It Takes Two is a co-op adventure with varied levels and puzzles. It keeps you involved, but some parts ask you to stop and think.

## Gameplay

The levels use different co-op mechanics, and there are few long cutscenes to interrupt play. Puzzles can take some discussion, though, so the pace is not always steady. Overall, the gameplay gives you plenty to do, with a few slower moments.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controls are simple, which helps when you are moving. But some sections call for quick reactions or actions at the same time, and those can pull your focus away from your workout. You will need to pay attention to your partner, too.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is not too demanding on your computer if it meets the minimum requirements. On a lower end machine, performance hiccups could get in the way.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

It Takes Two has enough variety to keep you playing, but its puzzles and quick reactions can make it a tricky workout game. It works better when you can give the game your attention, and performance may suffer on a weaker computer.

### Overall Score: 2.2 out of 3
