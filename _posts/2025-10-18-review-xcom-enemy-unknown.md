XCOM: Enemy Unknown puts you in charge of a military group defending Earth from an alien threat. You manage your squad, customize characters, and make decisions about resources.

## Gameplay

The turn based combat gives you time to plan each move. Missions keep things moving, but some moments need enough focus to make hard sprints difficult.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support and the lack of fast reactions make it manageable on a treadmill or bike. You can play at an easy pace, but you may want to pause if you are pushing your limits.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game has modest computer requirements and can run on a range of hardware. You do not need cutting edge technology to play it.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

XCOM: Enemy Unknown works for exercise when you can keep a steady pace and give the game some attention. Controller support and modest computer requirements help, but the strategy makes it a mixed fit for a workout.

### Overall Score: 2.3 out of 3
