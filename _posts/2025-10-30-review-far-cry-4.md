Far Cry 4 takes place in Kyrat, with an open world to explore and plenty of chances for trouble. It has a good mix of action and wandering, but that does not always fit neatly around a workout.

## Gameplay

You can sneak through enemy bases, drive into chases, explore new areas, take on side quests, and run into wildlife and NPCs. The variety is fun, but story scenes can last several minutes and slow things down. The action helps, though the gameplay is good rather than great for keeping your workout going.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Far Cry 4 works reasonably well with a treadmill or bike and supports a controller. The controls are responsive and do not demand constant twitch reactions. Some encounters still need quick reactions or careful moves, so you may have to focus more on the game than your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Far Cry 4 needs a fairly capable computer, especially if you want higher graphics settings. The review lists an Intel i5, a NVIDIA GeForce GTX 780 or AMD equivalent, and at least 4 GB of RAM. You may need to adjust settings to keep the game running smoothly.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Far Cry 4 offers a lot to do, but slower story scenes and demanding encounters can interrupt your workout rhythm. It is a reasonable fit for a treadmill or bike, not an especially easy one.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
