GUILTY GEAR -STRIVE- is a fighting game with fast, deep combat and little downtime. That makes it fun to play, though the quick action is harder to manage while exercising.

## Gameplay

The fights move fast, and you can link combos without losing the flow. The simpler mechanics make it easy to start, while character techniques give experienced players room to improve. There is little downtime, so you stay focused on the match.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

A controller gives you room to move, but quick inputs and reflexes can be tough when you are balancing on a treadmill or bike. It works, but takes more coordination than a relaxed workout game.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The graphics and animation call for a decent gaming computer, especially if you want higher frame rates. A mid range or high end system helps keep performance steady during fights. If you exercise for long sessions, cooling may also need attention.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The fast matches and low downtime make this a strong game, but not an easy workout pick. Quick inputs can interfere with balance, and the computer requirements add another hurdle. If you can manage those, the combat has plenty of depth to keep you playing.

### Overall Score: 2.3 out of 3
