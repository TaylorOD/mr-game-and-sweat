## Gameplay

POSTAL 2 mixes mundane errands with chaotic action and dark humor. You can go at your own pace, but some missions slow down and others may tempt you to change your jogging pace.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game supports controllers, though it works best with a keyboard and mouse. Some moments call for quick movement, so you may need to adjust your pace while running or riding.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

POSTAL 2 has forgiving computer requirements and can run on lower end machines. Older systems may get jittery when the chaos peaks, but most modern setups should run smoothly.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

POSTAL 2 has bizarre humor and chaotic gameplay, but its pace is not always a great fit for exercise. It is reasonably compatible with a treadmill or bike, and its low computer requirements make it easy to run on many machines.

### Overall Score: 2.3 out of 3
