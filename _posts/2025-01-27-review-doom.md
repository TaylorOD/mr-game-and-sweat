DOOM is fast and smooth, with plenty of demons to fight and no long cutscenes getting in the way. The action stays engaging, though it asks for your full attention.

## Gameplay

The controls are intuitive, and you can customize the button mapping before you start. Quick movement and dodging can be tricky while you exercise, so playing on a treadmill or bike takes some care.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You need a decent computer to get the most out of the graphics and fast action. An older capable machine should still run the game with reduced settings, but lower specs may make the experience less smooth.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

DOOM has strong, uninterrupted gameplay, but its quick fights can be hard to manage while exercising. The computer requirements are reasonable for a capable machine, though this is not an easy fit for every setup.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The action and smooth controls make DOOM fun to play, but they demand attention that can be hard to spare during a workout. Its computer requirements are manageable, though you may need to lower the settings on an older machine.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
