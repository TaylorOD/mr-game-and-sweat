Age of Empires II is a strategy game about gathering resources, building an empire, and fighting battles. That gives you plenty to manage, though it is not always a smooth match for exercise.

## Gameplay

Gathering resources, building, and combat keep you busy as you manage your economy and city layout. But long stretches of gathering or preparing for battle can drag. Those quieter moments give you room to adjust, but can also make it easy to lose focus. The lack of long cutscenes helps.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game does not ask for quick reactions, so you can keep a steady pace on a treadmill or bike without constantly reacting to the screen. Controller support makes it easier to fit into your setup. Still, complex plans can take two hands and more attention, so your workout pace may dip while you coordinate things.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Age of Empires II runs on basic hardware, so you do not need a powerful computer. That makes it a good pick if your system is older or you are trying to keep costs down. It should run smoothly without a top of the line gaming rig.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Age of Empires II is a decent choice for gaming while you exercise, thanks to its manageable pace and low computer requirements. The slow stretches and moments that need your full attention keep it from being an easy workout pick. Still, it gets the job done if you do not mind occasional dips in focus.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
