Bannerlord mixes tactical battles, character development, and kingdom building. Some parts are calmer, but the combat can take too much focus for a workout.

## Gameplay

The mix of battles and slower resource management keeps the gameplay interesting. Combat needs quick decisions, so you cannot always split your attention, but the quieter parts suit a light jog or casual ride.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller, which helps keep things simple while exercising. Travel times offer a break, but strategic positioning and fast combat can demand your full attention, so keeping a steady pace may be tough.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3  

## Computer Requirements

The game has fairly high computer requirements. Large battles can be demanding, and older systems may have performance issues that disrupt your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3  

## Conclusion and Score

Bannerlord has enough slower moments to work for some exercise, but its battles ask a lot of you. Its computer demands and uneven pace make it a mixed choice for gaming while you run or ride.

### Overall Score: 2.2 out of 3
