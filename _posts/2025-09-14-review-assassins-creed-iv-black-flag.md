Black Flag mixes pirate story, exploration, and action, but it is not a perfect fit for exercising. You get stretches of free roaming, along with cut scenes and missions that can interrupt your pace.

## Gameplay

Exploring, sailing, stealth, combat, hunting, and collecting give you plenty to do. But the long cut scenes slow things down, and the mix of activities does not always keep the pace up.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller works well, and free roaming lets you play at your own pace. Some missions need quick reactions, while combat can call for precision, which may pull your attention from your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Released in 2013, it asks for at least an Intel Core 2 Quad Q8400, 2 GB of RAM, and a graphics card like the NVIDIA GTX 460. Those requirements are moderate, though a stronger system should run it more smoothly. You do not need the latest hardware, but decent specs help avoid lag.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Black Flag has enough exploration and action to keep you interested, but cut scenes and precision sections can get in the way of a workout. It works reasonably well on a treadmill or bike, though it is not consistently easy to play while exercising.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
