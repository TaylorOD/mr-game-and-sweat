Farming Simulator 19 has you planting and harvesting crops, raising animals, and managing a farm. The slow pace suits exercise, but some parts can drag.

## Gameplay

There is plenty to manage, from crops and animals to farm equipment. Planting and harvesting can involve slow stretches while you wait for crops to grow, and the lack of many cut scenes means you stay in control. It is a decent fit for a workout, but the pace can get dull.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support and simple steering with occasional button presses make it easy to play while moving. It does not ask for quick reflexes, so you can keep a steady pace on a treadmill or bike. You can take it at your own speed without worrying about fast action.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game should run well on a mid range system, but higher graphics settings can be demanding. Minimum requirements include an Intel Core i3 processor, an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 graphics card, and 8GB of RAM. Older computers may lag or have longer load times, which can interrupt a workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Farming Simulator 19 is a decent workout game thanks to its relaxed pace and controller support. Slow stretches keep the gameplay from being a strong fit for everyone, and older computers may have performance issues. It works well on a treadmill or bike, but has some clear limits.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
