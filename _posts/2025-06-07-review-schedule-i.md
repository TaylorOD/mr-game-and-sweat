Schedule I mixes puzzles, strategic decisions, and fast combat. That variety gives you plenty to focus on while you work out.

## Gameplay

The mix of puzzles, combat, and changing objectives keeps gameplay moving. There are few slow stretches or cut scenes to break the flow, and the strategic parts give you something to think about. The fast combat takes attention, but the variety makes the gameplay strong.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support makes the game workable on a treadmill or bike, and the controls do not call for precise or extreme movements. Action can make you want to speed up, but low intensity sections give you room to settle back into your pace. That balance makes it a strong fit for cardio.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The graphics demands are moderate, and the game should run smoothly on most midrange systems. Still, it recommends at least 8GB of RAM and a decent graphics card, so the requirements are a bit more demanding than those of some casual games.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Schedule I is a good workout pick thanks to controller support, varied gameplay, and manageable controls. Its computer requirements are a little higher than those of some casual games, so it is a good fit for many setups, but not every one.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
