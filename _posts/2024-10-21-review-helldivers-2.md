HELLDIVERS 2 is a cooperative shooter about fighting alien enemies in chaotic missions. It has a funny edge, but the action and teamwork are the main draw.

## Gameplay

Working with friends and fighting off aliens is fun, and the missions have a mix of objectives. There is little time spent watching cutscenes, but cooldowns and respawns create stretches of downtime.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller play makes it manageable while you move, and you do not need constant quick, precise inputs. The fights can get hectic, though, so keeping track of enemies while staying balanced may take some effort.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

HELLDIVERS 2 uses Unreal Engine 5, but it is not the most demanding game around. A modern PC with decent specs should run it fine, while older hardware may cause problems.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

HELLDIVERS 2 is a fun shooter, though the downtime keeps the action from staying intense. Controller support helps on a treadmill or bike, but hectic fights can be distracting. Its computer requirements seem reasonable for a modern PC, with older hardware a possible snag.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
