A city sandbox where you pick your own goals could be a nice way to let a workout pass without constantly demanding a perfect reaction, but BROKE PROTOCOL sounds like it could also leave you standing around deciding what kind of person to be. I’d start with the jobs and see if making your own routine works for you.

## Gameplay

I like the idea of a game that lets you decide what counts as progress, because a job gives you something to aim at without making every minute feel like it’s part of a big story you have to follow. The city has jobs, apartments, garages, gangs, and NPCs, and that’s a lot of room to make your own plan. It’s also a lot of room to wander around thinking, “Well, I guess I should pick a plan.” That can be a relaxing sort of freedom, or a very slow afternoon.

The roleplaying focus is probably the part that will decide whether this works for you. If you enjoy making a character and giving them a reason to be in a city, the open ended structure sounds fun, and the custom content gives you another way to shape the experience. I don’t know how much direction the game gives you from moment to moment, so I’d go in expecting to make your own fun rather than waiting for the game to line it up neatly. Some people love that. I sometimes need a little more of a nudge.

The persistent, reactive world sounds like it could make your choices feel less temporary, which is a nice fit for a sandbox. But persistent worlds can also make a casual session feel like you’re stepping into something that has kept going without you, and that’s not always what I want when I’m trying to get some exercise in. The open ended setup is the appeal, and it’s also the part that could turn a quick session into me circling the city looking for a reason to move.

There’s action here, along with simulation and roleplaying, so I wouldn’t expect the whole game to be a gentle city stroll. I’d try jobs first, since they sound like a straightforward way to give yourself a goal, and then explore the other parts as they start to interest you. Gangs might suit you if you want more conflict in the roleplay, but I wouldn’t make that the first thing I try while also figuring out the game. I have enough trouble picking a lunch order when I’m hungry.

The moddable sandbox sounds generous, although custom content is only useful if you want to spend time finding and choosing it. I think BROKE PROTOCOL could keep you busy for a long time if building your own goals is the fun part for you. If you want a game that keeps handing you the next clear objective, this might feel like being left in a city with a map and no appointment.

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The self directed structure is a mixed fit for exercise. A job can give you a reason to keep moving through the game, but a city sandbox can also ask you to stop and make decisions, and I’d expect those pauses to be more noticeable on a treadmill than in a chair. You might spend a stretch moving around, then slow down while you decide what to do next. That’s manageable, but it won’t feel like a game that’s constantly keeping your attention on the action.

I can’t confirm full controller support from the information here, so I wouldn’t set off at a brisk pace before checking that the controls work the way you need them to. The action side makes me cautious too, because aiming or reacting quickly can get awkward when your hands are moving with your steps. I don’t know how demanding the combat is, so I’d keep the pace easy until you’ve got a feel for it. Your thumbs do not need to audition for anything.

On a bike, I’d start with a comfortable pace and give yourself room to look at the screen and make choices. If you’re playing with others, I’d avoid getting pulled into anything that needs fast reactions until you know how the controls behave while you’re moving. The multiplayer setting could add life to the city, but it also means I wouldn’t count on every moment being at your own pace.

I’d try a low pressure session first, focus on a job or another clear goal, and leave the more demanding action for when you’re not exercising. If you find yourself stopping every few minutes to work out where to go, that’s a sign this particular session may be better spent at the keyboard without a treadmill underneath you. It’s a little easier to enjoy a sandbox when you’re not negotiating with the belt.

## Computer Requirements

The listed minimums are very modest, and the store page says almost any graphics should do, so this shouldn’t need a recent gaming PC to get started. I’d check whether your system runs the game comfortably, then spend more time checking the control setup than worrying about the graphics card.

## Conclusion and Score

BROKE PROTOCOL sounds best for you if making your own goals and shaping a roleplaying sandbox is already appealing. I’d give it a try at an easy workout pace, but I’d skip it if you need a steady stream of clear tasks to keep you moving.
