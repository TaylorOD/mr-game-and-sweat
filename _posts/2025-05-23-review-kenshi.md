Kenshi drops you into a harsh world where you have to survive, explore, and build your own faction. Its open ended setup gives you plenty to manage, but it takes patience.

## Gameplay

You can manage your squad, explore, and plan without being rushed by cut scenes or long dialogue. The tactical play suits a steady workout, though long journeys and farming can leave you waiting around.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Kenshi supports controller input, and its basic movement does not call for fast reactions. Combat and strategy need more focus, which can make running harder, while the slower pace is a better fit for a bike.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is less demanding than many modern big budget games and can run on mid range systems. Its graphics are plain, but performance issues are not the main thing to worry about while exercising.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Kenshi can work for a treadmill or bike session if you like slower, strategic games. It has useful controller support, but waiting around and moments that need your attention keep it from being an easy workout game.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
