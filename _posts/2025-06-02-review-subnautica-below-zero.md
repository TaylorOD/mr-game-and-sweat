Subnautica: Below Zero follows Robin Ayou as she investigates what happened to her sister in an alien ocean. It has the underwater exploration of the first game, with new challenges and environments.

## Gameplay

Exploring, gathering resources, crafting, and staying alive give you plenty to do. The pace has quiet moments, but there are enough places to explore and things to manage to keep your attention during a workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller, and most of your inputs are for moving around and interacting with objects. It does not demand constant fast reactions, so you can keep a steady pace on a treadmill or bike.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game needs a decent computer for its graphics and large environments, so the requirements are not especially low. It can run on a system with an Intel i5 processor, 8GB of RAM, and a GTX 1060 or equivalent, but that is still a fairly capable setup.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

This is a good pick for playing while you exercise, thanks to its exploration, controller support, and steady pace. The computer requirements are less appealing, so it is not an easy fit for everyone.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
