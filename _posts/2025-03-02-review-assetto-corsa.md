Assetto Corsa is a racing game built around realistic driving. The cars feel different, and learning how to handle them gives you plenty to work on.

## Gameplay

The racing is the best part. The driving physics feel solid, the cars handle differently, and the tracks give you reasons to improve your consistency and speed. There are no long cut scenes or slow stretches to break up a race.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Steering through tight corners takes quick adjustments, which can be awkward when you are running or riding and holding a controller. The focus on balance and control helps, but that does not make it easy to play while exercising.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Assetto Corsa needs a decent gaming PC for a smooth experience. The review recommends an Intel i5 or equivalent, at least 8GB of RAM, and a graphics card like the NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660. On a more modest computer, you may need to lower the settings.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Assetto Corsa keeps the racing moving and gives you a good reason to focus on each race. It can work during a workout, but the steering takes attention, and you need a capable PC.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
