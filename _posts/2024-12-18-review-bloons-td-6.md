## Gameplay

Placing monkey towers to pop waves of bloons is satisfying, and upgrades give you a clear sense of progress. There are no long cut scenes or slow stretches, and the range of upgrades and strategies keeps the game interesting.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller, so you do not need to stop to make adjustments. The game does not demand quick reactions, and you can play at a steady pace. If you need a breather, wait for the next round to refresh your towers.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Bloons TD 6 is easy on system resources and can run smoothly on older computers. You do not need a high end gaming rig, and performance should not distract you from your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Overall, this is a good fit for gaming on a treadmill or stationary bike. It has controller support and low system requirements, with gameplay that suits a steady workout.

### Overall Score: 3 out of 3
