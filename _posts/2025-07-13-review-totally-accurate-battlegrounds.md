Totally Accurate Battlegrounds is a chaotic battle royale built around ragdoll physics. Fights can end in an unexpected kill or a faceplant, and that unpredictability is a big part of the fun.

## Gameplay

Matches are short and action packed, with little downtime between fights. You spend most of the match moving, dodging, and firing, though there can be a brief wait after you respawn. The silly physics keep the action entertaining.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play comfortably with a controller, which helps when you are exercising. But quick reactions and sudden turns can pull your attention away from running or riding, especially when you are moving fast. It takes some practice to balance the game with your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is lightweight and does not need a high end computer to run smoothly. That makes it a good option if your machine is not especially powerful.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Totally Accurate Battlegrounds is a fun, chaotic game with short matches and plenty to do. Controller support helps, but the quick reactions make it a mixed fit for a workout. Its modest computer requirements are a plus, though the game is not an easy match for focused exercise.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
