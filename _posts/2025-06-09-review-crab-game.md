Crab Game turns simple mini games into frantic rounds where you try to survive. It is fun and chaotic, and the rounds move along without long cut scenes or slow sections.

## Gameplay

The mini games call for quick reflexes and some strategy, and the short rounds make it easy to jump back in after elimination. The social play and unpredictable moments keep things lively. It is a strong party game, though the action can ask a lot of your attention.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a keyboard and mouse or a controller, and the directional controls are simple to pick up. Still, quick movement and reflexes can be hard to manage while you run or ride. Competitive play may take more focus than you want to give up during a workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Crab Game can run on modest PCs with basic specifications. Its lightweight graphics and straightforward mechanics do not call for a high end setup, so it should work well on lower performance devices.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Crab Game has fun mini games and simple controls, but its fast action can compete with your workout for attention. It is a good fit for active gamers who want a distraction, though it is not an easy game to focus on while exercising.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
