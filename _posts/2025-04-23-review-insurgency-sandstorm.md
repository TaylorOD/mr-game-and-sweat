Insurgency: Sandstorm is a tactical shooter built around realistic combat and team play. It can work during a workout, but it asks for more attention than a casual game.

## Gameplay

The firefights are tense, and every shot matters. There are no long cutscenes, but action can shift from calm to chaotic in seconds, with some downtime between objectives and fights. That pacing is mostly good, though the slow stretches can interrupt your workout rhythm.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support makes the game workable on a treadmill or bike. Some moves need precise control and quick reactions, so keeping your focus on both the game and your workout can be tough. Practice may help you find a rhythm, but this still takes more attention than a casual game.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game needs an Intel Core i5 4460 or equivalent, 8 GB of RAM, and a GTX 760 or similar graphics card. That may rule out older computers, though newer hardware should have an easier time with it.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The tense firefights can distract you from exercise, but slow moments, precise controls, and PC requirements hold the game back as a workout pick. It is worth considering if you want tactical action, but it takes more focus than many games you might play while exercising.

### Overall Score: 2.17 out of 3
