## Gameplay

You explore, solve puzzles, and fight as Lara Croft. The pacing works well, though quick action and slower moments both show up. Cutscenes are brief, but some parts still ask for more attention than you may want to give while exercising.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The standard controller is easy to use, and most of the game is fairly linear. Some fights and hazards need quick reactions, so you cannot always focus on your workout. There are calmer stretches, but the game is only a decent fit for a treadmill or bike.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game can be demanding, especially at the best graphics settings. A mid range processor and decent graphics card are recommended for smooth play. On a laptop or less powerful setup, you may need to lower the graphics settings.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Rise of the Tomb Raider has plenty to enjoy, but the quick reflex sections make it less than ideal for a treadmill or bike. It works better during calmer parts, and a less powerful computer may need lower graphics settings.

### Overall Score: 2.17 out of 3
