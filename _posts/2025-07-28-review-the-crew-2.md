The Crew 2 lets you switch between cars, boats, and planes with a button press. Races and challenges across city streets, rivers, and skies keep things varied, though some transitions between missions drag a little.

## Gameplay

There is plenty of action, but the pace is not consistent. You can play missions solo or with friends, and some moments call for quick reflexes, so it is not always easy to relax into a steady workout.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game works well with a treadmill or bike, and the controls mostly rely on smooth movements rather than twitch reactions. You still need to focus for sharp turns and vehicle changes, but the controls should not overwhelm you most of the time.

### Category Score: 2.8 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The Crew 2 needs a moderately powerful computer. The minimum requirements are an Intel Core i5 2500K or AMD FX 8120 processor, 8 GB of RAM, and an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 or AMD Radeon HD 7850 graphics card. Higher settings call for stronger hardware.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The variety of vehicles and locations makes The Crew 2 a solid workout game, and most of its controls are manageable while exercising. Slow sections and moments that need quick reactions hold it back a bit.

### Overall Score: 2.6 out of 3
