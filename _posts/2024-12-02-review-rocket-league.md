Rocket League is soccer with rocket powered cars, and the simple goal makes it easy to pick up. Matches are short and lively, though the action can demand more attention than your workout.

## Gameplay

Matches usually last about five minutes. You drive a car to hit a giant soccer ball into the other team’s goal, and there’s plenty to master, from aerial shots to dribbling and tackles. There’s little downtime, so you’ll likely be focused on the screen.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controls are responsive, and controller support makes setup easy. But the action changes quickly and calls for fast reactions, so it’s not a great choice for sprinting or intense cycling. At a moderate pace, it can work, though you may end up paying more attention to the game than your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Rocket League runs on a range of computers, including lower end machines. You don’t need the latest graphics card to play, so the computer requirements are modest.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The gameplay is a strong point, and short matches make it easy to fit in a game. The quick action can distract you from exercise, but it also means you need to keep your pace manageable.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3

Rocket League can be a fun way to pass the time during a moderate workout. It asks for too much focus to be a good match for hard running or cycling, but modest computer requirements make it easy to run on a range of systems.
