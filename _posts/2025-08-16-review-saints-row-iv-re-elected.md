Saints Row IV: Re-Elected puts you in control of the President during an alien invasion in a virtual world. Its over the top action makes for a lively workout game.

## Gameplay

There are plenty of missions, collectibles, and side quests, plus super speed and flight to get around the simulated city. The pace stays lively, and cutscenes and story sections are usually brief. Odd challenges, boss fights, and humor keep the action fun.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support and straightforward movement make it easy to play at a steady pace. You can use powers and shoot without fast button mashing, and the game also suits bursts of speed and jumps. Detailed combat can be harder on a steep treadmill incline, so keep your pace manageable.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

It is less demanding than many AAA games, but still needs a reasonable computer setup. Minimum requirements are a dual core processor, 2GB of RAM, and an NVIDIA GeForce 8800 series GPU or AMD equivalent. The recommended setup is a quad core processor and 4GB of RAM.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The busy missions, humor, and quick pace make it easy to keep moving during a workout. It works well on a treadmill or bike, though the computer requirements are worth checking first. It is a fun package, but not a perfect one.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3

If you want an action game that lets you keep moving without demanding fast inputs, this is a good workout pick. Keep your pace steady when combat gets busy.
