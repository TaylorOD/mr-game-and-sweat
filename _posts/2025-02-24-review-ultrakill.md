ULTRAKILL mixes retro FPS action with fast movement. It is a lot of fun, though its busy combat can make it tough to exercise at the same time.

## Gameplay

The action stays quick, with plenty of enemies, gunplay, and movement. Levels ask for quick reflexes and precise aiming, but there are few slow sections or long cutscenes to interrupt you. It is a strong gameplay loop, even if keeping up can pull your attention away from your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

ULTRAKILL supports a controller, so you do not have to use a keyboard and mouse while you run or bike. The fast fights still demand quick reactions and careful movement. A reasonable pace should help, but the game can be a lot to manage while exercising.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game can run on a range of systems, so you do not need a top of the line computer. A decent graphics card and processor can help you reach 60 FPS while shooting and dodging, though the requirements are not especially low.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

ULTRAKILL has excellent gameplay and keeps the action moving, but its demanding combat takes focus. That makes it a good game to play and a less comfortable fit for exercising at the same time.

### Overall Score: 2.3 out of 3
