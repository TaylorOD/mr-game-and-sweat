*Kingdom Come: Deliverance II* sticks with the series’ focus on realism and gives you a large world to explore. Some of its mechanics suit exercise, but slow sections can interrupt your rhythm.

## Gameplay

Quests and combat can hold your attention, but the pace drops during long cutscenes and dialogue. Those quiet stretches can make it easy to lose focus on your workout. The action helps, but it does not keep the game moving all the time.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support and less frantic gameplay make it manageable while running or biking. You can explore at your own pace, though dialogue heavy sections can leave you waiting around. It is a reasonable fit, with some clear slow spots.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game asks for a fairly capable computer if you want the best visuals. The recommended specs call for a mid to high range GPU and a decent amount of RAM. On a lower end system, frame dips may distract you during a workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The game has enough engaging moments to work for exercise, but its slow stretches and hardware demands keep it from being an ideal pick. It is a decent option if your computer can handle it and you do not mind breaks in the action.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
