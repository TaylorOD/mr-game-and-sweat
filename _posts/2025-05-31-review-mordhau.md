## Gameplay

MORDHAU’s skill based melee combat and team play make its battles fun to get stuck into. There are no lengthy cutscenes, and the fights keep changing, so you stay focused on dodging and fighting.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support is a plus, but quick reflexes and managing multiple foes take attention. It can work with moderate activity, though turning and swinging during a fight can make exercise harder to manage.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You need a moderately capable PC to run MORDHAU smoothly. Performance depends on settings and player count, and lower end machines may struggle during chaotic moments, with the game running from 30 to 60 FPS.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The combat is strong, but its quick fights make MORDHAU a tricky workout game. Controller support helps, while the need for a capable PC and careful movement keep it from being an easy fit.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
