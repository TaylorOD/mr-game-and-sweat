GTFO is a cooperative shooter set in an underground facility full of creatures. Its focus on teamwork and strategy gives it a clear hook, though the dark, tense setting can take a lot of your attention.

## Gameplay

The rounds mix tense stretches with bursts of action, and you need to communicate and plan with your team. The pacing keeps you engaged, and there are no long cut scenes or extended downtimes. That makes for strong gameplay, but gripping the handles while focusing on a plan is not ideal for a workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

GTFO favors strategy over quick reactions, and it supports controller play. That helps if you want to play while moving, but keeping track of the horror and your surroundings takes focus. It can work for steady cardio, though it is not an easy game to play while exercising.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

GTFO asks for a fairly capable computer, with an NVIDIA GTX 780 or equivalent GPU and at least 8GB of RAM. Your setup also needs to deliver smooth performance while you exercise. Those requirements may be a hurdle if your computer is less powerful.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

GTFO has engaging gameplay, but its focus on teamwork and horror makes it a mixed fit for a treadmill or bike. The computer requirements are also a consideration. It is worth a look for the gameplay, but it is not an easy choice for a workout.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
