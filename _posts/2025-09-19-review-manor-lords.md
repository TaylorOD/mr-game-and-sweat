Manor Lords mixes city building, strategy, and medieval simulation. You manage a growing settlement, with plenty of planning to keep you occupied.

## Gameplay

There is a good mix of building, resource management, and tactical battles, but the game asks for your attention. The early setup can be slow, though there are no cutscenes to interrupt your workout. You can take breaks, but you still need to keep track of what is happening.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support helps, and the controls do not depend much on quick reflexes. Some actions need precise movements, but they can usually be done at a relaxed pace. You can pause to manage tasks or regroup without stopping your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Manor Lords is fairly demanding, so older computers may have trouble. A solid graphics card and decent processor are recommended for the best experience. Mid range systems should meet the minimum requirements, but the review suggests you may want more power for the graphics and detail.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Manor Lords can work well alongside a workout, thanks to controller support and its slower pace. The strategy keeps you thinking, though it can also pull your focus away from exercising. It is a good option for cardio, but not a game you can play on autopilot.

### Overall Score: 2.5 out of 3
