The Walking Dead tells its story through tough choices and emotional scenes. Its slower pace can work during a workout, but some moments ask you to focus on the screen.

## Gameplay

The game mixes exploration with conversations where your choices can affect the story. Calm stretches are broken up by tense zombie scenes, but long cutscenes can distract you from your workout. Some moments keep you watching for too long.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The simple point and click controls and limited need for quick reflexes suit exercise with a controller. Quick time events call for sudden focus, so it fits better with moderate or low intensity workouts.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is not demanding on a PC, and its modest graphics can run on older systems. A decent CPU and GPU help it run at its best.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The story and difficult choices give you a reason to keep playing, but the pacing can interrupt your workout. The relaxed controls and low computer requirements help, though the cutscenes can make it harder to keep your rhythm.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
