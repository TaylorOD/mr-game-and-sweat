Just Cause 3 follows Rico Rodriguez as he tries to free his homeland from a dictator. It offers plenty of chaos, but quieter stretches make it a mixed fit for a workout.

## Gameplay

There is plenty of action and freedom to cause trouble, but some missions run long and objectives can get repetitive. The quieter moments and dialogue can make it harder to stay focused while you exercise.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller, and the controls are simple enough for a treadmill or bike. Still, navigating the large environment and pulling off stunts can take your attention away from your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Just Cause 3 needs a decent computer to run smoothly. A mid range processor and graphics card are listed as the minimum, while better performance calls for stronger hardware and enough memory and storage.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The action makes Just Cause 3 a decent workout game, but quieter stretches and stunts can interrupt your rhythm. It also needs a capable computer to run smoothly.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
