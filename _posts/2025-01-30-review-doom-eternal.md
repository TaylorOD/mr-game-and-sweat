DOOM Eternal is a fast paced sequel to 2016’s DOOM. Its constant action can pair with a workout, though the combat may take more focus than you want while exercising.

## Gameplay

The action rarely lets up, and you spend your time moving, dodging, and fighting demons. There are few slow sections or long cutscenes, so the gameplay keeps a strong pace.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller while running or riding, but the constant movement and combat still need your attention. Finding a steady rhythm may help, though the pace makes this a mixed fit for exercise.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

DOOM Eternal needs a decent computer to run smoothly, ideally with a higher mid range GPU and CPU. An older machine may need compromises in quality or performance, which could interrupt a workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The fast gameplay is a strong point, but it can be demanding to manage while exercising. The computer requirements also call for a capable setup, so this is a good game with some real limits as a workout companion.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
