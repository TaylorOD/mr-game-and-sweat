Blade and Sorcery is a VR game about medieval combat with a range of weapons. Its fast action is fun, but the movement makes it a tough match for exercising on a treadmill or bike.

## Gameplay

The physics based combat gives you room to try different weapons, spells, and tactics. Enemies react in realistic ways, and there are few slow sections or long cutscenes to interrupt the action. The combat is chaotic at times, but the gameplay stays engaging.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game is built for VR movement, and traditional controls are less effective during fast fights. Quick lateral movements and rapid reactions can be unsafe while you are on a treadmill or bike. It may work for someone who can manage both, but that is a big ask.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You need a VR capable computer, with at least a GTX 970 or better, a strong CPU, and enough RAM for the physics calculations and environments. That is a sizable setup requirement. Higher end computers run the game smoothly, but entry level setups may have to wait longer.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The combat is strong, but its fast pace and movement make it hard to focus on exercise at the same time. It is better suited to players who can handle the action without needing to keep their attention on a treadmill or bike.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3

Blade and Sorcery has engaging combat, but it is a poor fit for most treadmill and bike workouts. The VR setup also means you need a capable computer.
