FIFA 23 is a soccer game you can pair with a treadmill or bike. Its controls are manageable while exercising, though pauses in play can break your rhythm.

## Gameplay

The controls feel responsive, and matches keep moving most of the time. Cut scenes and goal celebrations slow things down and can throw off your exercise rhythm. They are brief, but the interruptions still get in the way.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

FIFA 23 is easy to control at a steady pace on a treadmill or bike, without quick reflexes or complicated moves. Career Mode and offline matches let you set the pace. Online play can feel more stressful and demand more focus.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The PC requirements are demanding, though manageable on a fairly recent computer. Recommended specs are an Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5, 8GB RAM, and an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 or AMD Radeon HD 7850. A weaker computer may have slower frame rates, which can get in the way.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

FIFA 23 is a good workout option thanks to its easy to manage controls and pace. Cut scenes interrupt the flow, and you need a recent enough computer for smooth play, so it is good but not a perfect fit.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
