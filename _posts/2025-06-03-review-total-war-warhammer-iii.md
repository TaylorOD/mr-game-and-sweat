It mixes turn based campaign planning with real time battles in the Warhammer fantasy setting. That can work for a workout, though parts of the game leave you mostly planning.

## Gameplay

The campaign gives you time to plan before battles, which is handy while exercising. Sieges and unit deployments can drag, especially on a treadmill, and quieter planning stretches may make it hard to keep your intensity up. Cutscenes are short enough to skim through.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game works well with a controller, and most of its strategy and unit placement does not demand quick reflexes. You may need to move around the battlefield quickly at times, though. The slower parts are easier to manage while riding or running, but the faster moments can throw off your rhythm.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

It asks for a mid to high end PC, with at least an Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5, 8GB of RAM, and an NVIDIA GTX 970 or AMD RX 570. Those requirements are reasonable, but they are not light. Keep your device in a ventilated area during a workout to avoid overheating.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The mix of planning and battles makes it a decent workout game, though slow stretches can take some energy out of your session. It works best if you are comfortable handling menus and the occasional burst of faster battlefield movement.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
