Mortal Kombat 11 has brutal fights and a lot of story. The long cutscenes can be a pain when you are trying to keep your workout going.

## Gameplay

The combat feels responsive, and there are plenty of fighting styles and fatalities. Story mode slows things down with long cutscenes, but you can get back into a fight quickly once they end.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The quick reflexes and combo inputs make Mortal Kombat 11 hard to play while running or cycling. Simpler moves give you some room to manage, but you will need to lower your intensity.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You need a moderately capable computer to run the game smoothly at higher settings. The listed minimums are an Intel Core i5, 8GB of RAM, and a dedicated graphics card like the NVIDIA GeForce GTX 950. Lag could cost you a match.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The fighting is fun, but the game is a poor fit for treadmill and bike workouts. It asks for quick reflexes and steady control, so keeping your heart rate up can be tough.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3 

Short matches may fit your workout better than long stretches of play. You will still need to adjust your expectations if you want to keep a steady rhythm.
