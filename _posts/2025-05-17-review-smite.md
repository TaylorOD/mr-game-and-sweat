SMITE is a multiplayer battle arena game inspired by mythology. Its matches can make a workout less dull, but the game asks for plenty of focus.

## Gameplay

Matches rely on teamwork, strategy, and quick decisions, with different gods to choose from. The action can distract you from a repetitive workout, but respawns and long character selection screens leave stretches of downtime.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

A controller makes SMITE fairly manageable on a treadmill or bike. Quick movement during team fights can be tricky while keeping a steady pace, and focusing on the game may throw off your rhythm.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

SMITE is moderately demanding on your computer. It runs on a range of systems, but a decent graphics card and a fairly modern CPU help, while struggling hardware can distract you.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

SMITE is a reasonable workout game, thanks to controller support and engaging matches. Still, the action can disrupt your pace, so it takes some attention away from your exercise.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
