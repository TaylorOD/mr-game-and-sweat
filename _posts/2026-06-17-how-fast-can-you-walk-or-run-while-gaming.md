There isnt a magic number, but most people should start around 1 to 2 mph. Your real limit is the fastest speed where you can keep your balance without thinking about it and still play. For a lot of people that is a walk, not a run.

## A rough guide to speed

These are starting points and not rules. Your height, fitness and treadmill all change the answer.

| Speed | What to expect |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 mph | Slow walk. A good place to start for any game. |
| 2 to 3 mph | Easy walk. Good for most slow games once you are used to it. |
| 3 to 4 mph | Brisk walk. Your hands start to bounce. Stick to easy games. |
| 5 mph and up | Running. Your head and hands bounce a lot, and it takes real focus. This is only for experienced runners playing very simple games, if at all. |

## How to find your speed

1. **Start slow.** Use your safety clip, and start with a game you already know.
2. **Play for 5 minutes.** Check whether your feet are staying in the middle of the belt.
3. **Bump it up by a small amount.** Try 0.2 or 0.3 mph, then play for another few minutes.
4. **Back off when something slips.** If you drift, stumble or keep missing inputs, go back to the last speed that felt easy.

Your best speed will change from game to game. A card game like [Balatro](/review-balatro/) can handle a faster pace than something with quick dodges.

## Signs you are going too fast

* You drift toward the back or side of the belt.
* You keep looking down at your feet.
* You have to grab the rail.
* Your controller hands are tense.
* You lose track of the game or your footing.

Any of these means slow down. Stumbling even once is a good reason to back off.

## Why running is so hard

When you run, your whole body moves up and down with each step. Your eyes have to fight that to read the screen, and your thumbs have to hit buttons while they bounce. You also have less time to react if your foot lands wrong.

So if you want to raise your heart rate, you have better options. An exercise bike lets you work harder while your upper body stays still. You could also walk on an incline if your treadmill has one and your body is fine with it. We compare them in [treadmill desk vs exercise bike for gaming](/treadmill-desk-vs-exercise-bike-for-gaming-which-is-better/).

## Make it easier

Change speed when the game is paused, never mid fight. It is also worth finding games that dont punish you for messy inputs. See [what makes a game good to play while exercising](/what-makes-a-game-good-to-play-while-exercising/).

#### Warning: Playing video games while using exercise equipment can lead to injury. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise routine, and always use the safety clip.
