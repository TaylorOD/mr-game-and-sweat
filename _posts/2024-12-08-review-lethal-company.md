Lethal Company is a cooperative horror game about collecting items while enemies hunt you. The brisk pace and short breaks between scares make it easy to stay engaged while you exercise.

## Gameplay

There is plenty happening, and the game avoids long slow sections and cutscenes. That keeps the action moving and earns it a strong score for gameplay.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller, which helps when you are on a treadmill or bike. Some moments call for quick reactions, though, and those can interrupt your rhythm.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You do not need a top of the line computer, but the recommended setup is an Intel Core i5 processor and 8GB of RAM. That is a reasonable requirement, though it still keeps the game from being an easy fit for every computer.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Lethal Company has engaging cooperative horror and gameplay that keeps moving. Quick reactions can disrupt your workout, and the computer requirements are not especially low, so it is a decent option rather than a perfect workout game.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
