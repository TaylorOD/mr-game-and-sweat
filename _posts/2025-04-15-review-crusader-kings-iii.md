Crusader Kings III is a strategy game about feudal politics, dynasties, and medieval wars. You guide a dynasty from humble count to powerful king across generations, dealing with intrigue and conflict. There is a lot to keep track of, so it can be hard to play while exercising.

## Gameplay

You spend your time plotting, arranging marriages, managing vassals, and fighting wars. The menus and text keep you involved, and there are few long cutscenes to interrupt your workout. Still, the complex strategy can take enough focus that you may need to ease off your pace.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support helps, and the game does not ask for fast reflexes or split second timing. You can make decisions while cycling or running, but stretches of concentrated thought are a poor match for vigorous exercise. It works better when you can keep your pace manageable.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game needs a decent computer to run smoothly, with a minimum 2.4 GHz dual core processor, 8 GB RAM, and an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 460 GPU. A better graphics card and stronger CPU can help if you want to keep a fixed resolution. An average or weaker system may need an upgrade for smooth play while you exercise.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Crusader Kings III can keep you busy while you exercise, but managing a dynasty takes focus. Controller support and few cutscenes help, though the strategy may distract from your workout. It is a workable match for exercise, but not an effortless one.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
