Monster Hunter Wilds takes you into a world of monsters to hunt. It has plenty to do, but it is not always a smooth fit for a workout.

## Gameplay

The hunts give you different weapons and monsters to learn, and combat keeps you paying attention. Exploring and fighting happen in real time, but tracking monster movements and reacting can take focus while you run or ride. The slower moments give you a chance to catch your breath, though they do not make the game effortless to play during exercise.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a standard controller, and the action does not demand ultra fast reflexes. Resource gathering, crafting, and menus can slow things down, which may feel clunky during a hard workout. Most of the time, the hunts give you enough to do without asking for perfect timing.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game asks for a decent computer. 8GB of RAM and a decent GPU are enough to get started, while 16GB of RAM is recommended for ultra settings. You will need a midrange or high end setup to get the best graphics and environments.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The action and exploration make for a solid game to pair with exercise, but slower moments get in the way. The computer requirements are manageable, though you still need a decent setup.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3

If you like hunting monsters, there is plenty here to keep you involved. Just expect some menu time and moments that need more focus than your workout may allow.
