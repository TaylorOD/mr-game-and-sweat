**Gorilla Tag** turns tag into a VR game where gorillas chase and tag each other. The simple controls make it easy to get into, though playing while using exercise equipment is another matter.

## Gameplay

You use your arms to move through the environments, with no complex button combos or menus to deal with. Chasing and evading other players keeps the action moving, and rounds start fresh without long interruptions. The gameplay works well on its own.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game encourages quick movement, turns, and leaps, which do not sync well with a treadmill or stationary bike. You may need to adjust your pace, and the quick movements can be an issue for motion sickness. It can fit into a workout routine, but it takes some care.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You need a VR headset and a decent graphics card to play. The review recommends a mid range VR capable PC with an NVIDIA GTX 970 or equivalent, and says higher frame rates help. That makes the requirements manageable, but not especially light.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Gorilla Tag is fun and keeps the gameplay moving, but its VR controls and fast movement make it awkward to pair with a treadmill or bike. It is a solid game, though not an easy fit for exercising while you play.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
