PlanetSide 2 is a large scale online shooter built around team battles. You can play infantry, drive vehicles, or fly aircraft while fighting over objectives.

## Gameplay

Battles put hundreds of players across sprawling maps, and there are few slow sections or long cutscenes. The action stays busy, with team objectives keeping you involved.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game supports controllers, and its controls are responsive. There is some maneuvering, but the review finds it does not demand the kind of precision or quick reflexes that get in the way of working out.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

PlanetSide 2 has fairly demanding graphics, so an older PC may struggle. You do not need the latest hardware, but a mid range graphics card and enough RAM are recommended for better performance and stability.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

PlanetSide 2 is a good workout game thanks to its steady action and controller support. Its PC requirements are a drawback, so it is a solid choice if your setup can handle it.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
