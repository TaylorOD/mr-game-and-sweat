Darkest Dungeon mixes strategy with gothic horror and can be punishing. Its turn based combat makes it a strong fit if you like planning your moves.

## Gameplay

Combat rewards careful planning, and the pace gives you time to think between moves. There are no long cutscenes, so you can stay focused on the game.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support is useful, and the turn based pace avoids frantic button presses or split second reactions. Still, it was designed for keyboard and mouse, and keeping track of your strategy while exercising takes focus. You can pause between battles.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Darkest Dungeon has modest system requirements and should run on most computers. You do not need a high end gaming computer to play it.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The strategy and turn based combat make Darkest Dungeon a good game, and its modest computer requirements are a plus. Controller support helps, but playing while exercising takes more attention than the slow pace might suggest. It is a solid option, though not an ideal workout game.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
