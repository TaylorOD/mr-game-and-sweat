PowerWash Simulator is a good fit for a steady bike ride or an easy treadmill pace, since most of your time goes to slowly sweeping water over dirty surfaces. The careful aiming can get tricky if your movement is bouncy, though.

## Gameplay

Cleaning each surface is simple and satisfying, but the pace is slow and the work can get repetitive. You stay busy aiming the washer and chasing patches of grime, with no need for fast reactions.  
### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Full controller support is a big plus, and the game has no twitch action to throw off your rhythm. You do need to aim carefully to find every speck of dirt, so a steady bike is a better match than a bouncy run.  
### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum specs call for a GeForce GTX 760 or AMD R7 260X, plus 4 GB of RAM. That is an older dedicated graphics card, so you do not need a high end gaming PC, but integrated graphics are not enough by the listed requirements.  
### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

PowerWash Simulator makes a relaxed workout companion if you enjoy slow, repetitive tasks and have a steady setup. If you want a game that keeps your heart rate up with quick action, this hose is not going to do the job.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
