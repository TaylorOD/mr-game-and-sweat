The Drifter’s point and click conspiracy story gives you plenty to think about, which is great at a desk and rough on a moving treadmill. Full controller support helps, but following the story and solving things still takes focus.

## Gameplay

The murdered drifter’s return to life makes for a strong pulp thriller hook, and the store page promises a fast paced ride. But point and click play asks you to slow down, inspect what’s happening, and think through your next move. That focus is a poor match for a workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game has full controller support, so you can leave the mouse and keyboard alone. Still, carefully following the plot and making deliberate point and click inputs is harder when you’re moving. Save it for when you can give it your full attention.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum specs ask for Windows 10, 4 GB of RAM, and a graphics card that supports DirectX 10. Those are modest requirements, and an older or basic dedicated graphics card should be enough.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The Drifter sounds like a good thriller with a clear hook and brisk pacing. Its story and point and click play make it a bad workout companion, even with full controller support. Play it at your desk.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3
