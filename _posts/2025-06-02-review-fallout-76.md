Fallout 76 takes the series online and sends you into West Virginia after a nuclear disaster. You explore, take quests, craft, and deal with mutated monsters and PvP encounters.

## Gameplay

The open world lets you explore at your own pace, which is handy on a treadmill or bike. But loading screens and drawn out quests can stall the action and break your exercise rhythm.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller, and combat is usually not too frantic for a steady pace. Some encounters need quick reflexes, though, so you may have to shift your focus, while quieter stretches give you room to ease off.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Fallout 76 asks for a fairly robust computer. The minimum specs include an Intel Core i5 and a GTX 550 or equivalent, while a smooth experience is said to call for at least an i7 and a GTX 780.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Fallout 76 has enough open world exploring to work with a workout, but its slow patches and sudden fights can get in the way. The computer requirements are also a real drawback, so overall it is a hard game to recommend for exercise.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3
