The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt is an open world RPG where Geralt searches for his missing adopted daughter. There’s a lot to explore, but the story and long stretches of dialogue can slow things down.

## Gameplay

Combat mixes sword fighting with magic, so you need quick reflexes, though you also get chances to plan your moves. Side quests can keep you playing, but long cutscenes and dialogue sometimes make the pace drag.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller, which helps when you’re moving on a treadmill or bike. Some action needs precise timing, and quick time events can disrupt your rhythm, while exploration and dialogue give you calmer moments to settle into a pace.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game needs a fairly modern computer to run well. The review lists 6GB of RAM and a GTX 660 as minimum specs, with more power recommended, while lower spec machines may need reduced graphics settings.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The Witcher 3 has a good story and plenty to do, but its slow sections can get tedious and distract from your workout. It works with a controller, though the combat and computer requirements make it a mixed fit for exercise.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
