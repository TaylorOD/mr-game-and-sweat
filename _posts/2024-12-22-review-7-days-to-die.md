7 Days to Die mixes survival, crafting, and open world exploration in a post apocalyptic setting full of zombies. You scavenge for supplies, build shelters, and fight off the undead.

## Gameplay

There is plenty to do, from gathering supplies to building and fighting, and the game keeps you making decisions. There are no long cutscenes or slow stretches to break things up, which helps when you are exercising.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller, and the game does not demand constant twitch reactions. But close combat and strategic maneuvering call for a steady hand, so it can be hard to manage while you are running or riding.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements are moderate, but they may still rule out some setups. The minimum specs listed are an Intel Core i5 2400 or AMD FX 8320, 8 GB of RAM, and a dedicated NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 or AMD Radeon HD 7870 graphics card.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The gameplay gives you a lot to do, and the controller works for exercise sessions. Still, the close combat and computer requirements make it a mixed fit for playing while you work out.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3

7 Days to Die has strong gameplay, but it takes focus to play while exercising. Its computer requirements and the attention needed for combat keep it from being an easy recommendation for treadmill or bike sessions.
