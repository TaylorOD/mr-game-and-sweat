Resident Evil Village mixes action, exploration, and horror. Its mix of quieter stretches and tense moments gives you some variety, though it is not always easy to keep your workout in focus.

## Gameplay

There is plenty of combat and exploration, and the game usually balances them with slower stretches. Some sections lean on longer cinematics, and the suspense can distract you from your workout. Overall, the gameplay is strong, but not always easy to follow while exercising.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game uses a gamepad and does not demand constant rapid movements, which helps while you exercise. Still, combat and boss fights sometimes call for quick reactions. That makes it manageable on a bike, but a less comfortable fit on a treadmill.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements are moderately high. You need a capable GPU and a decent CPU for high frame rates, so less powerful machines may struggle to run it smoothly.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Resident Evil Village has good gameplay, but its cinematic sections and quick fights can interrupt your workout. It works better as a bike game than a treadmill game, and its computer requirements are a bit demanding.

### Overall Score: 2.17 out of 3
