Tom Clancy's Rainbow Six Siege is a tactical shooter built around teamwork, strategy, and precision. That makes it a tricky fit for a workout, since the game asks for plenty of attention.

## Gameplay

Matches are round based, and each operator has unique abilities. Most rounds keep you busy with objectives and quick decisions, which can make it hard to keep your workout pace steady.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller means you don’t have to use a keyboard and mouse while moving. But when a firefight starts, focusing on the game can take priority over your speed on the treadmill or bike.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game runs well on mid range PCs without demanding top tier hardware. If your PC is older, you may need to lower the graphics settings for smooth play.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The tactical gameplay gives you something to focus on while you run or ride, but its fast pace can get in the way of your workout. You may need to adjust your pace to manage both.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3

Rainbow Six Siege can work for a workout, especially with a controller, but it takes some juggling. The quick reactions and attention it requires make it a mixed choice for exercising.
