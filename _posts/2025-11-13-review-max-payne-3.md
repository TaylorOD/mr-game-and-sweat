Max Payne 3 has sharp bursts of shooting that can keep your workout moving, but long story scenes will leave you coasting. Precise aiming also gets tough when your stride or hands are wobbly.

## Gameplay

You play as former New York cop Max Payne, now in São Paulo protecting a wealthy family while trying to escape his past. The shooting is engaging, with bullet time adding some style, but frequent cut scenes slow the pace. It can distract you from a workout, then suddenly demand your full attention.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game supports controllers, which is much easier than wrestling with a mouse while exercising. But you need accurate aim and quick reactions in fights, so loose inputs can get you in trouble. Better for a slow bike ride than a brisk run.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The listed graphics requirements start at old dedicated cards like the NVIDIA 8600 GT or Radeon HD 3400, though the game also lists much stronger options. It needs 35 GB of storage, and integrated graphics may not work. You probably do not need a new gaming PC, but check your hardware before buying.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Max Payne 3 is a strong action game, but its mix of long scenes and precision shooting makes it a fussy workout companion. The complete edition includes the original game and all previously released downloadable content. Choose it for a steady bike session, not a run where you want to zone out.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3
