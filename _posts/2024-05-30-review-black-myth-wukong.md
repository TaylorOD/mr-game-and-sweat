Black Myth: Wukong is an action adventure game based on the Chinese tale "Journey to the West." You play as Sun Wukong, the Monkey King, in a story driven by combat and mythology. The action is fun, but it may not be easy to pair with exercise.

## Gameplay

Combat is fast and rewards good timing. There are few slow sections or long cut scenes, so the game keeps moving, though intense fights can pull your focus away from exercising. That makes it fun to play, but not always easy to fit around a workout.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller, which helps when playing on a treadmill or bike. But fights call for quick movements, dodges, and precise timing, so keeping a steady pace can be difficult. If you like multitasking while you exercise, this may take more focus than you want.

### Category Score: 1.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game asks a fair amount from your computer. You need a capable graphics card and processor to get the full visual quality and smooth gameplay. That can be a hurdle if your computer is less powerful.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The story, combat, and visuals make Black Myth: Wukong appealing if you like this kind of game. Its quick combat is harder to manage during a workout, and its computer requirements may rule out some setups. It is good, but not an easy exercise game.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3

Black Myth: Wukong is worth a look if you want an engaging game while you exercise. Expect to give the combat your attention, which can make it harder to keep your workout steady.
