FINAL FANTASY XIV Online is an MMORPG built around quests, combat, exploration, and story. It has plenty to do, but some parts move slowly.

## Gameplay

Combat happens often enough to keep things moving, but lengthy cutscenes and story dialogue can bring the pace down. Side quests and dungeons offer more to do, though the slower sections may make it harder to keep a steady workout going.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game supports a controller, and it does not usually demand fast reflexes. Some encounters do require quick responses, so staying stable on your treadmill or bike matters.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You will need a decent computer to run the game well. The minimum requirements include an Intel Core i5 processor and an equivalent graphics card, while smoother play in busy raids calls for a more powerful GPU. Laptops should have good cooling for long play sessions.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The story and gameplay give you plenty to do, and controller support helps when you exercise. Slow sections and moments that need quick responses keep it from being an easy fit for every workout. The computer requirements are also worth checking before you start.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
