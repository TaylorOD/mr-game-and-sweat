Space Marine 2 is a sequel with plenty of Warhammer lore and a focus on combat. Its controls also work well if you want to play while exercising.

## Gameplay

Combat mixes melee and ranged weapons, with different enemies to keep fights from feeling too repetitive. The action flows well, though occasional story sections slow things down.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller input makes the game a good fit for a treadmill or bike. The combat does not demand rapid movements or much finesse, so you can focus on your workout while using the thumbsticks.

### Category Score: 2.8 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game needs fairly robust hardware, and lower spec PCs may have performance dips during intense fights. The review recommends at least an Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 with a GTX 1060 or equivalent.

### Category Score: 2.2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Space Marine 2 is a solid workout game, with manageable controls and plenty of action. Pacing slowdowns and hardware demands keep it from being an easy recommendation for everyone.

### Overall Score: 2.5 out of 3
