NARAKA: BLADEPOINT mixes melee combat with ranged attacks in a battle royale. Its characters have different abilities, but the action can be tough to keep up with while you exercise.

## Gameplay

Combat moves quickly between ranged and melee attacks, and fights take some skill. You may get a break while searching for loot or planning your next move, though the action usually returns soon.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller, and the movement does not demand constant quick reactions. Still, some fights may make it hard to keep your posture steady, even with the spacious arenas.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The system requirements are moderate, with a minimum of an Intel Core i5 3450 or AMD FX 6300, 8GB of RAM, and a NVIDIA GeForce GTX 660 or AMD Radeon HD 7850. A newer computer should be fine, but a more powerful system is recommended for smooth performance.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

NARAKA: BLADEPOINT has plenty of action, but slower moments and demanding fights can interrupt your workout rhythm. It works well enough on a treadmill or bike, though it is not the easiest game to play while exercising.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
