Battlerite offers a brisk and energetic gameplay experience that perfectly aligns with exercising on a treadmill or bike. Its fast-paced action keeps your heart rate up, but the lack of controller support may make for a tricky challenge while trying to juggle movement and gameplay.

# Gameplay

Battlerite is an action-packed team arena brawler that successfully fuses top-down shooting with fighting game dynamics. The matches are engaging, requiring swift reactions and constant engagement, which can be exhilarating. However, there are no lengthy cut scenes or significant downtime to detract from the intensity of the action. This continuous flow makes it an excellent choice for staying motivated while you exercise, as there is usually something happening on-screen to keep your mind occupied and your energy high.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

# Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Unfortunately, Battlerite does not support controllers, which can make it a challenge to play while exercising. The game demands precise movements and fast reactions, which could become cumbersome when combined with the instability of a treadmill or bike. Rapid responses and aiming can be difficult to manage while trying to maintain balance, making your workout less enjoyable and possibly frustrating.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

# Computer Requirements

On the technical side, Battlerite runs smoothly on older hardware, given its minimum requirement of just an Intel HD 3000 graphics and a dual-core processor. This means that it’s quite accessible, allowing players with older PCs to join the action without needing a fully equipped gaming rig. It does require a stable broadband internet connection, ensuring you can participate in quick-paced multiplayer matches without interruptions.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

# Conclusion and Score

In summary, Battlerite shines in terms of engaging gameplay and accessibility, making it a thrilling choice for gamers looking to exercise simultaneously. However, the lack of controller support and the potential for twitch reactions may hinder your experience on a treadmill or bike, making it less ideal for those seeking a seamless workout-gaming blend.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
