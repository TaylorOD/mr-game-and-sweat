BattleBlock Theater is a platformer with comedic narration and quirky characters. You move through short levels, collect gems, and rescue imprisoned friends.

## Gameplay

The levels have obstacles to work around, but they are short and there are no lengthy cutscenes or slow sections. That makes it easy to play in quick sessions without losing track of your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controls are simple, and the pace is manageable for a treadmill or bike. Some moments call for quick navigation, but the game is forgiving enough to let you keep a steady rhythm.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

BattleBlock Theater does not ask much of your PC and can run on a wide range of systems. Its simple art style helps keep system demands low.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

BattleBlock Theater is a solid choice for gaming during cardio. Its short levels, manageable action, and low computer requirements make it easy to fit into a workout.
