Space Engineers is about building and maintaining ships, space stations, and planetary bases. There is plenty to do, but the appeal depends on enjoying that kind of hands on engineering.

## Gameplay

Building, mining, and designing machinery can keep you busy for a long time. Complex problems can also leave you standing still, though you can put activities down whenever you like.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller works well enough for playing while you exercise. Moving blocks or fighting needs more focus, and you can pause whenever you need a break.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game has moderate computer requirements. A mid range gaming computer can run it, but higher graphics settings and larger builds need more power.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Space Engineers has a lot to offer if you like building, but it can distract you from your workout. Pausing helps, and the computer requirements are manageable for many systems.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
