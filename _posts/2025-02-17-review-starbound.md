Starbound is an exploration and sandbox game with procedurally generated worlds, alien societies, and treasures to find. Its pixel art world gives you room to explore and build without much fuss.

## Gameplay

You can mine resources, craft items, and build anything from a small home to a large fortress. Many tasks use simple clicks and navigation, but managing inventory and equipment takes attention, and combat can call for quick reactions. Building or waiting for crops to grow gives you a calmer stretch.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The flexible controls and controller options make it possible to play on a treadmill or bike, but frequent direction changes and combat can be awkward while you are moving. You may need to slow down or pause during more demanding moments.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Starbound is not particularly demanding, and its pixel art helps it run smoothly on modest hardware. You should not need an expensive setup to play it.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

There is plenty to do, and the calmer parts can pair well with exercise. But quick reactions and direction changes make it less comfortable to play while you are running or riding.

### Overall Score: 2.3 out of 3

Starbound can keep you busy during a workout, but it asks for more attention than an easygoing exercise game. Its strengths make it a decent option, though the occasional need to slow down or pause holds it back.
