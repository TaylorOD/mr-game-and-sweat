Ready or Not is a tactical shooter built around strategy, teamwork, and careful play. That deliberate pace suits a workout better than constant frantic movement.

## Gameplay

Missions keep you busy, with cooperative play that asks you to communicate and think tactically. There are no long cutscenes or extended slow stretches, and the mission variety keeps things fresh. You stay involved without needing to rush every second.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller setup and measured pace make Ready or Not a good fit for a treadmill or bike. Some moments need quick decisions and finger dexterity, but you can adjust your pace, pause at a strategic point, or take a break during a slower stretch.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Ready or Not needs a decent computer to run smoothly, especially if you want higher frame rates and enhanced effects. The minimum requirements are more accessible, but a stronger CPU and GPU may be needed for better performance or to run other demanding applications at the same time.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Ready or Not pairs tactical missions with a pace that works well for exercise. The computer requirements are a real consideration, though, so it is not an easy recommendation for every setup.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
