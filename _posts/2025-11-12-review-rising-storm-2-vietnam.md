Rising Storm 2: Vietnam gives you plenty to focus on during a workout, but sudden firefights and careful aiming make it a rough pick for running.

## Gameplay

The 64 player battles mix tense stretches of moving and waiting with sudden, frantic fights. There are more than 20 maps, several factions, helicopters, traps, and plenty of weapons, so matches have variety, but the action can get punishing fast.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Competitive multiplayer means you can’t pause when you need to adjust your pace or catch your breath. Precise aiming and quick reactions are a bad match for loose movement on a treadmill or bike, even if you can keep your hands on the controls.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum calls for a 64 bit Windows 10 system, 4 GB of memory, and an older dedicated graphics card such as a GeForce GTX 460. That should be manageable for a basic gaming PC, but integrated graphics are below the stated requirement, and you need 60 GB of storage.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Rising Storm 2: Vietnam has tense, varied battles, but its demanding aim and unpausable multiplayer make it a poor workout companion. Save it for when you can give the match your full attention.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3
