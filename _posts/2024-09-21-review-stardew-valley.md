Stardew Valley is a farming game with pixel art and a soothing soundtrack. Its relaxed pace makes it a good fit for playing while you exercise.

## Gameplay

Farming, crafting, fishing, and community building give you plenty to do. You can set your own pace, and the lack of long cutscenes means fewer interruptions while you run or ride.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller support and slow pace make Stardew Valley easy to play on a treadmill or bike. You do not need fast reflexes, so it is less likely to throw off your rhythm.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Stardew Valley is lightweight and runs on a wide range of systems. Its modest graphics demands help it run smoothly without taxing your computer.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Stardew Valley keeps you busy without demanding complex mechanics or pulling you away for long cutscenes. Its easy pace, controller support, and low computer requirements make it a strong choice for exercise gaming.

### Overall Score: 3 out of 3
