INSIDE is a puzzle game set in a dystopian world, with a story told through its visuals and sound. It has mystery and danger, though its atmosphere does not make it an ideal fit for everyone.

## Gameplay

The gameplay mixes platforming, puzzles, and stealth, with a fairly steady pace. There are a few brief pauses and short cut scenes, but they should not interrupt your workout for long.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The simple controls and occasional quick reactions make INSIDE a good fit for a treadmill or bike. You can keep a steady pace, though some sections call for more cautious movement.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

INSIDE has fairly modest computer requirements, and older computers can run it smoothly. The requirements are reasonable, but they are not a major plus for the game.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

INSIDE is a solid choice for exercising while you play, thanks to its simple controls and steady pacing. Its atmosphere and story work well, though its computer requirements are only reasonable, not a standout advantage.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
