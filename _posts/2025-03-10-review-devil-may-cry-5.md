Devil May Cry 5 is a fast action game with combat that gives you plenty to do. It can work during a workout, though the busy fights ask for your attention.

## Gameplay

You can switch between characters and use a range of combat moves, which keeps fights varied. That action takes focus, and the cutscenes add some downtime, but they are generally short. You can get back to playing quickly.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller or keyboard, and the combat leans more on timing and precision than quick reactions. That suits the steady movement of running or cycling. Boss fights may need more concentration, but the overall pace works well for a workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Devil May Cry 5 can be demanding on your computer. A mid range gaming PC with a decent graphics card should handle it, but a weaker system may struggle and interrupt your focus.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The combat and steady pace make this a strong choice for treadmill or bike workouts. Some fights take concentration, and the computer requirements are not especially light, but those issues do not outweigh the game’s workout fit.

### Overall Score: 2.5 out of 3
