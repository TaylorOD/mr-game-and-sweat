Hotline Miami is a top down shooter built around short, brutal missions. Its quick action suits short bursts of play, though the fast pace can be hard to manage while exercising.

## Gameplay

You wear animal masks and take on missions that call for precision, strategy, and trial and error. The action moves in quick bursts, and instant restarts keep you from waiting through long pauses after a mistake. Gameplay is sharp and brisk, with little downtime.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The frantic action can pull your attention away from keeping a steady rhythm on a treadmill or bike. You can play with a controller, but quick reactions may make it harder to focus on your workout. You may need to pause to catch your breath.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Hotline Miami has modest graphics, RAM, and CPU demands. It runs smoothly on anything from a modest laptop to a gaming rig, so your computer should not get in the way of playing.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The quick missions and instant restarts make Hotline Miami a strong fit for short bursts of play. Its frantic action can still disrupt your rhythm while exercising, so it is a mixed choice for cardio.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
