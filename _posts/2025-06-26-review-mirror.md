## Gameplay

Mirror is a puzzle platformer where you use your character’s abilities to get through levels with reflective surfaces. The mechanics are easy to follow, though some puzzles can make you stop and think.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The simple controls work well with a controller or keyboard, and the game does not ask for quick reactions. A few puzzles can pull your focus from your workout, so it is a good fit most of the time, not all the time.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Mirror runs smoothly on mid-range computers and does not demand top-tier hardware. You can focus on playing without worrying much about lag or stuttering.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Mirror is a solid pick for treadmill or bike play, with simple controls and low computer demands. The occasional tricky puzzle can interrupt your rhythm, but it does not spoil the experience.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
