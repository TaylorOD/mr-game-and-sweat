Human Fall Flat is a physics puzzle platformer where you guide a wobbly human named Bob. Its clumsy controls give the puzzles a lot of funny moments.

## Gameplay

The puzzles are fun, and Bob’s clumsy movement makes solving them entertaining. You can explore and try different ways to tackle each puzzle, without long cutscenes or slow sections getting in the way.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

This works well on a treadmill or bike because you can take your time with the puzzles. The controls are simple to use with a controller, and the game does not demand quick reflexes.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements are fairly modest, and the game can run smoothly on lower spec computers. That is helpful for a workout setup, though it is not the game’s main strength.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Human Fall Flat is a good choice for exercise thanks to its puzzles and relaxed pace. The computer requirements are a little less of a selling point, but the game is still an enjoyable fit for a workout.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
