Blender is about exploring and making things, so it is not an obvious workout game. Still, it has some useful qualities if you want a creative distraction while exercising.

## Gameplay

There is plenty to do, and the game avoids long cut scenes or stretches where nothing happens. Some creation tasks need focused attention, but lighter moments give you a break. It works fairly well as a workout distraction, though it can ask more of your attention than you might want while moving.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a regular controller, and the game does not rely on quick reflexes. Some tasks call for precise movements and focus, so keeping a steady rhythm on a treadmill or bike may take effort. It is workable, but not an effortless fit for exercise.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Blender has moderate computer requirements, and mid range systems can run it without major performance issues. A stable frame rate helps keep the gameplay flowing, so a decent graphics card can make a difference.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Blender can be a decent choice if you want a creative distraction during cardio, and its computer requirements are reasonable. The need to focus on some tasks can interrupt your workout rhythm, so it is a good fit in parts, not all the time.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
