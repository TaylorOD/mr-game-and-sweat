Frostpunk mixes city building, survival, and strategy in a world where freezing weather has pushed humanity to the brink. You build a city around a generator and manage resources, morale, and public policies.

## Gameplay

You juggle coal, wood, food, and steel while making difficult decisions as a leader. The slower planning stretches can distract you from your workout, even without long cutscenes.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

A controller makes Frostpunk easy to play on a treadmill or bike. It does not demand quick reactions or precise timing, so you can keep exercising while managing your city.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Frostpunk needs a mid range gaming PC, with a modest GPU and a decent amount of RAM. That makes its computer requirements manageable, but not especially low.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Frostpunk is a good fit for exercise because its controller controls and slower pace do not demand frantic inputs. The planning can still compete with your attention, and the gameplay is not strong enough to make the whole package a clear recommendation.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
