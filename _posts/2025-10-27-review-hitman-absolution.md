Hitman: Absolution follows Agent 47, a clone turned assassin, through a stealth focused story. Its missions give you room to plan, but the slower pace does not always fit a workout.

## Gameplay

You can scout your surroundings and choose how to deal with guards and targets. Watching NPC behavior can be interesting, though slower sections and cutscenes sometimes interrupt your rhythm. It keeps you involved, but the pacing is uneven.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The methodical pace and controller support make it a reasonable fit for a treadmill or bike. Some missions still ask for precision and timing, which can be harder while exercising. The lack of quick time events helps.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game needs at least a dual core CPU and a decent graphics card, so older computers may struggle. An average gaming PC can run it smoothly at medium settings. That makes the requirements manageable, but not especially light.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The gameplay has enough planning to hold your attention, but pacing issues get in the way. It is a decent workout choice, though precision can be awkward during cardio, and its computer requirements are moderate.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
