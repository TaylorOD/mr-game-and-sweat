Supermarket Simulator has you stock shelves, manage inventory, and deal with customers. It makes for a decent workout distraction, even if the store management is not always exciting.

## Gameplay

You navigate aisles, manage inventory, and serve customers. The tasks involve some quick thinking, but there are slower stretches too, so the gameplay is engaging without being consistently absorbing.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game works well with a treadmill or bike. Its controller options make it manageable while your legs are moving, and the moderate pace does not demand quick reactions.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements are reasonable, but not especially low. It runs smoothly on mid range PCs and needs minimal graphical power, so most users should be able to run it without top of the line hardware.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Supermarket Simulator is a solid choice for gaming during cardio. The controls suit a workout, and the gameplay is good enough to keep you occupied, though it has a few dull patches. The computer requirements are reasonable, not a major selling point.

### Overall Score: 2.5 out of 3
