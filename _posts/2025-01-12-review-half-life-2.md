Half-Life 2 is a strong shooter with puzzles, combat, and story moments. It can work for a workout, though the first person action sometimes asks for quick reactions.

## Gameplay

You play Gordon Freeman through puzzles, combat, and quieter exploration. The action has a good mix, and long cutscenes rarely interrupt your pace. The gameplay keeps you interested without constant battles.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller setup is useful, and the controls are straightforward. Still, quick reactions can be awkward while you run or ride. You can adjust your speed and intensity, but this is not an effortless workout game.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Even though it came out in 2004, Half-Life 2 runs on modest hardware. Most current systems should meet or exceed its requirements, so it is easy to run.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Half-Life 2 is a great game, but only an okay workout pick. Its mix of action and quieter sections helps, while the shooter controls can pull your attention away from exercise.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
