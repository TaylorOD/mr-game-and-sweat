You can play as Jason Voorhees or as a counselor trying to survive at Camp Crystal Lake. The multiplayer setup has a clear horror hook, but the game is only a decent fit for working out.

## Gameplay

As a counselor, you scavenge for resources, repair vehicles and communicate with teammates. Those slower tasks can interrupt your cardio, and generator repairs may take longer than expected. Evading Jason adds some urgency, but the pace is uneven.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller while on a treadmill or bike. Escaping Jason can call for quick reflexes and sudden sprints, so keeping your workout steady may be tricky. The movements are not too complex, but the escape sequences need focus.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements are fairly forgiving and do not call for high end specs. The review says minimum requirements run the game fairly smoothly on mid tier systems. Poor performance can still hurt the experience.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The game is decent for playing while you exercise, but the slow sections can throw off your rhythm. The controller helps, though the action is not consistent enough to make this a perfect workout fit.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3

It offers some active moments, along with stretches of waiting and strategizing. That makes it a moderate option for adding cardio to a gaming session.
