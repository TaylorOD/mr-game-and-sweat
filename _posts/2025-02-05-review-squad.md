Squad is a tactical shooter built around teamwork and communication. That gives you a reason to pay attention, but it also means the game can be awkward to manage during a workout.

## Gameplay

You capture objectives in large battles, and coordinating with your team keeps you thinking. There is also downtime while you organize squads and respond to orders, so the pace can drag. The action is engaging when a plan works, but it is not constant.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller or keyboard, but keeping your hands on the controls can be tough while running or riding. The moderate pace helps, though quick decisions and voice chat still take attention. It is a mixed fit for exercise, especially if you prefer fast action.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Squad has moderately high computer requirements, and a midrange graphics card is ideal. Less capable machines may lag, especially during intense sequences. Most modern setups should run it smoothly.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Squad can give you something to focus on during a moderate workout, but its controls and slower stretches hold it back. It is a decent fit if you can manage the hand use, though it is clunky for harder workouts. Its computer requirements are another drawback to keep in mind.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
