Resident Evil 7 Biohazard is a survival horror game with exploration, puzzles, and tense moments. Some parts work for a workout, but others ask for more attention than you may want to give while moving.

## Gameplay

The first person view makes you feel vulnerable, and the mix of exploration, puzzles, and survival keeps things engaging. Slow sections and long cut scenes can interrupt your workout, so the pacing is a mixed bag.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller while you exercise, but some puzzles and tense moments need precise movements and quick reflexes. That can be awkward on a treadmill or bike, especially when a jump scare catches you off guard.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is not overly demanding, and its recommended requirements should be manageable for many mid tier gaming setups. Higher graphics settings or extra apps for streaming and workout stats can put more strain on a lower end machine.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Resident Evil 7 Biohazard has tense moments that can help keep you engaged, but its slower stretches and need for focus may distract from your workout. It can work as a cardio game, though it is not always an easy one to play while moving.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
