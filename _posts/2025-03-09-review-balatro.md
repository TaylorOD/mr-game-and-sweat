Balatro mixes roguelike play with card strategy and a bit of charm. It has a clear fit for exercise, thanks to its steady pace.

## Gameplay

The card play gives you choices without making things feel chaotic. Rounds are not too long, and there is little downtime, though you will need to stop and think about your cards and moves.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a keyboard or controller, and the game does not demand quick reactions. Its turn based pace lets you take a moment to catch your breath without losing track of the game.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game runs smoothly on mid range computers, according to the review. Its card art and effects do not get in the way of stable frame rates, though computer requirements are not a major selling point.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Balatro is a good fit for treadmill and bike sessions, with strategic play that leaves room to keep a steady pace. The computer requirements are less of a sure strength, but the overall package works well for exercise.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
