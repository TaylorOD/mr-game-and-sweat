Fallout: New Vegas is a strong post-apocalyptic RPG with a detailed world, plenty of quests, and a story worth following. It also works well as a game to play while you work out.

## Gameplay

There is plenty to do, from side quests and character interactions to joining factions and exploring the wasteland. Combat mixes real time action with turn based choices, so you can make decisions without rushing. The pacing is steady, with few long cut scenes, and you can take your time with exploration or dialogue while you run or ride.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game suits a treadmill or bike because it is a single player game played with a controller, and its menus are easy to navigate. Combat is not rapid fire, so you can keep a moderate pace without having to react constantly. The controls are responsive without demanding twitchy movements.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Fallout: New Vegas does not need a powerful computer, and it can run on a wide range of hardware. Older systems should handle it without much lag. Lower settings still give you a playable experience without using too many system resources.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Fallout: New Vegas is a good workout game thanks to its steady pace, flexible gameplay, and manageable combat. Its low computer requirements are another plus. You can keep moving without the game demanding all of your attention.

### Overall Score: 3 out of 3
