Deep Rock Galactic is a cooperative shooter about space dwarves mining resources and fighting alien hordes. Its mix of teamwork, exploration, and humor gives the missions a clear sense of purpose.

## Gameplay

Missions take you through procedurally generated caves filled with enemies and resources, and there is plenty to do. You can mine, explore, fight, and coordinate with your squad without stopping for lengthy cut scenes.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Full controller support and deliberate movement make the game manageable on a treadmill or bike, and it does not demand constant twitch reactions. Still, ambushes can call for quick responses, so it takes some attention away from your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game has modest computer requirements, with a minimum Intel Core i5-2500K or equivalent and a GTX 660. Most systems available today should be able to handle it without much trouble.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The missions keep you busy, and the computer requirements are reasonable. But quick reactions during ambushes make it harder to stay focused on running or biking, so it is only a partial fit for exercise.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
