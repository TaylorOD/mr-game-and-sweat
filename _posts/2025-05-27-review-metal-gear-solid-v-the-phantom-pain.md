METAL GEAR SOLID V: THE PHANTOM PAIN gives you plenty of ways to handle stealth and action missions. Its mix of open areas and tactical choices works well, though the story can take time away from your workout.

## Gameplay

You can approach missions in different ways, with tools and gadgets to help. Cutscenes can run long and get in the way of your workout, but many missions are short enough to play in bursts.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game works well with a controller, and its controls do not call for constant quick movements. Intense stealth moments can make you slow down or stay still, which is awkward if you want to keep a steady pace.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum computer requirements are not especially high, but a decent graphics card and processor are recommended for a smooth experience. You do not need the latest hardware, though pushing the visuals will take more resources.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

This is a good fit for treadmill or bike sessions, thanks to its controller friendly play and missions that can be done in short bursts. Long cutscenes and stealth moments can interrupt your pace, and the computer requirements are worth checking before you start.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
