Batman: Arkham Knight has a good story and a mix of combat, chases, and stealth. It can work during a workout, but the slower sections may break your rhythm.

## Gameplay

The missions mix combat, chases, stealth, and story scenes. Frequent cutscenes interrupt the pace, so you may need to pause your workout or lose focus on the game.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller makes it fairly easy to play on a bike, and the game does not demand constant fast reactions. Combat still takes some timing and coordination, so it is not effortless to play while exercising.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game can put a fair bit of demand on your computer. The recommended setup includes a solid graphics card and a decent amount of RAM, so check that your system can run it smoothly.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The story and gameplay are good, but cutscenes and slower sections can get in the way of a workout. Controller play helps, though you still need a computer that can handle the game without lag.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3

Batman fans who like exercising may enjoy playing this, but the story can make you want to pause. It is a workable choice for a workout, with some interruptions.
