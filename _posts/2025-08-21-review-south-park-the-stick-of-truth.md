The Stick of Truth has simple RPG gameplay and plenty of South Park humor. It is a decent fit for a workout, but long dialogue scenes can slow things down.

## Gameplay

You control the New Kid around South Park, taking on side quests and watching cutscenes. Battles are turn based and easy to follow, but there is a lot of dialogue, and some story sections drag.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller while sitting or standing still, and turn based fights give you time to catch your breath. It suits slower workouts, though boss fights can involve button mashing and the story still takes some attention.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game has light computer requirements and runs smoothly on a variety of systems. You do not need top of the line hardware to play without interruptions.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The humor and straightforward RPG gameplay make this a decent workout game, especially at an easy pace. Long story scenes and some button mashing hold it back, so it is not a great fit for every workout.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
