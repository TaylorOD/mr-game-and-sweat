ARK: Survival Evolved mixes exploration, crafting, and survival in a world full of dinosaurs and other threats. Balancing those activities can be tricky while you exercise.

## Gameplay

You can tame and ride creatures, build bases, and gather resources, with plenty of systems to keep you playing. But gathering and waiting for health to return can drag, while defending your base or taming a dinosaur takes more attention. It keeps you busy overall, though the pace is uneven for a workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support makes ARK manageable on a treadmill or stationary bike. Battles and taming can call for quick reflexes, which may be hard to manage when your heart rate is up. The pacing works in calmer stretches, but expect some fumbles.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

ARK needs a mid to high end PC to run smoothly at decent settings. On a basic system, performance issues can disrupt both the game and your workout.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

ARK has plenty to do, and controller play makes it possible to use while exercising. Still, intense moments can pull your attention away, and the demanding computer requirements are a real hurdle. It is an entertaining survival game, but not a great fit for every workout.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3
