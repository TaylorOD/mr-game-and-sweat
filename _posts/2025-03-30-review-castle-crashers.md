Castle Crashers sends you into combat in a colorful medieval world with plenty of jokes. Its straightforward controls and fast pace make it a good fit for a workout.

## Gameplay

You guide your knight through groups of enemies and boss fights with simple controls. The combat keeps moving, and there are few slow sections or long cutscenes to pull you away from the game. It’s fun to play while you exercise.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controls work well with a controller, and the game does not ask for intense reflexes. You can keep a steady pace on the treadmill or bike without juggling complex moves or split second reactions.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Castle Crashers runs smoothly on basic computer systems. Its modest graphics needs make it an easy choice for a treadmill or bike setup with less powerful hardware.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Castle Crashers is a strong pick for playing while you work out. The action keeps you engaged, the controls suit treadmill and bike use, and it runs on basic systems. The humor adds a bit of fun without getting in the way.

### Overall Score: 3 out of 3
