Company of Heroes 2 is a World War II strategy game built around squad combat and battlefield commands. Its focus on planning gives you something to think about, though it can be a lot to manage while exercising.

## Gameplay

You command squads, capture territory, use cover, and keep track of resources. Missions shift between bursts of action and planning, but cutscenes and waits for resources can slow things down. That uneven pace makes it harder to keep a steady workout going.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game is made mainly for mouse and keyboard, with some gamepad support. Its quick decisions and precise commands can be tricky while you run or pedal. If you can keep your focus on the game, it can work, but the controls may disrupt your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game has moderate computer requirements. A quad core processor, 4GB of RAM, and a dedicated graphics card are recommended for smooth play with decent graphics. Older hardware may lag or drop frames.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Company of Heroes 2 has engaging strategy, but slower stretches can make it tough to keep a consistent workout pace. Its computer demands are moderate, and the controls may not feel comfortable while you exercise.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
