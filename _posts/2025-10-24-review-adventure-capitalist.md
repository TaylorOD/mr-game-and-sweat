AdVenture Capitalist is an idle clicker about building a business empire. Its simple play works with exercise, but the game is more about making money than fitness.

## Gameplay

You start with a lemon stand, then buy businesses and upgrades to build your empire. The idle mechanics let you step away without losing progress, but the clicking and upgrades can get repetitive. After a while, you may zone out instead of staying engaged.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with standard keyboard controls, and the game does not require quick reflexes or precise movements. You do need to check your progress now and then, but that is manageable at a steady pace on a treadmill or bike.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

AdVenture Capitalist runs on a wide range of hardware, including basic laptops. Its simple graphics and interface do not require a powerful gaming computer.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The idle gameplay and low computer demands make AdVenture Capitalist easy to fit into a workout. Repetition can make longer sessions less engaging, so it is a solid workout game but not a great one.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
