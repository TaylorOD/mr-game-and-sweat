Portal 2 mixes a story with physics puzzles in the Aperture Science facility. You guide Chell through challenges that ask you to think and try different solutions.

## Gameplay

The puzzles keep your mind busy, and the game moves along without long interruptions. There is some thinking to do, but not much that should pull you out of your workout. Wheatley and the other characters add some good humor along the way.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller, so you do not need to reach for a keyboard and mouse. The puzzles do not rely on quick reflexes, which makes it easy to keep a steady pace on a treadmill or bike.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Portal 2 runs on modest computer hardware, so you do not need a powerful machine. That means less chance of your computer getting in the way while you play and exercise.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Portal 2 is a great fit for playing while you work out. Its thoughtful puzzles, controller support and modest computer requirements make it easy to keep moving while you play.

### Overall Score: 3 out of 3
