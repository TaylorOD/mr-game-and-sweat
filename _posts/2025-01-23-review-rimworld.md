RimWorld is a colony simulation where you guide a group of stranded colonists trying to build a home on the edge of space. You manage their needs while dealing with unexpected problems.

## Gameplay

There is always something to handle, from pirate raids to food shortages and psychic drones. The game runs in real time, but you can change the speed or pause, which makes it easy to fit around your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a mouse and keyboard or a controller. The strategic pace does not demand many quick reactions, and the click based interface is manageable if you are a little out of breath.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

RimWorld has modest computer requirements and can run on mid range hardware. Its low graphical demands also make it a practical choice if you want to multitask while exercising.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

RimWorld keeps you busy with a steady stream of colony problems without asking for fast reflexes. Its adjustable pace and low computer requirements make it a solid choice for treadmill or bike sessions.

### Overall Score: 3 out of 3
