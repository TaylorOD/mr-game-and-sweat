Trove is a blocky MMORPG from Trion Worlds, with a voxel world where you can build, fight, and tackle dungeons. There is plenty to do, but it is not a perfect fit for every computer.

## Gameplay

You can adventure, mine, build, and take on quests and events. Combat is simple, and the steady mix of exploring and fighting keeps downtime low.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Trove supports various controllers, which makes it easy to play while you exercise. You can take your time exploring, and the controls do not demand quick reflexes.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Trove is not especially demanding, and it runs smoothly on mid range PCs. Check the specifications for your setup before you play.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Trove is a solid game to pair with a workout, with simple combat and little downtime. Its computer requirements are reasonable, though you should still check that your setup can run it well.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
