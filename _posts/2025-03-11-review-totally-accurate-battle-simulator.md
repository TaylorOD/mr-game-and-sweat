Totally Accurate Battle Simulator lets you pit strange units against each other in goofy battles. Its exaggerated physics and unit designs make the chaos fun to watch.

## Gameplay

You place units on a battlefield and send them against AI opponents. Battles are quick, often lasting only a couple of minutes, and there are no long cutscenes or slow stretches. The silly physics keep things entertaining without taking all your attention.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

TABS works well with a controller and does not ask for quick reactions. Most of what you do is position units, so you can play at a relaxed pace while exercising.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is not very demanding, but larger battles can need a reasonably modern computer to run smoothly. The minimum specs include an Intel Core i3, 4 GB of RAM, and a decent graphics card. Older hardware may lag during busy scenes, though the game runs well on most mid tier systems.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

TABS is a fun fit for the treadmill or bike, with simple controls and no intense reflex tests. Older computers may struggle with larger battles, so it is not an easy choice for everyone.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
