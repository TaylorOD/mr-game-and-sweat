Barotrauma puts you in a submarine crew exploring Europa’s harsh ocean. Managing resources, handling threats, and completing missions make for a good cooperative game, though the emergencies can be a lot to juggle while exercising.

## Gameplay

The gameplay earns its keep with teamwork, planning, and plenty to do. You stay busy with action, exploration, and problem solving, so there is little downtime to lose focus.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support helps, and you can usually keep a steady pace. Still, navigating the submarine and handling emergencies can call for precise movements or quick reactions, which makes this only a decent fit for a treadmill or bike.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You do not need a powerful computer to run Barotrauma. The listed requirements include a dual core CPU, 4GB of RAM, and a graphics card that supports OpenGL 3.2, so modest systems should be enough.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Barotrauma is a strong cooperative game, but the quick reactions it sometimes asks for limit how well it fits a workout. It can work on a treadmill or bike, just not as smoothly as a game that lets you stay focused on your pace.

### Overall Score: 2.3 out of 3
