Palworld mixes crafting, exploring, and battling with pals. The colorful look is nice, and the action usually keeps moving, though the game is not a perfect match for cardio.

## Gameplay

There is plenty to do, and slow stretches are rare. Small cutscenes interrupt the flow now and then, while combat asks you to move, dodge, and keep track of your pals. That makes the gameplay fun, but it can pull your focus from your workout.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controls mostly stick to movement and occasional button presses, so they are manageable while exercising. Combat can get hectic when you need to dodge or deal with larger groups of enemies. It is playable on a treadmill or bike, but you may have to ease up to keep up.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements are moderate, though you will want a midrange GPU for better performance at higher settings. A stronger computer can help keep things smooth during demanding scenes. You do not need a high end setup, but performance may vary.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Palworld is fun, with colorful visuals and a good mix of activities. Its simple controls help during a workout, but combat can be distracting and the computer requirements are not especially light. It works for cardio, though it is not an easy game to play on autopilot.

### Overall Score: 2.2 out of 3
