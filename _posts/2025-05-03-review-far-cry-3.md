Far Cry 3 mixes exploration and combat in a tropical setting. It has slower stretches, so it is not always easy to stay focused on your workout.

## Gameplay

You get missions, side quests, stealth, combat and a story set among rival factions and wild animals. Most of it keeps things moving, but navigating the environment and less action focused tasks can drag a bit.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a standard controller, and the controls do not call for constant fast movements. Still, aiming and some boss fights need you to focus, which makes it harder to keep a strong workout going.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Far Cry 3 needs a decent graphics card and CPU, but it runs smoothly on mid range systems. If you play on a laptop, make sure it has enough ventilation to avoid overheating.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The mix of action and exploration makes Far Cry 3 a decent workout game, but the slower sections and demanding fights can interrupt your pace. Controller support helps, and the computer requirements are reasonable if you have a solid mid range setup.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
