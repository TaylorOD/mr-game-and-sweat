## Gameplay

PC Building Simulator is a poor workout companion because building PCs takes careful clicks and menu work, not quick decisions you can make while bouncing along. The repair jobs give you clear goals, from diagnosing faults to choosing and installing parts, but the pace is slow and methodical. You may enjoy the hands on process, but it does little to distract you from a workout.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The pace is forgiving, but you need accurate inputs to select parts and complete assembly steps. That precision is awkward while your body is moving, so this suits a very steady bike or a slow treadmill much better than a brisk run.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum specs ask for a GeForce GTX 660 or Radeon R9 285, both older dedicated graphics cards. Integrated graphics may work, but are not supported, so this is not a safe pick if your computer relies on them.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

PC Building Simulator is a relaxed repair sim with a satisfying list of jobs to tackle, but the careful clicking makes it a poor fit for exercise. You can play at a gentle pace, though you may spend more time looking at the screen than focusing on your stride.

### Overall Score: 1.33 out of 3
