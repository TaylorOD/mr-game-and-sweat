Albion Online is a sandbox MMORPG built around exploring, crafting, gathering resources, and PvP. Some parts suit a workout better than others, so how much attention you need depends on what you are doing.

## Gameplay

Gathering and quieter parts of the world move at a relaxed pace, and the interface is simple to manage. Real time combat and player encounters can demand more focus, though, so it is not always easy to split your attention. There are few cutscenes to interrupt play.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play on a treadmill or bike, and the game supports controller input. Combat does not rely on fast reflexes, but PvP can require more attention and may disrupt your exercise pace. That makes it a decent fit, with some limits when fights start.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Albion Online has modest computer requirements. The minimum calls for a 2.0 GHz processor and 4GB of RAM, while the recommended setup includes a dedicated graphics card.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The slow stretches and simple controls make Albion Online a reasonable workout companion, but PvP can take more focus than you may want while exercising. It works well enough on a treadmill or bike, though the fit is not perfect.

### Overall Score: 2.5 out of 3
