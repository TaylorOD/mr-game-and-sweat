War Thunder lets you fight in tanks, planes, and naval vessels, with a mix of simulation and arcade action. You can take the battles to the air or the ground.

## Gameplay

Combat moves quickly, with short pauses while you look for a target. Those breaks make the pace less constant, but the battles give you plenty to focus on.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game supports gamepads, which makes it possible to play while exercising. Precise aiming and involved controls take more dexterity than you may want on a treadmill or bike, though the learning curve is manageable.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

War Thunder can run on a midrange PC. Graphics settings and the number of troops on the battlefield can affect performance, and higher specs are needed for the best visuals.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

War Thunder has strong battles, but its controls can be a hassle during cardio. Check that your PC can handle it, and expect to trade some workout convenience for the action.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
