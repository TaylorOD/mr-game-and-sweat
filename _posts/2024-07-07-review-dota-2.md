Dota 2 has deep strategy and a large hero roster, giving matches plenty to think about. That focus can pull your attention away from your workout.

## Gameplay

Matches usually last 30 to 50 minutes and mix action with planning and teamwork. The strategic fights can keep you engaged, but they can also make you lose track of your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller, but keyboard and mouse are the standard, which makes Dota 2 a clumsy fit for many workout setups. It does not demand constant rapid movement, though team fights and other intense moments still need quick coordination.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You do not need a top end PC, but a decent midrange system should run the game smoothly. Higher settings can ask more from your hardware, so the requirements are not a complete nonissue.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Dota 2 is strong as a strategy game, but it is only an okay workout partner. The usual controls and moments that demand quick reactions can make it hard to balance the match with exercise.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
