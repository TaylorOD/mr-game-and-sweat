Trying to jog steadily while your crew is loading cannons and another ship is trying to board you sounds fun in theory, but I think Blackwake asks for more attention than your legs can spare. I’d save it for sitting down, unless your workout is mostly a gentle ride and you’re happy to let the game take priority.

## Gameplay

The teamwork is the part that makes Blackwake sound appealing to me. You aren’t just pointing a gun at a ship and hoping for the best, since your crew has to work together to fire cannons, sink the other ship or get aboard and fight with firearms and steel. That gives the action a shared purpose, which I like more than a shooter where everyone runs off to do their own thing.

I also think the same teamwork could make the game hard to enjoy casually. When other people are relying on you, it’s tough to decide that you’re going to take a few minutes to focus on your stride, and it’s not like a single player game where you can pause while you sort yourself out. I don’t know how forgiving a particular match will feel, but the basic setup puts a lot of pressure on staying involved.

The ship combat sounds like the most promising part for me, because aiming cannons at another ship is a pretty clear job and the payoff is easy to understand. You’re trying to damage the other crew’s ship, and there’s something appealing about that being a group effort instead of one person doing all the work. I can picture myself getting invested in the plan and then forgetting I was supposed to be exercising, which is not ideal, but it does sound fun.

Boarding sounds like the point where I’d get much less comfortable. Fighting with firearms and steel once you’re on the enemy ship sounds more like a regular first person shooter, and fast aiming is not what I’d want to be doing while bouncing along on a treadmill. Maybe the boarding fights are the part you’d look forward to most, and I get that, but I think the cannons are a better fit for me.

As a free to play game, Blackwake is easier to recommend trying than a game that asks you to pay up front, and that matters when you’re not sure whether the teamwork will click for you. I’d give it a try if the idea of working with a crew sounds fun, but I wouldn’t expect it to become an easy background game. It wants your attention.

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The bigger issue for exercise is that the game’s action depends on what other players are doing, so you can’t set your own pace and take a break whenever your breathing gets rough. A treadmill makes the problem more obvious because your hands and aim are moving while your body is moving, and a bike doesn’t solve the attention part either. I think a gentle ride would be less awkward than running, but I’d still expect to focus on the match more than the workout.

I can’t confirm full controller support from the information here, so I wouldn’t plan a workout around a controller before testing it. And even with a controller, aiming at ships and fighting during a boarding action sound like the sort of things where a small mistake could matter. You might be able to manage the cannon work at an easy pace, but I wouldn’t assume the rest will feel relaxed.

If you’re set on trying it while moving, I’d use a stationary bike, keep the effort low, and start with the ship combat rather than treating every boarding fight as something you need to win. I’d also try a short session first, because it’s much easier to turn the bike off than to discover halfway through a hard run that you’re trying to steer a ship and keep your feet under you at the same time. That’s a lot to ask of one brain.

I don’t know that Blackwake is a game I’d choose specifically for exercise, but I can see a player who already loves coordinated multiplayer having a good time with it. For me, the match asking for attention isn’t a small detail. It’s the whole reason I’d keep it off the treadmill.

## Computer Requirements

This needs a dedicated graphics card in the range of an older GTX 660 or R9 270, so I wouldn’t count on integrated graphics to do the job. I’d check the graphics card first, since the minimum target is for low settings and medium shadows at about 60 frames per second at 1080p.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Blackwake sounds like a fun team shooter, but I’d play it seated rather than trying to make it part of a run. Try it if the crew based ship combat is what you want, and skip it as an exercise game if you need to keep your attention on moving.
