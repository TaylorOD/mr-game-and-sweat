Euro Truck Simulator 2 puts you in the role of a truck driver hauling goods across Europe. Its slow, methodical pace is a good fit if you like driving and managing logistics.

## Gameplay

The long drives and lack of fast action make it easy to keep your focus on the workout. Some stretches can feel repetitive, but the forgiving pace means you can keep moving without losing track of the game.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The relaxed controls work well on a treadmill or bike, and the game supports plenty of controllers and steering wheels. You do not need constant hand movement or quick reflexes, though you may need to focus more when precise steering comes up.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Euro Truck Simulator 2 is relatively light on computer requirements and can run smoothly on mid range systems. It does not need the latest graphics card.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Euro Truck Simulator 2 is a good workout game thanks to its calm pace and flexible controls. The driving can get repetitive, but the game does not demand much of your reflexes or computer.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
