Hell Let Loose is a World War II game built around teamwork, strategy, and communication. Its large maps and tactical battles can be engaging, but that does not always make it a good workout game.

## Gameplay

Matches have 50 players, and the action shifts between firefights and tactical positioning. Long periods of repositioning or waiting to respawn can slow things down and make it harder to keep a steady pace. The strong moments help, but the downtime gets in the way.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller, but quick movements and reflexes make this a tough game to play while exercising. Intense moments can pull your focus from your rhythm or footing. It is manageable if you can shift your attention back quickly, but it is not a great fit for focused cardio.

### Category Score: 1.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Hell Let Loose needs a solid computer to run well. Minimum settings call for an NVIDIA GTX 1050 or AMD Radeon R9 380, while the recommended specs include an RTX 2060 or equivalent.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Hell Let Loose has good teamwork and firefights, but slow stretches and demanding moments make it a mixed choice for running or biking. The computer requirements are also worth checking before you play. Overall, it works better as a game to focus on than as a workout companion.

### Overall Score: 1.83 out of 3
