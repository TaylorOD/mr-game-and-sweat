FIFA 22 is a fast football sim that suits a workout. Matches keep you involved, and the short cutscenes do not interrupt your pace for long.

## Gameplay

Matches move quickly, with realistic ball physics and player movement. You get a mix of quick decisions and strategy, while brief cutscenes keep interruptions to a minimum.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

A controller makes FIFA 22 easy to manage while you run or ride. Some plays need quick timing, but the controls do not demand frantic button presses or complicated moves, so you can keep a comfortable pace.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

For smooth graphics and fast loading, you need a decent CPU and GPU. A general gaming setup should run it fine, and mid range computers can use lower settings, but high resolution or frame rates can hurt performance while you exercise.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

FIFA 22 is a good fit for workouts thanks to its active gameplay and controller support. Its PC requirements are the main drawback, so it is a solid choice if your setup can handle it.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
