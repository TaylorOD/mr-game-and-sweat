theHunter: Call of the Wild is a hunting game with a lot of open space and wildlife to track. Its calm pace suits a workout better than a game that keeps you busy every second.

## Gameplay

Tracking animals, setting up blinds, and taking shots make for satisfying gameplay. Most of the time, you can explore at your own pace, with few cutscenes or long tutorials to interrupt you.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controls work with a gamepad or keyboard, and the slow pace gives you time to catch your breath. But animals can appear suddenly, so you may need quick reactions while running or riding.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game asks for a decent processor and graphics card to run smoothly on high settings. Older computers can use lower settings, but you will lose some detail.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The steady pace and enjoyable hunting gameplay make this a good option for exercise. Sudden encounters can be awkward while you are moving, and the computer requirements may be a hurdle for some players.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3

If you want to track wildlife while getting some cardio, this game is worth a look. Just expect to manage the occasional quick reaction and check that your computer can run it.
