Brawlhalla is a free to play platform fighting game where you pick a legend with their own abilities and weapons. It is easy to get into, but its competitive matches still ask you to pay attention.

## Gameplay

Combat is quick and fluid, and matches last only a few minutes. The mix of characters and weapons gives each match some variety, and there is no long downtime to break up your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

A controller lets you play without reaching for a keyboard and mouse. Quick reactions are still part of the game, so it can be harder to manage during an intense workout than the controls alone suggest.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Brawlhalla runs on mid range computers and is designed to work on lower end systems too. You do not need top of the line hardware, though performance still depends on your setup.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The fast matches can keep you occupied while you run or ride, but the quick reactions take some attention. It is manageable, though not an ideal fit if you want to focus fully on your pace.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3

Brawlhalla is a solid game to play while exercising, but it takes more coordination than a workout friendly game should. Its modest computer requirements help, while its quick action keeps it from being an easy pick for every run or ride.
