Planet Zoo has you build and manage a zoo, with animal welfare, conservation and guest needs to consider. It can work during a moderate workout, but some of its management asks for more focus than you may want to give while moving.

## Gameplay

Building habitats, managing animal welfare and keeping guests happy make up most of the work. You can spend a lot of time planning paths, habitats and landscaping, though some of that is slow enough to handle at a steady pace. Micromanagement can pull your attention away from running.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support lets you move around your zoo without sticking to a keyboard and mouse. Most actions are manageable, and planning and building leave room to keep a moderate pace. Detailed management or quick adjustments can be awkward if you’re bouncing on a treadmill.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Expect a reasonably capable computer: the minimum is an Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5, 8 GB of RAM, and an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 770 or AMD Radeon RX 470. Lower end systems may hiccup, especially as your zoo grows. A large, complex zoo can push your computer, so the requirements are worth considering.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Planet Zoo is a decent fit for a moderate workout when you’re building, but micromanagement can take more focus. Controller support helps, though a less powerful computer may struggle as your zoo grows.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
