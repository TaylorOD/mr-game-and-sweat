Call of Duty: Black Ops III has fast, intense action and keeps you busy with combat and objectives. It can work for cardio, but its quick firefights take focus.

## Gameplay

The action moves quickly, and the maps offer plenty of detail and tactical options. Multiplayer and campaign missions keep things engaging, with few long cutscenes to interrupt the flow.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You use a traditional controller, which is handy while exercising, but firefights call for precision and quick reflexes. At a steady jogging or cycling pace, it is manageable, though you still need to pay attention to your balance.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Black Ops III is demanding on PC, and a mid to high end machine is recommended for smooth play. Lower end computers may have performance dips that can distract you during a workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The gameplay is strong, but the precision controls make it only a decent fit for exercise. It also asks a lot from your PC, so the overall package is good, not a workout essential.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
