Cyberpunk 2077 gives you a big city to explore, with role playing, customization, and plenty of story. There is a lot to do, but the action and long conversations make it a mixed fit for exercising.

## Gameplay

You get a mix of fast action and story quests, with stealth, combat, and decisions along the way. The slower scenes and cutscenes give you a breather, but lots of dialogue can break your workout rhythm.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game supports a controller, which helps when you are exercising. Combat can call for quick reflexes and precise movements, so it may be hard to keep a steady pace, while quieter moments are easier to follow.

### Category Score: 1.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Cyberpunk 2077 asks a lot of your computer. You need a relatively powerful setup with substantial RAM and a strong graphics card, which can be a problem if your machine is less capable.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Cyberpunk 2077 can work alongside a workout, but its action and dialogue make it hard to keep a steady rhythm. You get plenty of story and fast combat, though neither is always easy to manage while exercising.

### Overall Score: 1.83 out of 3
