Forza Horizon 5 is an open world racing game set in Mexico. The varied environments and range of vehicles make it fun to explore, though your computer needs enough power to run it well.

## Gameplay

The driving is fun, with races and casual drives across deserts and jungles. There is little downtime, and the occasional short cutscene does not interrupt the flow for long.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The standard controller inputs are manageable while you run or ride. Sharp turns and manual gear changes take some coordination, but the game does not depend on constant quick reflexes.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Forza Horizon 5 can be demanding on your computer, and a lower spec system may lag during races. The listed requirements call for an AMD or Intel processor, an NVIDIA GTX 970 or AMD RX 0500 series graphics card, and 8GB or more of RAM.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Forza Horizon 5 pairs well with a workout, and its races can help take your mind off exercise. The main drawback is its computer requirements, which keep it from being an easy recommendation overall.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
