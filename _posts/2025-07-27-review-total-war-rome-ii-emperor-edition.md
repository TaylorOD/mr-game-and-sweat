Total War: ROME II - Emperor Edition mixes turn based planning with real time battles. It can work during a workout, but some parts are slow and others need more of your attention.

## Gameplay

The campaign map and battles give you plenty to think about. Diplomacy and AI turns can run long, so the pace is uneven.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support and the focus on planning make it playable while you exercise. Battles can pull your attention away from your workout, so it fits better with moderate exercise than fast input does.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Large battles can be demanding on your computer. It runs on a wide range of setups, but older hardware may get choppy during busy scenes.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

ROME II is a decent workout game, though slow turns and demanding battles can interrupt your rhythm. Controller support helps, but you still need a computer that can handle it.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
