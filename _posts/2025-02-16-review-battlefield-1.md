Battlefield 1 is set during World War I and pairs a campaign with multiplayer battles. The visuals are strong, and the fast action makes it a good fit for a workout.

## Gameplay

Battles are fast paced, with gunfights and squad coordination keeping you busy. There is little downtime, and campaign cutscenes are brief, so the action keeps moving.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller works well for playing on a treadmill or bike. Some moments call for quick reactions, but they are not too demanding while you keep moving.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Battlefield 1 needs a decent graphics card and processor. Minimum settings are manageable on most modern computers, but higher settings can push your hardware hard.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The gameplay and controller support make Battlefield 1 a good workout game. Its demanding computer requirements make it a less easy recommendation overall.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
