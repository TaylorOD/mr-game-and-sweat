Fallout 4 has a large world to explore, with quests, characters, and combat. For a workout, though, the slower dialogue and cutscenes can make it hard to keep a steady pace.

## Gameplay

Exploration is engaging, and combat can keep you alert, but some parts slow down for lengthy dialogue and cutscenes. Those pauses can interrupt your rhythm, even if they give you a chance to catch your breath.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller is manageable, and much of the game does not demand quick reflexes. Firefights can get hectic, though, as you move and look for cover, which may throw off your pace on a treadmill or bike.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Fallout 4 needs a decent computer to run well, and weaker setups may lag. The review recommends at least an Intel Core i5 processor, 8GB RAM, and a dedicated graphics card, so this is a real drawback if your computer falls short.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The open world is a plus, but slow sections and computer demands make Fallout 4 a mixed pick for exercising. You can play while working out, but you may need to adjust your pace during dialogue and firefights.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3

The game has plenty to explore, but its stops and starts can get in the way of a steady workout. It may suit you if you are willing to adapt, though it is not an especially easy game to pair with exercise.
