Terraria is a 2D sandbox adventure built around exploring, building, and fighting. There is plenty to do at your own pace, which makes it easy to stay engaged during a workout.

## Gameplay

You can dig underground, find treasures, build structures, and fight enemies at your own pace. There is very little downtime, and cut scenes are almost nonexistent. The mix keeps the game moving without making every moment feel demanding.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The simple controls and controller support make Terraria a good fit while running or riding. You can keep a steady pace, since the game rarely asks for fast reactions or movements. That makes it easy to follow during a workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Terraria runs smoothly on a wide range of computers, including older systems. Its low requirements mean technical issues are unlikely to interrupt your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Terraria is a strong choice for gaming while you exercise. It has engaging gameplay, works well on a treadmill or bike, and does not ask much of your computer.

### Overall Score: 3 out of 3
