Grounded shrinks you to the size of an ant in a suburban backyard. You gather resources, face insects, and build a base to survive.

## Gameplay

You run, jump, and climb as you explore, and the goals are clear. Upgrading gear and finding crafting recipes give you a solid sense of progress, while the lack of long cutscenes keeps the pace moving. You can explore at your own pace when you need a breather.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The first person view and controller controls work well enough for a treadmill or bike. Some fights with aggressive insects call for quick reflexes, though, so you may need to pay closer attention and adjust your workout. It is playable while exercising, but not effortless.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum requirements are an Intel i5 2500 or AMD FX 6100, 8 GB of RAM, and a GTX 650 or Radeon R7 360. More demanding graphics settings or a smoother experience call for a stronger system. It does not need a high end PC, but the requirements are still worth checking.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Grounded has good survival and exploration gameplay, but its action can interrupt your workout rhythm. The ability to explore at your own pace helps, though the game still asks for focus and balance. It is a solid game, but only an okay fit for fitness gaming.

### Overall Score: 2.3 out of 3
