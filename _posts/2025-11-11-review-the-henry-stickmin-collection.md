The Henry Stickmin Collection can work for a treadmill if you are fine with watching animated scenes between choices. Some quick prompts need your attention and timing, so it is less suited to a hard run.

## Gameplay

You watch Henry’s plans go wrong, pick from a few options, and see what happens. Failed choices are often funny, and you can quickly try another path, but there are plenty of scenes where you are mostly watching instead of playing. It is engaging in short bursts, with some slower stretches.  
### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Most choices are simple, but some prompts call for quick reactions or accurate timing. That can be awkward when you are moving, especially if you need to steady your hands to get through a section.  
### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum specs ask for an Intel HD 4000, 4 GB of RAM, and just 1 GB of storage. That is a light load for a PC, and integrated graphics are enough.  
### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The Henry Stickmin Collection is a funny, low demand game with lots of branching choices and silly failures. It is better for an easy workout than a fast run, since the timed prompts can pull your attention away from your pace.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
