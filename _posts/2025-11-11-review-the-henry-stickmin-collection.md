The Henry Stickmin Collection offers a light-hearted choose-your-own-path adventure that lends itself to a casual gaming experience. However, the game's reliance on decision-making and humor may detract from intense workout sessions since it requires a bit more focus than more active titles. 

# Gameplay

The gameplay consists of a series of scenarios where players must make choices to progress, often facing hilarious and unexpected consequences. The charm of failing in creative ways can keep players engaged, making you want to jump back on the treadmill or bike to see what happens next. However, certain sections do rely on reading and decision-making, which can pull your attention away from your workout. Overall, while there’s enough variety to keep the gameplay entertaining, be prepared for slow moments as you weigh your options.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

# Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

With no controller support, The Henry Stickmin Collection requires a keyboard and mouse for full control, making it less ideal for use while exercising. The gameplay isn't particularly fast-paced, but the need for precise mouse movements and timed choices could be a distraction on a treadmill or bike, especially during those critical decision moments when you might need to focus. This limitations make it challenging to play comfortably while staying active.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

# Computer Requirements

The game is quite lightweight and can run on integrated graphics, requiring just a 64-bit OS, a dual-core processor, 4 GB of RAM, and minimal storage space. This low-demand nature allows it to be accessible on older or less capable PC setups, meaning you won’t need a powerhouse to enjoy the experience.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

# Conclusion and Score

In conclusion, The Henry Stickmin Collection is a whimsical adventure that can provide moments of clever fun but may not be the best fit for those looking to maintain a high-energy exercise session. The absence of controller support and occasional slower gameplay could hinder your ability to enjoy it fully while working out. Ultimately, pick this game for a relaxing break from your routine rather than a companion to your treadmill or bike ride.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
