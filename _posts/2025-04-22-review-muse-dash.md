Muse Dash is a rhythm action game where you tap or swipe to the beat. You use those moves to get past obstacles, defeat enemies, and collect items.

## Gameplay

The levels move quickly, with little downtime and no long cut scenes. Some parts need quick reflexes and precise timing, which can be hard when you are also trying to hold a steady pace.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller, which works well for using the game on a treadmill or bike. But the quick rhythmic movements can make it harder to keep your balance or a steady pace, and pushing harder physically can hurt your timing.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Muse Dash has low computer requirements and can run smoothly on a wide range of devices. You do not need a high end graphics card or much RAM.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Muse Dash is a solid rhythm game, but its timing can make it a mixed fit for exercise. Controller support and low computer requirements help, though it still takes focus to play while keeping your pace.

### Overall Score: 2.5 out of 3
