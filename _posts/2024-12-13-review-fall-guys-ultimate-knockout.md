Fall Guys mixes obstacle courses, team challenges, and survival rounds. The colorful chaos is fun, and matches move along quickly.

## Gameplay

The rounds are fast, with quick eliminations and little downtime. You dodge hammers, jump across spinning platforms, and race other players, so there is plenty to keep you focused.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

It is made for a controller, and the simple movement controls work from a stationary position. Quick reactions can be hard at higher exercise intensities, though, so it is easier to play at a steady pace.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Fall Guys does not need a powerful computer. A basic Windows 10 setup with a dual core processor and integrated graphics can run it, though some visual settings may need more power.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The quick rounds and goofy challenges make Fall Guys a fun game to play while exercising. Still, the quick reactions can be a problem on a treadmill or bike, which keeps it from being an ideal fit.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3 

Fall Guys is fun and accessible, but it takes focus while you exercise. It is a decent workout game, though the controller demands make it a mixed choice.
