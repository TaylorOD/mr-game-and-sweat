Wildlands takes you to Bolivia to take on a drug cartel. Its open world gives you room to tackle missions with stealth or go in shooting.

## Gameplay

The mix of strategy and action gives you choices in how to approach missions. There are plenty of missions and side quests, though cutscenes slow things down now and then. Co-op is a fun option, too.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller, which helps when you’re exercising. But firefights call for quick reflexes, and intense sequences can make it tough to keep your balance and focus on your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Wildlands needs a decent gaming computer to run smoothly at higher settings. A steady frame rate matters most during firefights and while exploring the open world.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Wildlands has solid tactical gameplay, but its action can be awkward to handle during a workout. Controller support helps, though the computer demands and quick firefights keep it from being an easy pick for the treadmill or bike.

### Overall Score: 2.2 out of 3
