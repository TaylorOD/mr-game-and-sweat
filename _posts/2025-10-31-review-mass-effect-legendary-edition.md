Mass Effect Legendary Edition brings the trilogy together with improved graphics and gameplay. It has a lot to offer, though that doesn’t automatically make it a perfect fit for exercise.

## Gameplay

The story, characters, and tactical combat make for strong gameplay. Action and dialogue keep things moving, and cutscenes are shorter and less frequent than in the original games. There are still plenty of decisions to make during combat, so you’ll need to keep your attention on the game.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You play with a controller, and the action works on both a treadmill and a bike. Quick time events can call for sudden movements, which are awkward when you’re trying to keep your balance. It doesn’t demand extreme speed or coordination, but it still takes some focus to play while exercising.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements are fairly manageable, and the game can run on mid tier hardware. A decent CPU and GPU help with the improved visuals, especially during action scenes. Keeping your system updated can also help performance.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The story and gameplay make Mass Effect Legendary Edition a good game, but exercise adds a few hassles. Quick time events and combat decisions can distract you from keeping a steady pace. Its computer requirements are reasonable, though not especially light.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
