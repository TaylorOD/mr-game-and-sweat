## Gameplay

Titanfall 2 has fast, fun gameplay, with wall running, double jumps, and tactical moves that keep you busy. Its levels encourage exploration, and the campaign takes about 6 to 8 hours without many long cutscenes.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller works well, but the quick movement and reflexes needed in some moments can make it harder to focus on your workout. Multiplayer can be especially demanding, so this is more manageable at a steady pace than during a hard run or ride.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The PC requirements are moderate, but you still need a decent setup. The recommended specs are an Intel i5 processor or equivalent, 8GB of RAM, and a GTX 660.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Titanfall 2 is a good game, with lively action and a campaign that keeps moving. It can take some focus to play while exercising, and its PC requirements are not especially light, so it is a decent workout pick rather than an easy fit.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
