Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition mixes exploration, puzzles, action, and story. It has good moments, but it is not a perfect fit for a workout.

## Gameplay

Exploration and puzzles are the best parts of the gameplay, with action and platforming mixed in. Slower sections and cutscenes interrupt the pace, and quick time events can be tricky when you are exercising.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller works well, which helps when your focus is split between the game and your workout. Combat and traversal take hand eye coordination and quick reactions, so the game is manageable on a treadmill or bike, but not effortless.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is moderately demanding on PC, and you will need a decent setup. A mid range gaming PC should be enough, but lower end machines may have frame drops during intense scenes.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition has solid gameplay, but cutscenes and slower stretches can disrupt your workout. It works reasonably well on a treadmill or bike, though it takes some attention and a decent PC.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
