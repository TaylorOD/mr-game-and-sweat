Conan Exiles is a survival game about gathering resources, crafting, and fighting in an open world based on Conan. You can explore alone or play with others, though the pace is not always a great fit for a workout.

## Gameplay

You gather resources, build shelters, and fight wild beasts or hostile players. Combat feels responsive, but crafting and waiting for resources slow things down. The mix works for a workout, though the pace is uneven.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support means you do not need rapid mouse movements or lots of keyboard commands. Most movement and attacks can be handled at a moderate pace, but combat still asks for quick reactions. That can interrupt your rhythm, especially if you want faster action.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Conan Exiles asks more of your computer than some other survival games, especially on populated servers or high graphics settings. The review lists an Intel Core i5 or equivalent, 8 GB of RAM, and a decent graphics card. That makes it a middling choice if your computer is older.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Conan Exiles has survival, exploration, and combat to keep you playing, but its slower crafting stretches can disrupt your workout flow. Controller support helps, while the computer requirements are a real consideration, so it is a decent but not ideal fit.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
