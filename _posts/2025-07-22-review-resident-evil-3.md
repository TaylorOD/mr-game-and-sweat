Resident Evil 3 is a remake of a survival horror game, with zombies, action, and puzzles. It has some good stretches, but the slower parts can drag during a workout.

## Gameplay

The action and puzzles can keep you engaged, and the mix of tense moments and bursts of action mostly works. Long cutscenes and slower sequences can interrupt your workout, so the pace is uneven.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support makes it easier to play on a treadmill or bike without reaching for a keyboard and mouse. Chase scenes with Nemesis call for quick reactions, which can distract you from keeping a steady pace. You can make it work, but it takes some attention.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You need a decent computer to run the game smoothly. The minimum specs are an Intel Core i5 4460 or AMD FX 6300 processor, 8 GB of RAM, and a GTX 760 or Radeon R7 260 graphics card. Higher settings need more, which can make gaming and exercise harder to manage at the same time.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Resident Evil 3 has plenty of action, but slower moments and quick reaction demands make it a mixed fit for exercise. It works for a workout, as long as you do not expect a steady pace from the game.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
