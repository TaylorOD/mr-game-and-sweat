Ring of Elysium is a battle royale game about surviving in a changing environment with extreme weather. Its constant movement and action could suit a workout, though combat can demand more attention than you want to give it while exercising.

## Gameplay

The action is engaging and moves along without many slow sections or long cutscenes. You can take different approaches as you move across varied terrain, but frantic fights can distract you from keeping a steady pace on a treadmill or bike.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller, which helps when you are exercising. Most moments call for moderate decisions rather than quick reactions, but combat can still get hectic. That makes it workable on a treadmill or bike, not an especially easy fit.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game does not require the latest hardware, though a mid range computer is advisable for smoother play. Lower end systems may lag during intense or crowded matches, which can interrupt your workout. It is accessible for many players, but not every computer will run it smoothly.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Ring of Elysium has engaging action, but intense fights can make it hard to focus on your workout. Controller support helps, yet the game still takes enough attention that it is only an okay fit for treadmill or bike play. Its computer requirements are fairly reasonable, with some risk of lag on lower end systems.

### Overall Score: 2.2 out of 3
