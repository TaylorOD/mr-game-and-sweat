Phasmophobia is a co-op horror game about investigating haunted places with friends. You use ghost hunting gear and clues to figure out what spirit is there, which makes for a good team game.

## Gameplay

The ghost hunting is fun, and the mix of exploring, using gear and piecing together clues keeps you involved. Ghost encounters can get tense, while the slower clue checks give you a chance to catch your breath. There are no long cutscenes getting in the way.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a traditional controller, and the game does not rely on fast reflexes. Still, you need to move around and pay attention to what you are doing, so it is not an easy fit while exercising. VR is optional, but it can make things more complicated on a treadmill or bike.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The recommended specs include an Intel Core i5 4590 or AMD FX 8350 processor and at least 8GB of RAM. It also runs best with a dedicated graphics card, such as an NVIDIA GTX 970 or AMD Radeon RX 480. That is a fair bit to ask of a computer, so check your setup before you play.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Phasmophobia has strong ghost hunting gameplay and works reasonably well with a workout, but using a controller while exercising takes some attention. The computer requirements are a drawback, so this is a good game with a few practical limits.

### Overall Score: 2.5 out of 3
