Sleeping Dogs: Definitive Edition is an action game set in Hong Kong, with martial arts combat, driving and stealth. It has plenty to do, but some of that action can be tough to manage while you exercise.

## Gameplay

The missions mix hand to hand combat, vehicle chases and stealth. Cutscenes are usually short enough that you can get back to playing without a long break. There is a good variety of action and exploration to keep the game moving.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3  

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can control the game with a controller without constant rapid button presses. Some fights and timed chases need quick reactions, though, so they can disrupt your pace. Cruising around the city is more relaxed, but sudden action can still pull your focus away from your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3  

## Computer Requirements

The game needs at least an Intel Core i5, 4GB of RAM and an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 560 or AMD Radeon HD 6870 graphics card for a smooth experience. Older systems may need lower graphics settings. Check that your computer can handle it before you start exercising.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3  

## Conclusion and Score

Sleeping Dogs is a good action game, but it is only an okay fit for a workout. The varied gameplay is a plus, though the sudden action can make it harder to focus on running or riding.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
