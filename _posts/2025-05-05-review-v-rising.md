V Rising puts you in the role of a vampire waking after centuries of sleep. You gather resources, build a castle, fight enemies, and face rival vampires in the land of Vardoran. Exploration, crafting, combat, and multiplayer give you plenty to do.

## Gameplay

There is a good mix of exploring, gathering, farming, combat, and crafting. You manage your vampire’s thirst for blood while building a castle, and the game introduces new elements gradually. There are few long cutscenes or stretches of downtime to slow things down.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller while walking or biking, and the controls do not call for quick reflexes or complicated moves. Combat and exploration allow for moderate movement, so you are less likely to lose your balance. It is a strong fit for playing while you exercise.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

V Rising runs well on midrange PCs, but its ideal specs are a GTX 970 or equivalent, an i5 processor, and 16GB of RAM. That is a decent amount to ask of a computer, so older systems may need a closer look.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

V Rising is a good match for treadmill and bike play, with steady gameplay and controller friendly controls. The computer requirements keep it from being an easy recommendation for everyone.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
