StarCraft II is a great real time strategy game, but its mouse and keyboard controls make it a poor fit for a treadmill or bike. Building a base and directing troops take the kind of focus and precision that are much easier to manage at a desk.

## Gameplay

You gather resources, build a base, and command armies in battles that reward careful planning. The Wings of Liberty campaign and multiplayer are free to play through Battle.net, and both can keep you thinking hard, though campaign scenes and quieter stretches slow the pace. That focus is part of the appeal, but it can pull your attention away from your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

StarCraft II has no controller support and is built around mouse and keyboard. Precise clicks and fast decisions are awkward while moving, and competitive multiplayer does not wait for you to catch your breath.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum requirements are modest by current standards and call for older graphics hardware rather than a high end gaming PC. A basic dedicated graphics card should be enough.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

StarCraft II is worth playing for its strategy, battles, and free campaign, but those strengths depend on giving it your full attention. Skip it during your workout and play it at your desk.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3
