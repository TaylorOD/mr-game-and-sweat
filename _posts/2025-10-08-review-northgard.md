Northgard is a real time strategy game where you lead a Viking clan as it explores and settles a new continent. You gather resources, build up your settlement, and deal with threats.

## Gameplay

You manage resources, buildings, and your population’s happiness and health. The strategy is satisfying, and procedurally generated maps keep each game fresh.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can pause to give commands, so the game does not demand quick reactions while you run or ride. It has full controller support, which makes it easy to play without disrupting your pace.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Northgard runs smoothly on lower end systems and does not demand much from your computer. That makes it a practical choice if you want to play while exercising without worrying about lag or performance problems.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Northgard is a strong fit for a treadmill or bike workout. Its thoughtful pace, controller support, and modest computer requirements make it easy to keep playing while you exercise.

### Overall Score: 3 out of 3
