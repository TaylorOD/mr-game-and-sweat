## Gameplay

You play an immigration officer in the fictional dystopian nation of Arstotzka, checking documents and deciding who can enter. The moral choices give the routine some variety, but inspecting papers can get repetitive over time.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support makes it possible to play without a mouse and keyboard, and the controls do not call for quick movements. The game still demands attention and quick decisions, which can be tough during a hard workout. It works better at a manageable pace than at high intensity.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Papers, Please runs well on a range of systems, including older machines. Its minimalist art style keeps its computer requirements low, so you do not need a high end gaming computer to play.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Papers, Please is a good option for a workout if you want document checks and moral decisions to keep your mind busy. Controller play helps, but the focus it demands makes it less suited to an intense workout. Its low computer requirements make it easy to run on many setups.

### Overall Score: 2.5 out of 3
