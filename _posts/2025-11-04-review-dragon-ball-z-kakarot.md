"DRAGON BALL Z: KAKAROT" invites players into the beloved universe of Dragon Ball Z, offering an engaging RPG experience that can be challenging to balance with a workout routine. While the game's captivating storytelling and nostalgia factor can make it enticing, certain gameplay elements may detract from an effective exercise session.

# Gameplay

The gameplay is a blend of action and RPG elements, immersing players in the iconic story of Dragon Ball Z. Combat is fluid and exciting, with a mix of exploration and battles that keeps you engaged. However, the game can be bogged down by lengthy cut scenes and slow transitions, which may force you to pause your exercise routine. These slower sections can disrupt your heart rate and momentum if you're running or cycling, potentially leading to a less effective workout. 

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

# Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

"DRAGON BALL Z: KAKAROT" boasts full controller support, which is a significant advantage when playing on a treadmill or bike. The controls are intuitive and do not require the kind of quick-twitch reflexes that might interrupt your workout flow. However, the presence of slower-paced segments does mean that you may find yourself frequently slowing down or stopping to engage with the story, making it less than ideal for continuous physical activity.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

# Computer Requirements

The game requires a relatively modest setup, needing a 64-bit operating system and a decent processor like an Intel Core i5-2400 or AMD Phenom II X6 1100T. With minimum requirements including 4 GB RAM and a graphics card such as the GTX 750 Ti or Radeon HD 7950, it should run well on most modern gaming PCs. Just keep in mind that with 36 GB of storage needed, you'll want to clear some space if your rig is close to capacity.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

# Conclusion and Score

"DRAGON BALL Z: KAKAROT" succeeds in delivering an engaging RPG experience rich in nostalgia that will resonate with fans. While the full controller support is a plus for exercising gamers, the game does have pacing issues that can interrupt your workout flow. For those looking to balance gaming with fitness, it’s essential to consider these aspects carefully.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
