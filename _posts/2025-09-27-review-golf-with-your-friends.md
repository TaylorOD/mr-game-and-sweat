Golf With Your Friends puts a playful spin on mini golf with multiplayer. It’s a good fit for exercise, though the computer performance could be better.

## Gameplay

The gameplay is quick to pick up, with short rounds and no long cut scenes or tedious tutorials. Courses have obstacles like moving ramps and unpredictable slopes, so you still get some strategy between shots. It’s a fun way to break up a run or ride without needing to focus on constant action.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You take turns adjusting your shot, so the controls don’t demand rapid movements. Controller support makes it easy to play while running or riding, and the pace should let you keep your workout rhythm.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game runs on lower end systems, so you don’t need high end hardware. You can also turn up the graphics while keeping a good frame rate and clear visuals. Still, performance optimization could be better on more powerful systems.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Golf With Your Friends is a fun workout game, with short rounds and controls that work well on a treadmill or bike. Its computer performance has room to improve, but the game is still an enjoyable option for exercise.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
