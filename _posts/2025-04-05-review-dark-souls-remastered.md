DARK SOULS: REMASTERED takes you through Lordran, where each step can put you in danger. Its tough fights can hold your attention, though the quieter stretches are less useful for a workout.

## Gameplay

Combat takes focus and skill, and the challenge can help keep you alert while you exercise. But battles are broken up by slower stretches, and lore mostly comes through item descriptions, so your attention may wander.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller works well for playing while you ride or run, and the game does not demand constant rapid movements. Still, timing dodges and blocks can be tough when your heart rate is high, so you may need to ease up.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game does not ask for a high end PC, and players with moderate setups should be able to run it smoothly. Its system demands are fairly forgiving, but that alone does not make it a sure fit for every setup.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The demanding combat gives you a reason to stay focused, but the slower sections can interrupt your workout rhythm. It is a decent fit for exercise, though the timing and attention it asks for keep it from being an easy pick.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
