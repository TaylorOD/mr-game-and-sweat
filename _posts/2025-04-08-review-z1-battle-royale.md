Z1 Battle Royale sticks to classic battle royale gameplay. It has a nostalgic look, but the familiar mechanics do most of the work.

## Gameplay

Matches move quickly, with plenty of decisions and little downtime. You are always looking for weapons and gear or trying not to get ambushed, so the gameplay keeps your attention.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support makes the game manageable on a treadmill or bike. But keeping track of your surroundings takes focus, so it is only an okay fit for exercising.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is not especially demanding, and most midrange systems should handle it. The listed minimum is an Intel Core i3 or equivalent and 8 GB of RAM. Its graphics are modest, which helps keep the hardware requirements reasonable.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The fast gameplay is a plus, but the need to stay alert makes this a mixed choice for working out. Its hardware requirements are reasonable, though neither the workout fit nor the computer demands make it an easy recommendation overall.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
