Path of Exile 2 keeps the core mechanics of its predecessor and adds new features. Controller support also makes it practical to play while you work out.

## Gameplay

The combat is fast paced and engaging, and the quests give you something to think about as you run or pedal. Some cutscenes go on for a while, but there is plenty of action between them.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support means you do not need to fuss with a keyboard and mouse while exercising. Dodging and positioning matter at times, but the game does not demand quick reflexes or highly precise inputs, so you can play at a moderate pace.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You will want a decent GPU and CPU for smooth gameplay. On a low spec machine, expect some performance compromises, while a gaming rig should be fine.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Path of Exile 2 is a good workout pick thanks to its combat and controller support. The computer requirements may limit how well it runs on a weaker machine.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
