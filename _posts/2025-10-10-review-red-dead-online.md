Red Dead Online is the multiplayer part of Red Dead Redemption 2, with shooting, exploration, and player interactions in a large open world. There is plenty to do, but that does not always make it a smooth workout game.

## Gameplay

You can take on story missions, play free roam or competitive matches, or explore at your own pace. Cutscenes and slow stretches of travel can make it hard to keep a steady rhythm, so the gameplay is a mixed fit for exercise.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support and the generally relaxed pace make Red Dead Online manageable on a treadmill or bike. Aiming and moving do not demand constant quick reactions, but shootouts and horse pursuits can pull your focus away from your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements are demanding. You need a solid GPU, ample RAM, and a decent processor for a smooth experience, and upgrading your gear may be necessary.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Red Dead Online has enough to do, and its controller support makes exercising while playing possible. Still, slow sections and intense moments can disrupt your workout, and the demanding computer requirements are another drawback. It works, but it is not a great pick for staying active.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3
