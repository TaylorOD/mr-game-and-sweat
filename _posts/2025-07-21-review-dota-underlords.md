Dota Underlords is a strategy game set in the world of Dota 2. You build a team of heroes and make choices while they fight, and you can step away briefly during a round.

## Gameplay

The strategy gives you plenty to think about, and hero abilities and team combinations can change how a match goes. The rounds give you time to adjust your team, though the thinking can distract you from your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support makes it easier to navigate menus and make choices while you exercise. It does not need quick reactions, but getting caught up in strategy can take some attention away from your effort.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Dota Underlords can run on a relatively modest computer. Its requirements should not put much strain on your system.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The game’s strategic play works well for a workout, and its modest computer requirements are a plus. Still, the decisions can pull your focus from exercising, so it is a good fit with a few limits.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
