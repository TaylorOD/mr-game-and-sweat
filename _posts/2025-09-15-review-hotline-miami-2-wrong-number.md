If you liked the first *Hotline Miami*, the sequel keeps its fast action and stylized violence. The challenge and interruptions may not fit every workout.

## Gameplay

Combat asks for quick reflexes and fast decisions, and those parts work well. Slower sections and cutscenes interrupt the action, making the pacing uneven.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller support helps, but the rapid action demands a lot of attention. You may slow down to focus on the screen, so this is a poor fit for a relaxed workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game runs well on a wide range of hardware. You do not need a powerful computer to play it smoothly.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The action can keep you engaged, but cutscenes and slower sections can disrupt your workout rhythm. It may suit you if you like a challenge while exercising, but it will not fit every workout.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
