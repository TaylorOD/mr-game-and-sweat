Beat Saber is a VR rhythm game where you slice blocks to the music. It is a fun way to get moving, but it is built for playing while standing.

## Gameplay

You use two virtual lightsabers to slice blocks in time with the beat. The pace and patterns keep you busy, with no slow sections or long cut scenes. Songs vary in intensity, so the workout can change from track to track.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game does not officially support treadmills or bikes, and it is designed for standing. Walking or light jogging may work if you can keep a steady pace, but the fast movements can clash with riding a stationary bike.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You need a VR headset and a VR ready computer. The requirements are moderate, though you still need a decent GPU, and most mid range gaming PCs from 2018 onward can handle it.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Beat Saber has strong rhythm gameplay, but it is awkward to use with standard cardio equipment. It is a decent way to change up a workout if you are happy to play standing, though it is not an easy fit for a treadmill or bike.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
