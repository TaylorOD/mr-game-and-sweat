Pizza Tower is a throwback to 90s platformers, with a pizza loving protagonist and fast action. The gameplay is fun, but the pace can be a lot to handle during a workout.

## Gameplay

You dash, jump, and wall slide through levels filled with enemies and collectibles. The game rewards speed and exploration, so you can chase hidden items and high scores. There are no slow sections or long cut scenes, and the tight controls keep you focused.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game supports controllers, which helps when you’re on a treadmill or bike. Later levels call for quick reflexes and platforming skills, so keeping your balance and playing well at the same time can be tough. Chasing high scores can distract from the effort, but the game still takes focus.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Pizza Tower uses few system resources and runs smoothly on older machines. The review says you’ll need a mid range setup, though it should work for most players. You can focus on playing instead of worrying about performance.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Pizza Tower has strong gameplay and runs well on a range of computers. Its quick action is fun, but it takes enough focus that it is only a decent fit for exercising.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
