DayZ drops you into a harsh world with zombies and other players competing to survive. Exploring can be calm, but danger can show up fast.

## Gameplay

The open world lets you explore at your own pace, and scavenging can get quiet. Encounters with zombies or hostile players demand quick reactions, so the game can hold your attention but is not always easy to play while exercising.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

DayZ works with a controller, and most of its pace is manageable on a treadmill or bike. Combat can call for quick reflexes, though, which makes it harder to keep a steady workout rhythm.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

DayZ asks a fair amount of your computer. The listed requirements include a 2.4 GHz processor, 8 GB of RAM, and a graphics card equivalent to an Nvidia GTX 660 or AMD Radeon HD 7870.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The tense survival gameplay helps balance out the slower stretches. Controller support is useful, but sudden action can pull your attention away from your workout.

### Overall Score: 2.17 out of 3

DayZ can work alongside exercise, but it asks for too much attention when things get tense. It is a decent fit, not an easy game to play while working out.
