ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN’s brisk expeditions and frequent boss fights can make a workout fly by, but its precise dodges and punishing combat are a poor match for running at a pace where balance matters. It’s more practical on a stationary bike, where intense bursts of attention are less likely to throw off your footing.

# Gameplay

As a standalone adventure in the ELDEN RING universe, NIGHTREIGN reimagines the series’ core design around expeditions that mix exploration, combat, and major encounters. There’s plenty to hold your attention, and the pressure of a run keeps things moving. But exploration and preparation create slower stretches between fights, while demanding bosses require sustained concentration—not ideal if you’re trying to keep an easy workout rhythm.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

# Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game has full controller support, so you don’t need to reach for a keyboard or mouse. However, its combat depends on well-timed dodges and attacks, and mistakes can be costly. That makes it a challenging choice for treadmill play, especially at higher speeds; a bike is a safer fit, though you’ll still need to focus during fights.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

# Computer Requirements

The minimum requirements call for Windows 10, a 64-bit processor, an Intel Core i5-10600 or AMD Ryzen 5 5500, 12 GB of RAM, and a DirectX 12 graphics card such as an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 3GB or AMD Radeon RX 580 4GB. Those are older dedicated graphics cards, but they’re beyond integrated graphics.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

# Conclusion and Score

NIGHTREIGN offers an engaging, action-heavy workout companion, particularly for stationary cycling, but its precise combat makes it a risky pick for treadmill sessions. Consider it if you want a game that can absorb your attention and your PC meets the listed minimum requirements; choose a steadier, less demanding game for a relaxed run.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
