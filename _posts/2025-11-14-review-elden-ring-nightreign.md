Nightreign gives you plenty of fights to keep your workout moving, but its precise dodges and boss attacks need your full attention. It’s a better fit for an easy bike ride than a bumpy treadmill run.

## Gameplay

You explore, gather gear, and fight through a run that builds toward tough boss battles. The action is engaging, though the careful timing and stretches between fights keep it from being a smooth workout game.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Full controller support makes it easy to play without reaching for a keyboard. But quick reactions and well timed dodges are a bad match for a moving treadmill, so keep your pace steady or take this one to a bike.

### Category Score: 1 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The minimum asks for a Core i5 or Ryzen 5 processor, 12 GB of memory, and a GTX 1060 or Radeon RX 580 graphics card. That’s an older dedicated graphics card, but integrated graphics will not cut it.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Nightreign has engaging action and full controller support, but the demanding combat makes it hard to play safely at a fast workout pace. Save it for a bike ride or a slower treadmill session where you can focus on those boss fights.

### Overall Score: 1.67 out of 3
