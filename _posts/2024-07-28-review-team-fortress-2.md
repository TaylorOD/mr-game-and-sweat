Team Fortress 2 is a team shooter with cartoonish characters and a long history. Its quick matches are fun, though playing while exercising takes some adjustment.

## Gameplay

The nine classes give you plenty of ways to play, and modes like Capture the Flag and Control Points keep things moving. Rounds usually last 10 to 15 minutes, with little downtime or long cutscenes, though the pace and controls can be tough to handle while you exercise.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game supports controllers, and some classes are easier to use while you are moving. Others, like the Sniper, need more stillness and precise aiming, so keeping a steady pace on a treadmill or bike can be tricky.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Team Fortress 2 has modest system requirements and should run on most modern systems. A decent GPU from the past decade and a standard CPU should be enough.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Team Fortress 2 is a strong multiplayer game, but it is only a mixed fit for working out. Its fast action can be distracting in a good way, though some classes and quick movements are tough to manage while exercising.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
