Borderlands 2 mixes shooting, exploration and quests, with a distinctive art style and memorable characters. There is plenty to keep you interested, though the action can ask for more focus than a workout game should.

## Gameplay

The mix of exploration, combat and quests keeps you busy, and there is usually something to focus on. Gunfights and bosses ask for quick decisions, but exploration and puzzle solving give you a break. Cutscenes are generally short, so you can get back to playing without waiting long.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

A controller makes the basic movement manageable, and you do not need rapid button presses or complicated combos. Steady cardio works better than intense battles, where aiming accurately can compete with keeping your pace. You may need to ease off when a fight gets busy.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

It is fairly forgiving on PC, with an art style that can run on lower end systems without much visual compromise. The listed specs are an Intel Core 2 Duo or equivalent, 2GB of RAM, and a dedicated video card such as NVIDIA GeForce 8500 or ATI Radeon HD 2600.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Borderlands 2 is a good workout companion when the action is calm, but precision fights can interrupt your pace. The gameplay is strong, though aiming while moving and the need for a dedicated video card make it a mixed pick.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
