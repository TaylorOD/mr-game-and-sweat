## Gameplay

Split Fiction keeps the action and puzzles moving, with few long cutscenes or slow stretches. Research sections ask you to pause and think, but they don’t last too long. The pacing works well for a workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controls are intuitive, and you can play with a keyboard or a game controller. Combat doesn’t demand rapid reactions, and the game avoids sudden movements that could throw off your balance. That makes it easy to keep your attention on your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

You’ll need a mid tier graphics card and a basic multi core processor to run the game smoothly. It may work on older machines, but the requirements are still a possible barrier if you don’t have the right setup.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Split Fiction’s pacing and controls make it a solid workout game. Its computer requirements could limit who can play it, so it doesn’t quite earn a perfect score overall.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3
