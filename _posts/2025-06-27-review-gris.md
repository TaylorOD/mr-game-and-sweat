GRIS is a platformer from Nomada Studio. It focuses on exploration rather than fast action.

## Gameplay

You guide GRIS through levels with gentle platforming and puzzles. Some sections feel slow, and you may wait for the next part to unfold. That calm pace can be nice, but it can also make a workout feel dull.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controls are simple joystick movements, and you do not need quick reflexes or precise timing. That helps you keep your pace, but slower sections may leave you disengaged.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

GRIS does not need a powerful computer to run. A dual core CPU and integrated graphics meet the minimum requirements.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

GRIS pairs a slow pace with a story about loss and growth. It can suit a relaxed workout, but its slower sections may distract you. It runs on modest hardware.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
