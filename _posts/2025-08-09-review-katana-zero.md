Katana ZERO is a fast action platformer with swordplay, time manipulation, stealth, and a neo noir story. Its pixel art and combat give you plenty to focus on, though the quick pace is not always a comfortable fit for exercising.

## Gameplay

The combat is quick and asks you to think and react fast. Short levels help you stay focused, and the simple interface keeps dialogue choices easy to follow. It is a strong, satisfying game when you can give it your attention.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller and the controls are simple, but the game still asks for rapid movements and quick reflexes. Timing matters more than precise inputs, though that does not remove the challenge of playing while you exercise. It can work on a treadmill or bike, but it is not an effortless fit.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Katana ZERO does not need much from your computer. Its pixel art and chiptune soundtrack do not call for a high end graphics card, and a standard gaming setup should handle it without trouble.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The fast combat and short levels make Katana ZERO a good game, but its quick reactions can be awkward during a workout. The simple controls help, yet this is not a game you can play on autopilot.

### Overall Score: 2.67 out of 3 

Katana ZERO is a solid pick if you want a fast action game with a story. It works for some workouts, but the pace can make it harder to stay focused on both the game and your exercise.
