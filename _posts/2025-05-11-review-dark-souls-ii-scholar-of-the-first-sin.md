This is a tough game built around exploration and careful combat. It can hold your attention, but its pace leaves little room to relax.

## Gameplay

The level design, lore, and combat make for a strong game. Learning enemy patterns keeps you focused, and long cutscenes do not break up the action. The pace can feel relentless, though.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can play with a controller, and the game does not rely on rapid reflexes all the time. Boss fights and tricky encounters can still demand your full attention and disrupt your workout rhythm. It is manageable, but not an easy game to play while exercising.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements are fairly moderate, but you need a decent graphics card to get the most from the improved visuals. A mid range gaming computer should run it smoothly.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The gameplay is a good fit if you want a demanding game to keep you focused during a workout. Controller support helps, but tense fights can interrupt your pace, so it is not a perfect exercise game.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3

It is worth considering if you want a challenging game during your workout. The gameplay is strong, but the focus some encounters require makes it a mixed fit for running or riding.
