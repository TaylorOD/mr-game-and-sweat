FOR HONOR is a melee combat game about knights, samurais, and vikings. Its three stance fighting system gives combat some strategy, though the pacing can drag.

## Gameplay

Combat mixes fast fights with strategic choices, and you can attack from different directions using three stances. But slower sections and cutscenes can leave you waiting around instead of keeping your workout moving.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

You can use a controller, but the quick reflexes and precise movements needed in PvP matches make this a tough game to manage while exercising. Relaxed play is easier to pair with a treadmill or bike, though it can still distract you.

### Category Score: 1.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

FOR HONOR is moderately demanding on a computer. The recommended specs include an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 or equivalent, and older PCs or laptops may have performance issues.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

FOR HONOR has engaging combat, but its pacing and demanding controls make it a mixed fit for exercise. Relaxed play is the better option if you want to keep moving while you play.

### Overall Score: 1.83 out of 3
