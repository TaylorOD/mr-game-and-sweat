Hearts of Iron IV is a grand strategy game set during World War II. You lead a nation and manage alliances, military plans, resources, and technology, which gives you plenty to think about while you exercise.

## Gameplay

There’s a lot to consider, and your choices affect the campaign. But long planning stretches and pauses can make it hard to keep your workout going at a steady pace.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game doesn’t ask for quick reflexes, and its controls can be handled with modest movements. Still, you may need to look at the screen often to manage your troops, which can pull your attention from your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Hearts of Iron IV can run on relatively modest hardware, so you don’t need the latest graphics card. Enough RAM and a decent processor help prevent slowdowns, especially during complex battles or when managing large nations.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The strategic play can make for a decent workout, but the slow pace may not suit a high intensity session. It’s a reasonable fit for exercise if you’re fine with a more thoughtful game and the occasional distraction.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
