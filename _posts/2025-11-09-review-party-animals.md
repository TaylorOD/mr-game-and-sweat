Party Animals provides a lighthearted and chaotic experience that can serve as a fun distraction while you’re exercising. The game's emphasis on playful combat with adorable animals makes it an entertaining way to divert your attention from the workout itself—just don’t expect to break a sweat from the gameplay alone.

# Gameplay

The action in Party Animals is delivered through ridiculous, physics-based brawling, keeping the pace lively and engaging. Players can participate in quick matches that offer nonstop fun, with no lengthy cut scenes or excessive downtime. While the matches are relatively short, the anticipation and excitement keep your mind off your treadmill or bike. However, the game's charm lies in its silliness, which can sometimes lead to unpredictable moments that may not always align with a serious workout session. Still, you’ll find yourself laughing, which is a great distraction from your exertion.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

# Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Party Animals boasts complete controller support, which means you can easily navigate the game while you maintain your cardio routine. However, the gameplay mechanics require some degree of focus and timing, particularly when navigating chaotic brawls with friends. The imprecision of the game’s physics can lead to moments that require quick reactions, which may be a challenge while trying to stay on a moving treadmill. That said, the overall chaotic nature could potentially align well with the spirit of a fun workout, as you can laugh your way through the effort without too much penalty for imprecision.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

# Computer Requirements

Party Animals is fairly approachable in terms of computer specifications. It runs on a 64-bit OS, requires an i5 processor or AMD equivalent, and has modest graphics requirements with the NVIDIA GTX 750-Ti as a baseline. This means that most gamers with even a basic gaming rig should be able to run it smoothly, providing you with the opportunity to enjoy this communal chaos without needing state-of-the-art equipment.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

# Conclusion and Score

In summary, Party Animals offers an engaging and whimsical distraction that can enhance your workout, even if it’s not necessarily making you sweat. While it provides plenty of laugh-out-loud fun and features solid controller support, the need for quick reflexes and a bit of focus may not make it ideal for all types of exercise routines. Nevertheless, it still stands out as a delightful choice for a cardio session filled with furry chaos and friendly competition.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
