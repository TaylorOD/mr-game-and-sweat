Fishing Planet is a fishing simulator with simple actions: cast your line, wait for a bite, then reel in your catch. The slow pace can make it hard to stay focused on your workout.

## Gameplay

You can fish at different locations, each with its own challenges and species to catch. Waiting for a bite can drag, though, and watching the bobber may distract you from keeping a steady treadmill pace. There are no long cutscenes or extended dialogue to interrupt play.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The simple controls work well at a relaxed pace, and the game supports various controllers. Line adjustments and managing tension during a fight take some hand eye coordination, so you may need to focus more on the game. It is a decent fit for a workout, but not a great choice for high intensity sessions.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Fishing Planet is not especially demanding on computer hardware. It can run on a mid tier setup with a graphics card as low as a GTX 660, or on a dedicated laptop. You will want the game to run smoothly while you exercise.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Fishing Planet is a pleasant fit for a relaxed workout, but its slow sections may distract you from your exercise. The hardware demands are modest, though you still want smooth performance. Overall, it is a decent choice for playing while you exercise.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
