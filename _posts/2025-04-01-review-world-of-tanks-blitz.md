World of Tanks Blitz packs tank battles into short matches, so you can play without committing to a long session. It works well for quick breaks during a workout.

## Gameplay

Matches last about 7 minutes, with little downtime or long cut scenes. Planning your moves and landing shots keep you focused, though that can distract you from your cardio.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller friendly controls and slower, methodical combat suit a treadmill or bike. You can keep a steady pace, but remember to pay attention to your workout too.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game can run on a range of devices, including lower end machines and mobile platforms. You do not need high end computer specs to play it.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

World of Tanks Blitz is a strong fit for gaming during exercise. Short matches, controller friendly play, and modest computer requirements make it easy to pick up on a treadmill or bike.
