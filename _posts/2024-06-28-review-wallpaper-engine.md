Wallpaper Engine is an app for choosing and customizing animated wallpapers with visuals and audio. It is not a traditional game, and its appeal during a workout mostly comes from having something on screen.

## Gameplay

You browse a library of user made wallpapers and customize them to your taste. There is plenty to look through, but browsing can feel like a chore, especially when you are moving. Once you have picked a wallpaper, you can leave it alone and focus on your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The app does not ask for quick or precise movements, and it supports a controller. Setting things up before you start is the easier option, since navigating the wallpaper library while running or riding can still be awkward.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Wallpaper Engine is not very demanding on your computer. Higher resolution settings can put more strain on a lower end GPU, but you do not need cutting edge hardware.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Wallpaper Engine gives you a visual backdrop, but there is not much to do during a workout once your wallpaper is set. It can make exercise a little less dull, though browsing and setup are not especially convenient while you are moving.

### Overall Score: 2.3 out of 3
