Escape the Backrooms mixes exploration, puzzles, and horror. Its eerie maze gives you something to focus on while you work out, but the tense moments can pull your attention away from your movement.

## Gameplay

You explore the Backrooms, manage resources, and solve puzzles. The pace is decent, with few long cutscenes, but tense moments can demand your full attention. That makes it a solid game to exercise with, though not an easy one to tune out.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controls are straightforward, and controller support helps when you are on a treadmill or bike. Some sequences call for quick reactions or evasive moves, which can be awkward while keeping a steady rhythm. You can adjust, but it takes some coordination.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Escape the Backrooms does not ask much of your computer. You need a decent CPU and GPU for smooth play, but the game does not push modern hardware hard. Most standard PCs and laptops should be fine.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The exploration and puzzles keep the game moving, and it avoids many long interruptions. Controller support helps, but the tense moments and quick reactions make it less comfortable for a workout. Its low computer requirements are a plus.

### Overall Score: 2.5 out of 3
