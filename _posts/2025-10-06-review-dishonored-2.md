Arkane Studios’ Dishonored 2 is set in a steampunk world and follows up on the first game’s story and gameplay. You can make choices that affect how your experience plays out.

## Gameplay

You can play as Emily Kaldwin or Corvo Attano, and each has different abilities. Stealth and combat both work, but exploration and long cutscenes can slow things down, so expect some downtime between exercise and play.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The standard controller setup suits a treadmill or stationary bike, and the slower exploration gives you room to keep a steady pace. Combat can pull your attention away from your workout, so you may need to ease up when things get intense.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

Dishonored 2 can be demanding on a PC, so a fairly modern system is a good idea. The recommended specs include a GPU like the NVIDIA GTX 780 or better and at least 8 GB of RAM.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Dishonored 2 has good gameplay, but the uneven pacing keeps it from being an easy workout game. It works reasonably well on a treadmill or bike, though intense fights and long pauses can interrupt your rhythm.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
