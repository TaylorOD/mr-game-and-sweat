R.E.P.O. mixes action, strategy, and storytelling in a dystopian world where technology meets survival. Its short cutscenes and few slow stretches make it a good fit for playing while you work out.

## Gameplay

Procedurally generated levels bring choices that affect the story and your survival. The pace has few slow stretches, and cutscenes are brief, with occasional breaks between action. That gives you room to keep your stride without every moment demanding your full attention.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game supports compatible treadmill and bike controllers. Combat and exploration are forgiving, with moderate agility but no need for frantic, precise movements. You can focus on your workout without worrying about quick time events.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The review says R.E.P.O. runs smoothly on most machines and has modest requirements. But it gives no actual specs, so the computer requirements are hard to judge and not a major selling point.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

R.E.P.O. is a strong fit for running or riding, thanks to its pace and forgiving controls. The computer requirements are less clear, so the game is easier to recommend as a workout pick than as a sure bet for every PC.
