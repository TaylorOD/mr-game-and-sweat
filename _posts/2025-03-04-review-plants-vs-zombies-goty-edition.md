## Gameplay

Plants vs. Zombies GOTY Edition is a fun tower defense game with clever plant placement and resource management. The pace keeps you engaged, though you may need to change your strategy just as you settle into a rhythm.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

Controller support lets you play without relying on a mouse or keyboard. The game calls for strategy and quick decisions, but it does not demand constant twitch reactions, so you can keep your focus on your workout.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The game is not demanding on your computer and can run on older hardware. Its modest graphics needs mean you should not need a powerful PC to play while exercising.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Plants vs. Zombies GOTY Edition is a strong fit for running or riding while you play. It has engaging strategy, controller support and low computer requirements, with enough going on to keep your mind busy.

### Overall Score: 3 out of 3
