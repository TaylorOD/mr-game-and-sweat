The Witcher: Enhanced Edition Director's Cut has a lot of story and choices, but it moves slowly at times. It can work for a workout if you’re fine with pausing to focus on the game now and then.

## Gameplay

You play as Geralt of Rivia, a monster hunter, and your choices can change the game world. The story and dialogue are interesting, but long cutscenes and drawn out conversations can make it hard to keep your pace. Combat takes some strategy, though it may not give you much of a workout boost. You might get caught up in the story and forget to focus on your cardio.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game supports controllers, which helps when you’re exercising. Exploration and tactical combat still call for precise movement and timing, so this is a better fit for a moderate pace than a hard workout. You may find yourself paying more attention to the game than your exercise.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3 

## Computer Requirements

The game is not demanding on your computer and can run smoothly on older machines. That makes it a good choice if you want to keep your setup simple. Check the minimum requirements if you have a low end PC.

### Category Score: 3 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

There’s a lot to like in the story, but the slow parts can get in the way of a workout. Controller support and modest computer requirements help, though the game suits a lower intensity session better. It’s a decent option for cardio, but not an easy one to play without giving it some attention.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
