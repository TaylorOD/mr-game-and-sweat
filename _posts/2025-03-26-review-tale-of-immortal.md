Tale of Immortal draws on Chinese mythology and mixes exploration with RPG elements. Its slower stretches make it a mixed fit for exercise.

## Gameplay

There is plenty of exploring, quests, character conversations, and story progression. Some stretches move slowly, and long dialogue or cutscenes can distract you from your workout. Exploring can still give you something to focus on when exercise gets monotonous.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The game is not fast paced, so it does not demand constant quick reactions while you are moving. Controller support is available, though it leans toward keyboard and mouse, so set up the controls before you start.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements are moderate, and the game can run on a range of machines. For smooth play, a modest gaming computer with a decent CPU and mid range GPU is recommended. Many everyday computers should be able to handle it.

### Category Score: 2.5 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

Tale of Immortal has enough exploration to play during a workout, and its slower pace is manageable. Long dialogue and cutscenes are the main drawback, while moderate computer requirements and controller support make it easier to get started.

### Overall Score: 2.33 out of 3
