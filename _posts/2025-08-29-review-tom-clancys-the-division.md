The Division mixes RPG elements and tactical shooting in a New York City hit by a virus. It has some good workout moments, but the pace is uneven.

## Gameplay

Co-op play and team tactics give you plenty to do across missions and side activities. Combat can test your reflexes, while slow sections and long cut scenes can pull you out of your workout.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Treadmill and Bike Compatibility

The controller works well for playing while you run or ride, and exploration suits a steady pace. Combat with several enemies can disrupt your rhythm, so this is a mixed fit for exercise.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Computer Requirements

The computer requirements are moderate. Middle settings should be enough for smooth play, so you do not need top of the line hardware.

### Category Score: 2 out of 3

## Conclusion and Score

The Division has engaging tactical gameplay, but its slow stretches can break up your workout. It is a decent option if you want a mix of calmer play and combat, though neither part makes it an especially easy workout game.

### Overall Score: 2 out of 3
